## Claims to avoid or handle carefully
- 950+ A*s is an estimate with no exact records behind it. Do not lead with it or repeat it everywhere.
- Never promise a maximum class size. Show how attention works instead of giving a number.
- Do not promise doubt support outside class.
- Parent progress updates happen on request or when a teacher spots a problem, not on a schedule.
- "Center of Excellence" is part of the logo only, not a tagline.
- "Cambridge doesn't mark effort. It marks method." is not the brand promise.
- Do not put DHA Phase 7 in the homepage hero subheading.
- Permission for student photos, names and results has been informal or verbal so far.
