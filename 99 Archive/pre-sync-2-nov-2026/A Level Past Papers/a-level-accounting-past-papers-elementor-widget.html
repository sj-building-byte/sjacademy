The exam

What is on each Accounting paper

As set in the October/November 2025 papers. From 2016 to 2022 there were three papers (Paper 3 Structured Questions was 3 hours), and in 2015 there were four, with Paper 4 Problem Solving. Check the current syllabus on the Cambridge website before your exam.

PaperTimeMarksWhat you do
Paper 1 Multiple Choice1 hour3030 multiple choice questions. Answer all of them on the answer sheet.
Paper 2 Fundamentals of Accounting1 hour 45 minutes90Answer all questions. You write on the question paper.
Paper 3 Financial Accounting1 hour 30 minutes75Answer all questions, using the sources in the insert. You write on the question paper.
Paper 4 Cost and Management Accounting1 hour50Answer all questions, using the sources in the insert. You write on the question paper.
How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 1 is 1 hour for 30 questions. Paper 2 is 1 hour 45 minutes, Paper 3 is 1 hour 30 minutes and Paper 4 is 1 hour.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeCheck the format, headings and dates of every statement, not only the final figure.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksA missing heading, a figure in the wrong section, or a comment with no numbers from the question. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9706 past papers

Are these A Level Accounting past papers free?

Yes. Every file on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9706 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge International AS and A Level Accounting (9706) past papers from 2015 to 2026, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, in every paper and variant we hold. Each year shows the paper names used that year.

Which papers are AS and which are A Level?

From 2023, Papers 1 and 2 make up AS Level and Papers 3 and 4 complete the A Level.

Did the 9706 papers change in 2023?

Yes. From 2023 the papers are Multiple Choice, Fundamentals of Accounting, Financial Accounting, and Cost and Management Accounting. From 2016 to 2022 there were three papers: Multiple Choice, Structured Questions (AS) and Structured Questions (A Level). Each year on this page shows the paper names used that year.

How do I read a 9706 past paper file name?

Take 9706_w25_qp_12. 9706 is Accounting, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 12 is Paper 1, Variant 2. ms is the mark scheme and in is the insert.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.
