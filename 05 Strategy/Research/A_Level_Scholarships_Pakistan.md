# Comprehensive A-Level Scholarship & Financial Aid Database (Pakistan)

*Verified against official 2026 admission guidelines and institutional policies.*

This database outlines the A-Level scholarship and financial aid requirements across top colleges in Pakistan. The landscape is divided into two distinct models: **Quantitative Matrices** (guaranteed tuition waivers based on exact CAIE O Level grade counts) and **Holistic/Need-Based Models** (evaluating grades alongside interviews, essays, and financial necessity).

---

## Karachi

Karachi features a highly competitive mix of rigid quantitative matrices and holistic evaluations.

1. **Alpha College:** Employs a strict merit matrix. A 100% tuition waiver requires 9 A's with at least 7 A*s. *Note: 100% scholarship students must still pay a monthly administrative fee of Rs. 4,000.*
2. **The Lyceum:** Operates strictly on a need-based financial aid model. Aid applications are only given after academic admission is secured, and students must maintain a specific academic standing to retain the aid.
3. **Cedar College:** Uses a holistic merit evaluation dependent on final CAIE O Level results. Offers must be unconditional to switch subjects.
4. **Nixor College:** Focuses on a holistic assessment. While strong grades are the baseline, scholarships and admissions heavily weigh leadership and vision via essays and interviews.
5. **Karachi Grammar School (KGS):** Highly selective on pure academic merit. Instead of grade-based tuition waivers, KGS provides robust need-based financial aid to admitted students.
6. **Foundation Public School (FPS A Level):** Follows a clear matrix: 4 A's yield a 100% waiver, 3 A's yield 75%, and 2 A's yield 50%. Scholarships require an interview where the committee evaluates alignment with core values. 
7. **Whales College:** Offers 10% to 100% tuition fee waivers. To qualify for merit scholarships, students must secure a minimum of 4 A's in O Levels.
8. **Sceptre College:** Awards academic scholarships but enforces strict retention policies. Students must maintain 85% attendance, secure a minimum 'C' grade in all internal assessments, and strictly comply with college rules.
9. **Highbrow College:** Ensures an academically and economically diverse pool through holistic intake, blending academic baseline expectations with a strong emphasis on extracurricular engagement.
10. **Beaconhouse College Programme (BCP):** Awards Academic Merit scholarships based directly on the final results achieved in CAIE O Level Examinations and interview performance.

## Lahore

Lahore is characterized by highly structured, mathematically exact criteria and strict GPA retention policies.

1. **SICAS:** Utilizes an aggregate point system (A* = 10, A = 5). 40+ points earn a 100% scholarship. Students must maintain a high 3.67 A-Level GPA; dropping below 3.00 revokes the scholarship entirely.
2. **LACAS:** Uses a dual-matrix. Internal students require 6 A's for a 100% waiver, while external candidates require 7 A's.
3. **LGS Johar Town (JT) Boys:** Requires a baseline of 8 GCE O Level subjects or an 'A' grade in Matric just to apply. Merit scholarships mandate straight 'A' grades in all A-Level subjects to prevent revocation.
4. **Learning Alliance:** Highly structured matrix. A 100% tuition and charges waiver requires 10 A's or above in O Levels. The scholarship requires maintaining good attendance, discipline, and strong internal AS grades.
5. **LGS Defence:** Awards scholarships based on a flexible blend of academic merit, special talent, and need-based financial aid to ensure diverse socioeconomic access.
6. **Roots International Schools & Colleges:** Offers 100% tuition waivers for 8 A*/A's, 75% for 7 A*/A's, and scales down. Matriculation students with 90%+ receive a 60% waiver.
7. **Beaconhouse College Programme (BCP):** Awards academic merit scholarships to high achievers based purely on their O Level CAIE results.
8. **Aitchison College:** Maintains a highly competitive academic baseline for admission, offering extensive need-based financial aid rather than automatic grade matrices.
9. **The City School (Ravi Campus):** Offers merit-based tuition waivers directly scaling with the number of A's secured in the CAIE examinations.
10. **Bloomfield Hall School:** Combines academic merit scholarships for top O Level achievers with need-based provisions for deserving applicants.

## Islamabad & Rawalpindi

The capital region blends strict entrance tests with standardized merit matrices.

1. **Roots Ivy International College:** Requires all students to pass an entry test/interview. Straight A*/A students receive a 100% waiver, scaling down incrementally.
2. **Roots Millennium Education:** Local board students must secure an overall 'A' or 70%+ just to be eligible for AS Level admission. Scholarships are strictly merit-based.
3. **Froebel's International School:** Offers academic merit scholarships and unique talent-based scholarships for national/international achievements in sports, arts, and public speaking.
4. **Headstart School:** Highly proprietary process. Admissions rely on a 30-minute test (English, Math, Urdu) and a 15-minute interview evaluating critical thinking, alongside O Level mocks.
5. **Super Nova School:** Merit scholarships are tied to O Level achievements, but all applicants must clear a mandatory entry test and an interview.
6. **Westminster International School:** Offers a rigorous academic schedule and facilitates placements. Scholarships are awarded to top-performing students based on O Level grades.
7. **Beaconhouse College Programme (BMI/Margalla):** Like its national counterparts, awards Academic Merit scholarships based directly on CAIE O Level results.
8. **The City School (Capital Campus):** Implements a straightforward merit matrix granting tuition waivers based on the number of A*s and A's.
9. **LGS Islamabad:** Assesses students via a mix of O Level performance metrics and financial need evaluations.
10. **Schola Nova:** Focuses on holistic candidate evaluations, supplementing academic baseline requirements with interviews to award merit-based waivers.

## Other Major Educational Hubs

### Faisalabad
* **The University of Faisalabad (TUF) / Collegiate Programmes:** Offers highly structured merit waivers. Board Position Holders secure 100% waivers, while students scoring 90% or above receive proportional discounts.
* **Beaconhouse College Programme (BCP):** Nationwide merit policy applies; tuition waivers are granted based on O Level grades.
* **LGS Faisalabad:** Evaluates A-Level intake based on previous board/CAIE results, offering waivers for exceptional academic history.

### Multan
* **BCP Multan:** Standard BCP merit matrix; highly talented individuals receive waivers based on CAIE performance.
* **LGS Multan:** Awards scholarships based on O Level academic merit and offers need-based flexibility.
* **Bloomfield Hall Multan:** Grants merit scholarships specifically targeting top-tier CAIE O Level achievers.

### Peshawar
* **BCP Peshawar:** Adheres to the central BCP policy of rewarding O Level A* and A grades with tuition waivers.
* **The City School Peshawar:** Follows the corporate City School scholarship matrix, ensuring top O Level performers receive percentage-based waivers.
* **LGS Peshawar:** Evaluates O Level results to award scaling merit-based financial incentives.
