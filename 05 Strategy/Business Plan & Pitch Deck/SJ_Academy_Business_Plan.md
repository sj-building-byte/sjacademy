# SJ Academy: Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

SJ Academy is a scalable, growth-stage education company specializing in O and A Level learning, based in Karachi, Pakistan. Operational since 2018 and currently operating from DHA Phase 7, the academy provides highly structured physical and online tuition, A Level Lab Practicals, and comprehensive exam preparation.

Unlike traditional tuition centers built around single star teachers, SJ Academy is building a repeatable education model featuring subject-specialist teachers, parent communication loops, practical lab support, and digital learning assets. 

The business has established clear product-market fit, generating **PKR 16.13M in revenue** from August 2025 to May 2026 (its core academic season). Peak monthly revenue reached **PKR 2.73M** in April 2026, serving more than **200 active students** on a rolling peak-season basis. 

The immediate constraint is not demand, but physical capacity and operational scale. The current branch operates at a capacity of 20 to 35 students at any one point. 

**The Ask:** SJ Academy is raising **PKR 13M in exchange for 10% equity** to fund its next growth phase. This capital will unlock a larger central location (handling 80-100 students at a time), improved lab infrastructure, digital product development, professional workshops, and a stronger operating team. 

Post-expansion, management targets a revenue run-rate of approximately **PKR 40.3M** (2.5x the latest season), providing investors with distributable profit shares and dividend potential.

## 3. The Problem and Solution

**The Problem:**
The O and A Level tuition market is highly fragmented. Parents and students face:
*   Inconsistent teacher quality across subjects.
*   Scattered tuition schedules with no structured learning paths.
*   Limited access to fully equipped A Level lab practicals.
*   Weak visibility into student progress for parents.
*   Immense last-minute exam pressure and a lack of holistic exam strategy.

Parents aren't just buying teaching hours; they are buying confidence and exam performance.

**The Solution:**
SJ Academy aggregates the fragmented experience into a structured, single-point solution:
*   **Subject-Specialist Roster:** Vetted teachers under a unified academic standard.
*   **Physical & Online Delivery:** Blended access expanding beyond local catchment.
*   **Dedicated Lab Support:** Rare, structured A Level practical facilities.
*   **Syllabus & Paper Practice:** Laser-focused on Cambridge expectations.
*   **Next Phase Systems:** Investment will unlock mock exam systems, online tests, LMS dashboards, and self-serve learning platforms.

## 4. Market Opportunity

SJ Academy operates within the massive, recurring Cambridge International system. 

**Global & National Scale:**
*   **Global:** ~2M Cambridge students annually across 10,000+ schools in 160+ countries.
*   **Pakistan:** 100,000+ students from 700+ schools took June 2025 exams, with 127,900+ AS/A Level entries.

**Serviceable Opportunity:**
Rather than claiming unrealistic market share, SJ Academy focuses on specific expansion routes:
1.  **Karachi Physical Demand:** Expanding to a larger central DHA location immediately, followed by an expansion to PECHS in 2027 to capture unfulfilled local capacity.
2.  **Pakistan Online Demand:** Extending live classes outside Karachi.
3.  **Lab Practicals:** Utilizing physical infrastructure as a strong competitive moat.
4.  **Professional Workshops & Corporate Training:** Monetizing the 23M+ Instagram reach through workshops for students, freelancers, small business owners, and corporate trainings.
5.  **Digital Products & Global Subscription Platform:** Building a 3-year moat with digital products (solved past papers, notes, eBooks) and an O/A Level subscription platform to serve students globally.
6.  **University Test Prep:** A natural progression market leveraging the graduating A Level student base.

## 5. Business Model

The business model relies on recurring academic season revenue and adjacent, highly scalable digital/workshop revenue lines. 

**Revenue Focus (Near-Term & 3-Year Plan):**
1.  **Monthly Tuition Fees:** Recurring revenue (avg. PKR 15,000/month/student).
2.  **A Level Practicals:** High-margin, niche requirement with strong exam relevance.
3.  **Professional Workshops & Corporate Training:** Utilizing weekend/off-peak capacity for students, freelancers, business owners, and B2B corporate trainings. Target margins: 40-50%.
4.  **Digital Products & Subscription Platform:** Highly scalable solved past papers, O/A level notes, industry-specific eBooks, and a global subscription model.
5.  **University Test Prep:** Natural expansion leveraging the captive A Level graduate base.

**Margin Expansion Strategy:**
Currently, teacher payouts range from 50% to 70% of student fees. As scale increases, the model will shift toward a **fixed fee per batch**. This provides margin visibility and reduces variable cost dependency.
