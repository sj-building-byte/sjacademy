FAQ

Questions about SJ Academy fees

Are there any hidden charges besides the tuition fee?

No. New students pay a one-time admission fee of Rs. 3,500. After that you pay the monthly fee for each subject, plus the one-time lab practical fee if you take practicals.

How much is O Level and A Level tuition at SJ Academy?

O Level and IGCSE tuition is Rs. 8,500 per subject, per month. A Level tuition for AS or A2 is Rs. 10,500 per subject, per month. The A Level composite, where AS and A2 of a subject are taught together, is Rs. 20,000 per subject, per month, and covers both levels. Two composite subjects cost Rs. 40,000 per month.

How does the lab practical fee work?

A Level lab practicals cost Rs. 25,500 per subject, paid once. It covers the full practical course for one exam series, with no monthly lab charge. Practicals have no demo class; students join directly.

Is there a discount for paying in advance?

Yes. You get 10% off your monthly fees when you pay for three months upfront.

Does the monthly fee include the CAIE exam fee?

No. The monthly fee covers teaching, assessments and study material at SJ Academy. CAIE exam fees are paid separately to the British Council.

Are study materials and past papers included?

Yes. Topical worksheets, past papers and revision notes are part of your tuition.

What are the Oct/Nov fast-track batches?

They are faster-paced batches for students sitting the Oct/Nov series, with a different fee. Registration for Oct/Nov 2026 is closed and reopens in June 2027.

Do you offer scholarships or financial aid?

We offer need-based discounts. Contact our administration office to discuss your situation.
