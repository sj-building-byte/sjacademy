A Level Business 9609, Paper 1. Analyse two benefits to a business of using job enrichment. [8]
Job enrichment is when workers get more interesting work. This is good because workers will be happy and work harder. It also means the business will do better and make more profit. Workers will also not leave the business.
Job enrichment gives employees more challenging tasks and more responsibility.K For a business with skilled staff doing repetitive work,APP this meets Herzberg's motivators, so employees put in more effort, which raises output per worker and lowers unit labour cost.AN A second benefit is lower labour turnover.K Employees who feel trusted are less likely to leave, so the business spends less on recruitment and training and keeps experienced staff, which protects quality.AN
