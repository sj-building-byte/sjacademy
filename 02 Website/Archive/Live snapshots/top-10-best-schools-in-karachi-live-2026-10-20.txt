## 2. [Bay View High School](https://website.bayviewhighschool.edu.pk/)

[IMG alt=""]

Overview: Bay View Academy (BVA) was founded in 1990 and opened its first purpose-built Defence campus in 1999. Today, it runs three campuses across Karachi—including Clifton and PECHS—serving over 1,600 students from Nursery through Class XI. BVA is registered with Cambridge for O‑Level curriculum, providing a rigorous British-style education that preps students for top local and international universities.

At Bay View, academic excellence goes hand-in-hand with character growth. The school strives to cultivate lifelong learners by nurturing confidence, critical thinking, and initiative in a warm, supportive environment. Faculty and staff focus on continuous growth, both locally and internationally, to elevate teaching standards across all campuses.

Curriculum: Cambridge International Examinations (CIE)

Locations: PECHS, Clifton, KDA

Fee Structure: Tuition fees range from PKR 38,480 to PKR 46,375 per month, depending on the grade level. Additional charges include an admission fee of PKR 290,000 and a refundable security deposit of PKR 40,000. 

## 3. Haque Academy

[IMG alt=""]

Overview:
Haque Academy was established in 1991 and operates a purpose-built campus in DHA Phase 8, Karachi. It offers education from primary grades through O Levels and is registered with Cambridge for O Level examinations. The school has developed a reputation for strong academic outcomes, particularly in sciences and mathematics, along with a structured assessment and feedback system.

Haque Academy emphasizes disciplined study habits, regular testing, and close academic monitoring. Alongside academics, the school promotes student wellbeing, leadership opportunities, and co-curricular engagement within a controlled and focused learning environment.

Curriculum: Cambridge International Examinations (CIE)

Locations: DHA Phase 8, Karachi

Fee Structure:
Monthly tuition fees generally fall in the range of PKR 40,000 to PKR 55,000, depending on grade level. Additional charges include admission fees and refundable security deposits, payable at the time of enrollment.

Lyceum combines rigorous academics with a vibrant co‑curricular culture, including sports, debating (with wins at Harvard MUN and ROTMUN), clubs, community service, and leadership programs, all within a close-knit campus environment that many alumni describe as more “home” than school

## 6. Habib Public School

[IMG alt=""]

Habib Public School was founded in 1942 and is one of Karachi’s most well-known not-for-profit educational institutions. The school offers multiple academic streams, including Cambridge O Levels, and caters to students from primary classes through secondary education. Its large campus on M.T. Khan Road provides extensive sports, academic, and recreational facilities.

The school is particularly known for its disciplined environment, strong sports culture, and emphasis on character building. While academics are a core focus, Habib Public School is widely respected for producing well-rounded students through leadership programs, athletics, and extracurricular involvement.

Curriculum: Cambridge International Examinations (CIE)

Locations: M.T. Khan Road, Karachi

Fee Structure:
Tuition fees are comparatively moderate, generally ranging from PKR 18,000 to PKR 30,000 per month for the O Level stream. Admission and annual fees apply and are significantly lower than most private Cambridge schools in Karachi.

## 7. St. Joseph’s Convent School

[IMG alt=""]

Overview:
St. Joseph’s Convent School is a historic all-girls institution established in 1862 and is one of the oldest schools in Karachi. The school offers both Matriculation and Cambridge O Level streams and is widely regarded for its academic discipline, strong moral framework, and consistent examination results.

The O Level section maintains a structured and exam-focused academic approach, supported by experienced faculty and a highly disciplined school culture. While facilities are more traditional compared to newer campuses, the school’s reputation for academic seriousness and student conduct remains strong.

Curriculum: Cambridge International Examinations (CIE)

Locations: Saddar, Karachi

Fee Structure:
Fees are relatively affordable compared to most private Cambridge schools, typically ranging from PKR 12,000 to PKR 20,000 per month. Admission and annual charges apply but remain modest by Karachi O Level standards.

## 9. [Foundation Public School](https://fps.edu.pk/)

[IMG alt=""]

Overview: Founded in 1981, Foundation Public School began with just a dozen students and has since expanded into a network of 11 campuses across Karachi and Hyderabad, serving around 6,000+ students from Pre‑Nursery through A-Level. FPS follows the Cambridge (CAIE) curriculum, with a focus on critical thinking, intellectual curiosity, and real-world problem solving. The school’s “Knowledge Is Power” philosophy emphasizes freedom of thought, scientific inquiry, and compassionate decision-making to cultivate independent learners and responsible leaders.

Beyond academics, FPS champions holistic development. It offers a vibrant range of co‑curricular activities like sports, clubs, community service, STEAM programs, and leadership initiatives, that reinforce empathy and social responsibility. FPS also takes pride in its house system, competitive sports teams (including junior boys’ champions in the Karachi United School Championship), and a proud alumni network that includes notable personalities from various fields.

Curriculum: Cambridge (O & A Levels)

Locations: DHA, PECHS

Fee Structure: Admission fees start from PKR 15,000, with monthly tuition fees ranging from PKR 7,000 to PKR 20,000, depending on the grade level. 

## 10. [British International School](https://www.biskarachi.com/) (BIS)

[IMG alt=""]

Overview: British International School (BIS) is an independent, Edexcel-certified co‑educational school located in Clifton, Karachi. Offering classes from Pre‑Nursery through A‑Levels, BIS delivers a British-style education grounded in Pearson Edexcel qualifications like GCSEs and A‑Levels, with an intentionally international and multicultural environment.

BIS places students at the heart of everything, striving not only for academic excellence but also nurturing values like kindness, generosity, and thoughtfulness. Their curriculum includes robust academics alongside arts, drama, ICT, music, French, and physical education. With a strong focus on character development and well-rounded learning, BIS aims to prepare students for both university success and meaningful lives.

Curriculum: British (IGCSE, GCSE)

Location: Clifton

Fee Structure: Tuition fees are approximately PKR 222,000 per annum.
