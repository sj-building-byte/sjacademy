A Level Business 9609 · Paper 1Analyse two benefits to a hotel of using job enrichment for its employees. [8]
Job enrichment is when workers get more interesting work. This is good because workers will be happy and work harder. The hotel will do better and make more profit. Workers will also not leave.
Job enrichment gives employees more challenging tasks and more responsibility.K If the hotel lets reception staff resolve guest complaints themselves instead of calling a manager,APP they feel trusted, which meets Herzberg's motivators, so they put in more effort.AN Problems get solved faster, so guests are more likely to rebook, raising revenue.AN A second benefit is lower labour turnover.K Hotels rely on trained front-desk and housekeeping staff who know regular guests.APP Enriched jobs make these employees less likely to leave, so the hotel spends less on recruiting and training replacements.AN Keeping experienced staff also keeps service quality consistent, which protects its reputation.AN

What makes SJ Academy different from other O and A Level academies in Karachi?

SJ Academy teaches to the mark scheme. Students learn the syllabus, then practise past papers that are marked the way Cambridge examiners mark them, with feedback on where each mark was lost. Classes run in small batches at our coaching center in DHA Phase 7 and live online.

Is there an SJ Academy tutor academy near me in Karachi?

Our campus is at Plot 29C, Lane 5, Seher Commercial Area, DHA Phase 7, Karachi, close to Clifton and the rest of DHA. If you live further away, or outside Karachi, every class also runs live online. Get directions.

Who are the tutors? Are they qualified for Cambridge O and A Levels?

SJ Academy's O and A Level tutors include teachers from LUMS, IBA and KGS, each teaching the subject they specialise in. Meet the teachers.

Which subjects do you offer tuition for?

O Level tuition covers Maths, Add Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Pakistan Studies, Islamiyat, English, Urdu, Accounting, Business, Economics and Computer Science. A Level tuition (AS and A2) covers Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Business, Economics, Computer Science, Accounting, Psychology, Sociology, English and Urdu. We also run A Level lab practicals for Physics, Chemistry and Biology, including for private candidates.

Can my child try a class before we pay?

Yes, for O Level and A Level classes. The first demo class is free: pick a subject at the top of this page and book it in under a minute. Lab practicals have no demo class; students join directly.

How much does O and A Level tuition cost?

Fees are charged per subject, per month, and depend on the level. See the full fee structure for current figures.

Do you teach online as well as in-class?

Yes. Every class runs live online as well as in-class at DHA Phase 7. Students join from across Pakistan and from abroad.

How many students are in each batch?

We keep batches small so the teacher can give each student feedback. Message us for the current size of the batch you want to join.

Do you offer one-on-one or home tuition in Karachi?

No. We do not offer one-on-one or home tuition. We teach in small batches, at the academy and live online.
