How it works

Four answers, three routes

  1. Pick your O Level strengthsThe subjects you enjoy and expect strong grades in.
  2. Say how you score bestMaths problems, essays, applied science, business analysis or portfolios.
  3. Choose a directionUp to two, or undecided.
  4. Rule out what you refuseAny subject you will not take at A Level is removed from the routes.

How the ranking works

Your direction counts most. How you score best comes next, then your O Level strengths. A route that needs a subject you ruled out is removed, and the tool tells you when that changes your options.

Every route shows the degrees it opens, the entry tests, and universities in Pakistan and abroad.

Before you commit

What IBCC requires

If you need Pakistani equivalence for a university in Pakistan, IBCC fixes the A Level subjects for the science groups. These rules come from IBCC’s equivalence guidance.

GroupA Level subjects IBCC accepts
Pre-MedicalPhysics, Chemistry and Biology
Pre-EngineeringPhysics, Chemistry and Mathematics
Computer SciencePhysics, Computer Science and Mathematics
General ScienceMathematics with Economics and Computer Science, or Mathematics with Physics and Statistics, among other listed combinations
HumanitiesAny three A Level subjects

The science groups also need SSC Science (with Biology for Pre-Medical), and IBCC sets a minimum grade for each subject. Rules change, so confirm on the IBCC website before you finalise.

FAQ

Questions about subject choice

How many A Level subjects should I take?

Most students take three. IBCC equivalence is built on three A Level subjects, and a fourth adds workload. Take a fourth only if a degree you want asks for it.

Do I need Physics to study medicine in Pakistan?

Yes, for IBCC Pre-Medical equivalence you need Physics, Chemistry and Biology at A Level, plus SSC Science with Biology. Biology and Chemistry without Physics suits medicine abroad, but it does not earn Pre-Medical equivalence in Pakistan.

Which A Levels do I need for engineering?

Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry give you IBCC Pre-Engineering equivalence. Many UK engineering courses ask for Mathematics and Physics, and some prefer Further Mathematics. Check the course page for your target university.

Can I study business or law with science subjects?

Often yes. Many business and law programmes do not fix A Level subjects, though finance and economics programmes usually prefer Mathematics. Check each programme before you decide.

What is the difference between AS and A2?

Cambridge International AS and A Level is taught in two stages. AS is the first stage and A2 is the second. Together they make up the full A Level.

How accurate are the results?

The tool compares your answers with common degree routes and IBCC’s equivalence rules. It is guidance, not an admissions rule. Confirm requirements with each university and with IBCC before you finalise your subjects.
