The problem

Why good students still lose marks in Business

Most students do not lose marks because they know nothing. They lose marks because the answer does not match what the examiner rewards. Writing more does not fix that. Examiners are not paid by the page.

  • Sample answer lineBenefit of delegation for a restaurant chain
    Delegation means passing authority to subordinates. It motivates employees.which business? no APP

    They define instead of applying

    They know the term, but they never link it to the business in the question. The application marks stay on the table.

  • Sample answer lineImpact of a price cut on a clothing retailer
    A lower price will increase sales, which is good for the business.and then? chain stops here

    They explain instead of analysing

    They make a point, but they do not follow it from the decision to its consequence for the business. One step is not a chain.

  • Sample answer lineShould the business expand abroad?
    In conclusion, it depends on the business and its situation.depends on WHAT?

    They conclude instead of evaluating

    "It depends" is not evaluation. It is a cliffhanger. The marks are for saying what it depends on, using the case and the numbers.

The method

Know what each mark wants before you write

Every question size has its own split of knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation. On a 12-marker, 6 of the 12 marks are evaluation.

Pick a question sizetap one ↓

2 marks · Paper 1, Section A

2K

Two precise knowledge points. No example, no analysis and no essay. Write it and move on.

3 marks · Paper 1, Section A

1K2App

One point, explained in a business context. Two of the three marks are for the context, not the definition.

5 marks · Paper 1, Section A

1K2App2An

One developed chain, applied to the context: cause, impact, consequence.

8 marks · Paper 1, Section B

2K2App4An

Exactly two developed chains. There are no evaluation marks, so your conclusion earns nothing, however heartfelt.

12 marks · Paper 1, Section B

2K2App2An6Eval

Half the marks are evaluation. Keep the analysis short, weigh the points in context, and finish with a judgement you can justify.

20 marks · Paper 4 essay

3K2App8An7Eval

A strategy essay on the case. Evaluation runs through the whole answer, not only the last paragraph.

K = knowledgeApp = applicationAn = analysisEval = evaluation

The syllabus

The A Level Business syllabus (9609), paper by paper

The Cambridge syllabus covers five areas: business and its environment, human resource management, marketing, operations management, and finance and accounting. Each paper tests them with a different answer style. AS students sit Papers 1 and 2. A2 students sit Papers 3 and 4.

Paper 1 · AS LevelBusiness Concepts 1

  • 1h 15mduration
  • 40marks
  • 20%of the A Level

What it asks. Short answers worth 2, 3 and 5 marks, then one essay question split into an 8-mark analysis and a 12-mark evaluation.

What you practise. Clean definitions, one-chain answers, and the difference between an 8 and a 12, which is where most marks go missing.

Paper 2 · AS LevelBusiness Concepts 2

  • 1h 30mduration
  • 60marks
  • 30%of the A Level

What it asks. Two data response questions, each built on a short case about a business, including calculations.

What you practise. Pulling the right data out of the case, using it inside the answer, and making judgements that fit that business, not any business.

Paper 3 · A2 LevelBusiness Decision-Making

  • 1h 45mduration
  • 60marks
  • 30%of the A Level

What it asks. Five questions on one longer case study, with numerical data, calculations and evaluation-heavy 12-markers.

What you practise. Calculations you can trust, and recommendations that use your own numbers as evidence.

Paper 4 · A2 LevelBusiness Strategy

  • 1h 15mduration
  • 40marks
  • 20%of the A Level

What it asks. Two 20-mark strategy essays on one case study.

What you practise. Planning a 20-marker, evaluating throughout the essay, and ending with a judgement that is specific to the case.

How do I get an A* in Cambridge A Level Business (9609)?

Memorising notes is not enough. Examiners reward application to the case, chained analysis that shows each step of the impact on the business, and balanced evaluation. In class you practise these skills paper by paper, so you know how to structure 8, 12 and 20-mark answers.

What is the difference between AS and A2 Business?

AS Level is Papers 1 and 2: core business concepts, short answers, data response and shorter essays. A2 Level is Papers 3 and 4: decision-making with numerical data, business strategy and 20-mark essays. We teach the full 9609 syllabus for both.

Are A Level Business past papers enough to prepare?

Past papers are essential, but only with feedback. Practising the wrong answer structure again and again does not improve a grade. In the batch, past paper answers are marked and you are told what to change.

Is online A Level Business tuition as effective as in-class?
