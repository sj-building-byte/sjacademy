A Level Physics Practicals May/June 2026 in Karachi

At SJ Academy, our A Level Physics Practicals replicate real CAIE experiments. Students master critical lab techniques such as precision measurement, graph plotting, and data analysis, the foundation for success in Paper 3 and Paper 5.

Some of the key experiments include:

 

  • Determining acceleration due to gravity using a free-fall method
  • Verifying Ohm’s Law and Hooke’s Law
  • Measuring resistivity of a wire
  • Finding focal length of a lens

Each session is guided by experienced teachers who ensure you understand theory, experiment logic, and data evaluation. Every concept is reinforced with step-by-step demonstrations, making this one of Karachi’s most effective A Level Practical Classes in Karachi May/June 2026 programs.

A Level Chemistry Practicals May/June 2026 in Karachi

Our A Level Chemistry Practicals focus on precision, accuracy, and technique. Students gain mastery in titrations, qualitative analysis, observation recording, and analytical thinking, all tailored for CAIE success.

 

Key experiments include:

 

  • Acid-base and redox titration
  • Preparation of standard solutions
  • Qualitative tests for cations, anions, and gases
  • Organic tests for functional groups
  • Separation and purification methods

Every class gives you real-lab familiarity. You’ll work with proper apparatus, develop careful observation habits, and learn how to record and conclude your findings exactly as CAIE expects.

A Level Biology Practicals May/June 2026 in Karachi

The A Level Biology Practicals program sharpens observation and interpretation skills through live experiments designed for Paper 3 and Paper 5 success.

Students perform:

 

  • Microscope and slide preparation
  • Enzyme activity and osmosis experiments
  • Food tests and transpiration studies
  • Fieldwork including quadrat sampling
  • Dissections and photosynthesis experiments

Each practical helps you develop precision, patience, and confidence, ensuring full readiness for your A Level Practical Exam May/June 2026 in Karachi. Our supportive instructors guide every step, ensuring no student is left behind.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Will we be able to prepare completely for the A level practical exams in the workshops ?

Yes, you will be covering everything. Along with performing the practicals, you will be explained in detail how the CAIE practical exam is marked.

What is the fee for the workshops?

Fee is PKR 25000/subject + PKR 3000 Registration fees (one time).

Is there any discount if we take all 3 subjects?

Yes, you can get a 10% discount if you take all 3 subjects together.

What will be the class timings?

Class timings for Physics and Chemistry will be between 12 PM to 4 PM.

For Biology it will be 4 PM onwards on weekdays and 2 PM on weekends.

If you are not able to attend classes at the given times, you can inform us in advance and we will arrange a different time slot for you.

I will be doing practicals for the first time. Should I register for the practicals or register earlier?

If you do not have any experience with practicals, its better if you register earlier. The total fee will be the same anyways, but you will get time to practice at your own pace initially.

But even if you just come for the workshops, we will make sure your preparation is thorough. You might need to spend more time doing the practicals.

Do you offer crash courses for last-minute prep?

Yes, we offer short crash workshops closer to exam dates to help students revise key experiments, polish their observation skills, and learn quick techniques to avoid common exam mistakes.

Are these practicals enough to help me score well in Paper 3?

Absolutely. Our sessions are designed not just for hands-on practice but to train you on thinking Physics and Chemistry Practicals . We also give you tips on time management, observation writing, and drawing conclusions just like the marking schemes demand.

Can private candidates also register for practicals?

Yes! We welcome private candidates from all over Karachi. Whether you’re doing your Physics, Chemistry, or Biology Practicals for the first time or retaking the exam, as an independent student, you’ll get the same expert guidance and lab access.
