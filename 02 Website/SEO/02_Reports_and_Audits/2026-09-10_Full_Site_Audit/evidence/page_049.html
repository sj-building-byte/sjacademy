What is PUM (Percentage Uniform Mark)?

PUM is NOT your raw exam percentage. It is a standardized score showing your exact position within a grade boundary on Cambridge results slips:

PUM Range AS Grade Meaning
80 - 100% Grade a Top performance
70 - 79% Grade b Strong upper band
60 - 69% Grade c Solid average
50 - 59% Grade d Passing / Borderline
40 - 49% Grade e Minimum pass threshold
Below 40% Ungraded (u) Below minimum pass mark

Example: If the AS Grade 'a' threshold is 75/130 marks, scoring exactly 75 raw marks awards 80% PUM.
