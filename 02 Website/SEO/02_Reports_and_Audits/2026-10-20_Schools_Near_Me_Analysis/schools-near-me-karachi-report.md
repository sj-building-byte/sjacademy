# Schools Near Me Karachi: Traffic and Lead Analysis

**Page:** https://www.sjacademy.pk/schools-near-me-karachi/
**Date:** 20 Oct 2026
**Sources:** Google Search Console, GA4 (property 482748209), New Leads Tracker 2026-2027, live page fetch
**Ranges:** Search Console 20 Jun 2025 to 17 Oct 2026 (16 months) and 19 Jul to 17 Oct 2026 (3 months). GA4 22 Jul to 19 Oct 2026 (90 days).
Read-only analysis. Nothing was changed on the site, in Search Console or in GA4.

---

## a) The 5 most important findings

1. **The real traffic is about 56 Google clicks a month, not 19.5K.** In the last 3 months Search Console shows 168 clicks, and GA4 shows 186 sessions starting on this page. That is 0.69% of all site clicks (24,460). There was never a real spike: the best day was 20 Jan 2026 with 7 clicks. The most impressions in one day was 16 Jan 2026, with 799 impressions but only 3 clicks.
2. **In 90 days it produced one possible lead.** Sessions starting here fired these events:
   • `submit_lead_form` once, `generate_lead` once, and one visit to /thank-you/. These are probably the same single lead (inference).
   • 2 phone taps and 0 WhatsApp clicks.
   • 0 key events recorded.
   • For comparison, the whole site had 131 key events (`generate_lead`).
3. **The visitors are local.** 165 of 186 sessions (89%) came from Karachi and 174 (94%) from Pakistan. In Search Console all 418 clicks over 16 months came from Pakistan, even though queries mentioning "karachi" brought only 1 click.
4. **People use the tool, then leave.**
   • 57% engagement rate, 43% bounce, and about 62 seconds of engagement per session.
   • 462 page views across 186 sessions, so people search the list several times.
   • Only 1 session went on to /o-a-level-classes/, 1 to /o-level-tuition-center-karachi/, and none to a fee page.
   • The tuition ad card brought 0 sessions in 90 days (no visits carried its `school_finder_tool` tag).
5. **These visitors are parents choosing a school, not students looking for tuition.** The traffic is real, but it is the wrong audience for the business.

### Why Semrush is wrong (inference)

Semrush multiplies the national search volume by an expected click rate for position 5. "Near me" results change with each searcher's location, and the map pack sits above the normal results, so the real clicks are far lower. Google organic in GA4 (148 sessions) is a bit below Search Console (168 clicks), which is normal when cookies are declined or blocked.

## c) Verdict

**Keep the page as it is and spend little effort on it.**
• It is cheap to run and its visitors are in Karachi.
• It has brought at most one lead in 90 days, and its visitors are not shopping for tuition.
• Effort is better spent on the class pages and the schools blog post, which gets 35 times this page's traffic.

---

## d) Actions, quick wins first

1. **Make the ad card relevant to parents and remove its UTM tags.** Try something like "Your child's O Levels: free demo class". Link plainly to `https://www.sjacademy.pk/o-a-level-classes/` with www. *Effect:* the card's clicks become measurable, and it may start getting some.
2. **Add GA4 events through GTM (GTM-K9KZDNP8)** for finder search, "View on Map" click and ad card click. *Effect:* you see what people do on the tool.
3. **Mark `submit_lead_form` and `whatsapp_click` as key events in GA4.** Both fire across the site (1,047 and 499 times in 90 days) but don't count as key events. *Effect:* the lead counts in GA4 become complete.
4. **Copy the landing page from WPForms User Journey into the Lead Tracker.** The plugin is already installed. *Effect:* you can count leads per page.
5. **Merge the two FAQPage blocks and remove the second `<title>` tag** ("SJ Academy - Schools Near Me Finder") that appears inside the page body. *Effect:* cleaner page code. FAQ rich results won't show for a site like this.
6. **Improve the "o level tutor in karachi" ranking** (4,870 impressions, position 9.6, 16 clicks). *Effect:* moving onto page 1 for a query that brings leads (inference).
7. **Put a tuition call to action on the Top 10 schools blog post.** *Effect:* more leads from the site's biggest page.

## Technical check

• **Indexing (URL Inspection):** submitted and indexed. Last crawled 12 Oct 2026 as mobile. Robots allowed. Google canonical matches the declared canonical.
• **Title:** "Find the Best Schools Near Me in Karachi | SJ Academy"
• **Meta description:** "Discover top private & government schools near you in Karachi. Search by area, board & type to find the best schools in DHA, PECHS, and more."
• **Canonical:** self-referencing. **Robots meta:** index, follow.
• **School list:** not in the raw HTML. The page first shows "Loading schools data...". JavaScript then loads 518 schools from a public Google Sheet CSV. Googlebot may still render it, but this was not confirmed.
• **Structured data:** WebPage, BreadcrumbList, WebSite, Organization, TutoringService, EducationalOrganization, and two duplicate FAQPage blocks. Only Breadcrumbs is detected as a rich result (shown as "Unnamed item"). There is no ItemList of schools.
• **Tracking:** the school finder script has no GA4 or dataLayer calls. The ad card link carries internal UTM tags (`utm_source=school_finder_tool`).
• **Page speed:** not checked. The PageSpeed Insights daily quota was used up.

• **PageSpeed and Core Web Vitals:** quota used up. Retry another day.
