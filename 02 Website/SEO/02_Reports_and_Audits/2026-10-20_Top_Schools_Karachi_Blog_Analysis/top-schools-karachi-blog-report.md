# Top Schools in Karachi Blog Post: Traffic and Lead Analysis

**Page:** https://www.sjacademy.pk/blog/top-10-best-schools-in-karachi-with-fee-structures/
**Date:** 20 Oct 2026
**Sources:** Google Search Console, GA4 (property 482748209), live page fetch
**Ranges:** Search Console 20 Jun 2025 to 17 Oct 2026 (16 months) and 19 Jul to 17 Oct 2026 (3 months). GA4 22 Jul to 19 Oct 2026 (90 days), monthly from Jun 2025.
Read-only analysis. Nothing was changed on the site, in Search Console or in GA4.

---

## a) The 5 most important findings

1. **It is the site's biggest traffic page.** In the last 3 months it got 5,926 clicks from 183,022 impressions (CTR 3.24%, position 5.8). That is 24% of all site clicks (24,460). GA4 shows 6,011 sessions starting here.
2. **The visitors are local.** 5,031 of 6,011 sessions (84%) came from Karachi and 5,672 (94%) from Pakistan.
3. **Very few visitors become leads.**
   • 4 key events in 90 days, which is 0.07% of sessions. The O Level tuition centre page converts 0.8% (4 of 504).
   • 13 `generate_lead` events, 1 WhatsApp click and 2 phone taps.
   • Where visitors went next: 114 sessions to the schools finder, 15 to /o-a-level-classes/, 13 to contact, 9 to the fee page and 4 to the O Level tuition centre page.
4. **Clicks have halved since last year, but rankings have not dropped.**
   • 21 Sep to 17 Oct: 1,194 clicks this year against 2,522 last year (down 53%). Impressions are down 40%.
   • Rankings held. "best schools in karachi" was at position 2.4 last year and 2.0 now.
   • Fewer people click at the same position. On that query CTR fell from 19.6% to 10.7%. *Inference:* AI Overviews or other search features are taking clicks.
5. **The page contradicts itself.** The title and H1 say "Top 15", the meta description says "top 10", and the headings number 14 schools. One FAQ answer says a list of schools by area is "coming soon", but the schools finder already exists.

## c) Verdict

**Worth improving, and the work should go into turning visitors into leads, not into more traffic.**
• It brings about 2,000 Karachi visitors a month, mostly parents.
• Small changes have a big effect at this volume. At the O Level page's lead rate it would produce dozens of leads a quarter instead of 4 (inference).

---

## d) Actions, quick wins first

1. **Fix the title, H1 and meta description so they match the real count**, and add the year and a reason to click. For example: title "Top 14 Schools in Karachi With 2026-27 Fees". *Effect:* consistent search snippets, and a CTR gain at position 2 (inference).
2. **Replace the outdated FAQ answer with a link to the schools finder.** *Effect:* removes a stale promise. The finder already gets 114 visits from here.
3. **Add a short parent-focused call to action after school #3 and at the end.** For example: "Picked the school? O Level tuition with a free demo class." Link it to /o-a-level-classes/ and WhatsApp. *Effect:* more leads from 2,000 monthly visitors (inference).
4. **Fix the two links that point to `sjacademy.pk` without www.** *Effect:* removes a redirect.
5. **Update the fee data for 2026-27, starting with the line that says "As of 2025".** Check the figures against each school's source before publishing. *Effect:* keeps rankings for the fee queries, which make up 2,880 clicks.
6. **Add alt text to the 21 images that have none.** *Effect:* accessibility and image search.
7. **Before admissions season in Feb to April, refresh the page and resubmit it in Search Console.** *Effect:* protects the April peak.
