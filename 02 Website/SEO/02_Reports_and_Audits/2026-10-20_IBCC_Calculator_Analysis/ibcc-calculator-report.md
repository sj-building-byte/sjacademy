## a) The 5 most important findings

1. **It is a new page and still small.** Its first Google impression was 23 Aug 2026. Since then it has had 158 clicks from 6,623 impressions (CTR 2.39%, position 6.9). GA4 shows 421 sessions starting here.
2. **It is the best lead source of the three pages analysed today.** In 90 days, sessions that started here produced:
   • 4 `submit_lead_form` events and 5 sessions that reached /demo-class-registration/
   • 2 WhatsApp clicks
   • That is about 1% of sessions. The schools finder produced at most 1 lead from 186 sessions, and the schools blog post 4 key events from 6,011 sessions.
   • GA4 records 0 key events here, because `submit_lead_form` and `whatsapp_click` are not marked as key events.
3. **Its audience is students across Pakistan, not just Karachi.** Karachi 130 sessions (31%), Lahore 80, Islamabad 44, Multan 21, Rawalpindi 20, plus Saudi Arabia 14. Engagement is high at 76%. That suits your online classes (inference).
4. **Under half the traffic comes from Google.** Google organic 161, direct 110, reachlee.co 82, Instagram 36, Bing 25, ChatGPT 3. The reachlee.co referral and the Instagram posts drove the September peak.
5. **Impressions are rising but clicks are flat.** Last 28 days: 79 clicks from 4,296 impressions (1.84% CTR). Previous 28 days: 79 clicks from 2,320 impressions (3.41% CTR). Rankings sit at position 7 to 9 for the main queries, the bottom of page 1.

7. **Add a short explainer and FAQ section below the calculator.** Cover how IBCC converts grades, the 2026 A* values and which subjects count. *Effect:* moves the main queries up from position 7 to 9 and wins the O Level variants (inference).
