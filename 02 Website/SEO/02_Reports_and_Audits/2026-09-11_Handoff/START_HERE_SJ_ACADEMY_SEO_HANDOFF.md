## 12. Education accuracy and trust issues

These are the highest-risk findings because they can affect student decisions and reduce the site's eligibility as a trustworthy source for search and AI systems.

### IBCC equivalence calculator

The published calculator needs a teacher/editor and developer review against current official rules.

Observed risks:

- It appeared to convert A* to 90 automatically.
- Current IBCC guidance can assign subject- and session-specific values above 90 for A*.
- It appeared to choose a best-eight or best-three result without sufficiently applying group requirements.
- Equivalence is not simply the highest marks in every case. Compulsory subjects, subject groups, year/session and intended field matter.

Required action:

- document every supported qualification route;
- use current official conversion tables;
- apply compulsory-subject and group rules;
- clearly label routes that the tool cannot certify;
- show the calculation and assumptions;
- state when official IBCC confirmation is needed;
- create worked test cases before republishing or promoting the tool.

### Subject-combination guidance

The A Level subjects article requires correction and more precise wording.

Observed risks:

- pre-medical Physics was described as highly recommended when the relevant equivalence group requires Physics, Chemistry and Biology;
- FAST computing guidance was presented too broadly when current computing eligibility centres on Mathematics, while engineering criteria are separate;
- the article risked mixing IBCC equivalence requirements with individual university admission requirements;
- claims that all universities use an identical three-subject calculation are unsafe.

Required action:

- separate IBCC equivalence, university eligibility and programme-specific selection criteria;
- cite current primary sources for each institution;
- state the date checked;
- avoid blanket statements across Pakistani universities;
- have a teacher or admissions specialist approve the final guidance.

### Other trust inconsistencies

Reconcile these before expanding promotion:

- fees: admission and practical fee amounts differed across pages/forms;
- batch dates: the Business page and FAQ displayed different June start dates;
- proof: homepage and schema used different A* result counts;
- review totals: different pages used different review counts;
- an AI-oriented guide reportedly used an unsupported statistic that 80% of students need tuition;
- named institutional and teacher affiliations need verification;
- testimonials should be traceable and used with consent;
- `1-on-1 attention` must be defined if teaching is actually delivered in groups.

The audit did not determine which conflicting fee, date or proof figure is correct. The academy must provide one current source of truth.

### Cambridge syllabus transition

Cambridge confirmed that O Level Business Studies 7115 changes to Business 7081 for first assessment in June 2027.

Do not replace all legacy 7115 resources indiscriminately. Keep historical papers accurately labelled, update forward-looking guidance and create a clear transition note for affected cohorts.

## 13. Structured data and entity findings

All 166 scanned pages contained a custom `TutoringService` declaration. During verification, `https://schema.org/TutoringService` returned 404 and no matching documented type was found in the focused Schema.org search.

Treat the type as unverified/unsupported. Recommended approach:

- use a documented organisation type such as `EducationalOrganization` where accurate;
- use documented `Service`, `Course` and `Offer` entities for real offerings where supported;
- connect page entities to one consistent organisation `@id`;
- use the complete verified address;
- use one accurate entity name;
- remove keyword-stuffed `alternateName` values that are not genuine business names;
- reconcile result counts, review totals and other facts before including them in schema;
- validate the resulting JSON-LD and test representative page types.

Schema helps machines understand supported facts. It does not force Google to classify or rank a site, and it cannot compensate for contradictory information.

FAQ content can remain useful for people, but Google's documentation updates recorded the retirement of FAQ rich results from May 7, 2026. Do not count FAQ schema as a current rich-result win.

## 14. AI search and citation strategy

The positive finding is that SJ Academy already has measurable AI-related visibility:

- 429K impressions in the inspected GSC generative AI features report;
- 307 GA4 AI Assistant sessions in the reviewed 28 days;
- 296 sessions attributed specifically to `chatgpt.com / ai-assistant`;
- 4 AI Assistant key events in that period.

The existing strategy should move away from `citation bait` and toward reliable, original reference material.

Recommended AI-search principles:

- answer one real parent or student question clearly on each page;
- show who reviewed the guidance and when;
- cite primary sources for equivalence, syllabus, deadlines and admissions claims;
- use clear headings and tables when they make information easier to verify;
- publish teacher explanations, worked examples and original resources;
- keep dates, fees, statistics and programme claims consistent;
- allow relevant search crawlers deliberately and verify access in server/Cloudflare logs;
- distinguish OAI-SearchBot, used for search discovery, from GPTBot, used for model-training collection;
- do not assume an `llms.txt` file or special AI schema is required by current Google guidance;
- avoid manufactured mentions or scripted keyword reviews.

Measurement should distinguish:

- AI-feature impression;
- brand mention;
- linked citation;
- referral session;
- enquiry;
- qualified enquiry;
- demo attendance;
- enrolment and revenue.

Maintain a repeatable question panel covering Karachi tuition, nationwide online tuition, Business 9609, practicals, past papers, subject combinations and IBCC. Record engine, date, locale, exact question, answer, cited URL and factual accuracy.

The September 10 audit did not conduct a reproducible multi-engine prompt panel and therefore did not claim ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity citation share.
