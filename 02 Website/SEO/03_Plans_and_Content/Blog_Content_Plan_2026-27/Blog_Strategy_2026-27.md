**Goal:** make the SJ Academy blog the go-to place for O and A Level students and parents in Pakistan (and Cambridge students worldwide), across resources, subject advice, tech, study spots, websites/apps, careers and shareable community content. Keep SJ Academy top of mind for every student and parent in the process.

**Companion file:** `Blog_Calendar_Oct-Dec_2026.csv` (36 dated posts, 3 per week, Mon/Wed/Fri, 5 Oct to 25 Dec 2026).

Rule of thumb: roughly 40% tuition-supporting, 45% audience/affiliate, 15% brand/share.

## 3. How each type of post earns its place

- **Tuition posts** (resources, subject advice, parents' guides): measured by clicks to tuition pages, WhatsApp clicks and `generate_lead`. Always end with a relevant, low-pressure CTA (free demo or WhatsApp question), and link to the matching past-paper hub and tuition page.
- **Audience/affiliate posts** (tech, websites, spots, careers): measured by organic sessions and affiliate clicks. They need affiliate links, a clear affiliate disclosure, prices and dates that are genuinely current, and a soft SJ Academy mention (resources, newsletter).
- **Shareable posts:** measured by social shares, Instagram saves, Reddit/backlink referrals and branded searches ("sj academy"). **Be realistic:** Buzzfeed-style posts rarely rank in Google. They work through distribution: Instagram carousels, Reddit (already 640 sessions and 113 backlinks in the 10 Sep sample), WhatsApp groups and student ambassadors. Every shareable post needs a social version planned before it is written.
- **Original data** (student survey, year in review) is the best way to earn backlinks and AI citations. Aim for one such piece per quarter.

## 6. Quality rules for every post

- Named author or reviewer on subject and exam posts (your teachers are the advantage competitors lack).
- Facts about exams, IBCC, universities and prices come from official or current sources, with the date checked. Never invent statistics.
- Affiliate disclosure on every post with affiliate links. Prices marked "as of [date]".
- Own photos for study spots. Original graphics for shareable posts (no copyrighted memes or GIFs).
- Only change the "updated" date when the content really changed.
- No em dashes in copy. Natural, student-friendly tone.
- Each post links to: its pillar hub, 2 to 3 related posts, and the matching tuition page or resource.
- Mobile first: short paragraphs, tables that scroll, fast images.

## 7. Distribution (every post)

1. Instagram: carousel or reel version (shareable posts are designed for this first).
2. YouTube: short or full video for subject advice and tech posts, linking to the post.
3. WhatsApp: student and parent broadcast lists.
4. Reddit: genuinely helpful answers in Pakistani student communities, linking only when relevant.
5. Newsletter: weekly roundup.
6. Internal links: add links to the new post from 2 to 3 existing high-traffic posts.

## 8. Measuring success

| Track | Metric | Check |
|---|---|---|
| All | New posts indexed within 2 weeks | GSC URL inspection |
| Audience/affiliate | Organic clicks per post at 8 and 12 weeks; affiliate clicks | GSC + GA4 `affiliate_click` (to be set up) |
| Tuition | Clicks to tuition pages from blog posts; WhatsApp clicks; `generate_lead` from blog landing pages | GA4 |
| Brand/share | Instagram saves/shares, Reddit referrals, backlinks, branded search volume | Instagram insights, GA4, GSC "sj academy" queries |
| Refreshes | CTR and clicks vs the baseline in the calendar's evidence column | GSC, 4 weeks after refresh |

Review the calendar monthly: double down on pillars that grow and drop formats that don't.

## 9. Capacity assumption

The calendar assumes **3 posts per week** (about 12 per month). If that is more than your team can do well, cut the Friday slot first (2 per week). Quality matters more than volume: one strong, accurate post beats three thin ones, especially under Google's helpful-content signals.
