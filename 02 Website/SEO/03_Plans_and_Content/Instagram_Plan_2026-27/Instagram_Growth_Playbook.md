# SJ Academy Instagram Growth Playbook (Oct 2026 to Jan 2027)

**Goal:** make @sjacademypk the account every O and A Level student in Pakistan follows, and the one their parents trust. Turn that attention into enquiries and enrolments.

**Companion file:** `Instagram_Calendar_Oct2026-Jan2027.csv`. It has 91 posts, one a day from Mon 5 Oct 2026 to Sun 3 Jan 2027, each with a hook, concept, CTA, teacher, blog tie-in, goal and boost/collab note.

**Built on:** the 30 Sep 2026 analysis of 373 posts, 1,042 stories, audience data and 42 ads (`02_Reports_and_Audits/2026-09-30_Instagram_Analysis/`). Nothing here guarantees virality. Every choice below is weighted towards what has already worked on this account.

---

## 1. The formula (from your own data)

| What your data shows | What we do |
|---|---|
| Carousels bring 78% of organic follows and 5x the saves | 4 carousels a week are the backbone |
| Tech/tools posts get 42 saves per 1k reach; exam technique gets the best follow rate (2.33 per 1k) | Weekly "Tool Drop" and "Examiner Mode" series |
| 0-8s reels have the highest share rate (98 per 1k) but few follows | One 5-8s POV reel a week for reach, with a pinned comment pointing to a useful post |
| Comment-to-get: median 75 comments vs 5, and 70% more follows | Weekly "Comment-to-Get", DM delivers a UTM link |
| Teachers named in only 10 of 373 captions | Weekly "Sir Says" reel, always named |
| 43.5% of the audience is 35+ (women 35-44 are the largest group) | Weekly "Parent Guide" carousel |
| ~73% of the audience is outside Karachi | Online classes mentioned in every enrolment post; city-specific posts for Lahore and Islamabad |
| Stories: link stickers get 3.8% clicks but appear on only 10% of stories | A link sticker and a quiz/poll every day |
| Ads: Messages objective at PKR 100 to 150 per conversation for batch offers; 30% of spend reported no results | Boost with Messages or website-visitor objectives only, always with UTMs |
| More posts in Q3 didn't add follows | 1 strong post a day, no filler reels |

## 2. The weekly rhythm (7 series)

| Day | Series | Format | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Examiner Mode** | Carousel | Subject exam technique. Save + follow |
| Tue | **Sir Says** | Teacher reel, 30-45s | Named teacher, one tip. Trust + tuition |
| Wed | **Tool Drop** | Carousel | Tech, apps, websites, careers, tied to that week's blog post. Saves + affiliate/blog clicks |
| Thu | **POV** | Reel, 5-8s | Relatable moment, trending audio. Shares + reach |
| Fri | **Parent Guide** | Carousel | For mothers and fathers. Trust + enquiries |
| Sat | **Comment-to-Get** | Reel or carousel | "Comment WORD": DM delivers a UTM link. Comments + site visits + leads |
| Sun | **Sunday Special** | Varies | Study spots, collabs, spotlights, big share pieces, or a boosted enrolment post |

Series names matter. When followers recognise "Sir Says" or "Examiner Mode", they come back for the next one, and the algorithm rewards repeat viewers.

## 3. Daily stories (5 or 6 frames, most important first)

1. **Link sticker** to that day's post, blog or tool, with a UTM tag (`utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=<post>`)
2. **Past Paper Question of the Day**: quiz sticker with a real past-paper MCQ. Reveal the answer the next day to build a daily habit
3. Reshare of the day's feed post
4. Poll or question box. Wednesdays: "Ask Sir ___ anything"
5. Behind the scenes: classes, teachers, campus, students (with consent)
6. Optional: enrolment reminder (online + Karachi)

## 4. Viral mechanics checklist (every post)

**Hooks**
- The first second or first slide must state a problem or promise in under 8 words: "Stop losing these 3 marks", "The only apps you need", "POV: pens down and you're mid-sentence".
- Formulas that work for this audience: "Stop ___", "You're doing ___ wrong", "The only ___ you need", "___ in 60 seconds", "POV: ___", "Parents: ___", numbers ("10 mistakes", "25 websites").

**Carousels**
- Slide 1 is the hook only: big text, brand yellow and brown.
- One idea per slide, 7 to 10 slides. The last slide gives a reason to save or follow.
- Remake winners every 4 to 6 weeks. Calculator Tricks Part 2 remakes the 5,004-save post.

**Reels**
- On-screen text throughout, because most people watch with the sound off.
- Trending audio from Instagram's library for POV reels. Teacher reels use their own voice plus captions.
- Loop the ending into the start.

**Captions**
- Line 1 repeats the hook. Then the value, then **one** CTA: save, share, comment WORD, or message us.
- Put the website or WhatsApp CTA on every useful post, not just promos.

**Hashtags:** 3 to 5 relevant ones (#olevel #alevel #caie #olevelspakistan #sjacademy). They matter far less than hooks.

**After posting**
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Pin the best comment, and turn good questions into reply-reels.
- Share to stories within 30 minutes.

**Accuracy:** exam rules, dates, thresholds, quotes and prices must be checked against official sources. Leaks get verified facts and advice only, never leaked material.

## 5. Growth levers beyond posting

1. **Collab posts.** Instagram's co-author feature shows the post to both audiences. Plan 2 a month with:
   - student creators in Lahore and Islamabad (you reach ~48% outside Karachi but have no local faces there)
   - Karachi study-spot venues
   - other education creators (never direct competitors)
2. **Student Ambassador programme (launch in November).** 1 or 2 students per major city and school. They post "study with SJ" content, share resources in class groups, and earn perks (free resources, merch, discounts). This is how "every student knows us" happens beyond the feed.
3. **UGC challenge: #SJStudyWithMe.** Students post their study setup or timelapse and tag @sjacademypk. Reshare the best weekly and feature winners on Sunday.
4. **Broadcast channel "SJ Exam Alerts".** For timetables, results dates, threshold releases and verified news. Followers get notified, which keeps them close during results months.
5. **Profile fixes (do this week)**
   - **Bio:** "O & A Level classes · DHA Karachi + Live Online across Pakistan · Free tools & past papers ↓"
   - **Link in bio:** a UTM-tagged links page on sjacademy.pk (tools, blog, WhatsApp, enrol)
   - **Highlights:** Online Classes · Results · Free Tools · Teachers · Reviews · Practicals · Enrol
   - **Pinned posts:** best current enrolment post, the top-saved tool post, and a teacher intro
6. **Remix and reply.** Answer popular comments with reels. Remix trending education reels with a teacher's correction or tip, credited and respectful.

## 6. Paid support (small, targeted)

| When | Boost | Objective | Targeting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly (Sunday enrolment posts) | Batch/registration offer | **Messages** (past best: PKR 100 to 150 per conversation) | Two campaigns: Karachi (parents 35-54 + students 16-24, physical + online) and Pakistan excl. Karachi + Gulf (online) |
| Monthly | Best organic carousel of the month | Profile visits or reach | Pakistan 16-24 |
| Practicals | Practicals workshop | **Website visitors** to the practicals page with a WhatsApp button (past: PKR 3/click vs PKR 500 to 1,200 per chat) | Karachi A Level science students |
| Never | "Get more leads through messaging" unless lead questions are set up and tested | n/a | n/a |

**Rules**
- Pause any messaging ad above about PKR 300 per conversation after 3 days.
- UTM-tag every website ad.
- Label every ad conversation in WhatsApp/DMs with the ad name, and count enrolments weekly.

**Monthly review:** find the top 5 posts by follows per 1k reach and by saves. Remake them in a new format next month, and drop series that underperform for 4 weeks straight.

## 8. Production notes

- **Batch-shoot teacher reels:** one 2-hour session a month per teacher gives 4 "Sir Says" reels.
- **Templates:** create Canva/Figma templates for each series (consistent colours: yellow `#FCE004`, brown `#453516`, orange `#fe6100`), so a post takes 30 to 45 minutes.
- **Accessibility:** dark text on orange (white on orange fails contrast), large fonts, captions on all reels.
- **Consent:** written consent before featuring any student, their results or photos.
- **No em dashes** in captions. Keep copy natural and student-friendly.
- **Tone with Cambridge:** firm and factual on issues like leaks, never insulting. Parents and schools are watching too.
