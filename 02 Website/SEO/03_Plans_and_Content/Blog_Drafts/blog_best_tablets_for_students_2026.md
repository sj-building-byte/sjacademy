## Why does the stylus matter so much for O and A Level past papers?

If you take essay-based subjects like Sociology, History or Law, you can get away with a Bluetooth keyboard.

If you take **Mathematics (4024 / 9709)**, **Physics (5054 / 9702)**, **Chemistry (5070 / 9701)**, **Accounting (7707 / 9706)** or **Economics (2281 / 9708)**, a tablet without a proper palm-rejection stylus is just an expensive YouTube screen.

Here is why an active stylus matters so much for Cambridge science, maths and commerce students:

1. **Working out carries marks:** In O and A Level Maths, Physics and Accounting, method marks (M1) depend on writing out step-by-step equations, T-accounts and force diagrams directly below the question.
2. **Graph and diagram precision:** Whether you are shifting a macroeconomic AD/AS curve in Economics or drawing ray diagrams in Physics, cheap rubber-tip capacitive pens lag and block your view of the grid lines.
3. **True palm rejection:** Official active pens (Apple Pencil, Samsung S-Pen, Xiaomi Smart Pen, Redmi Smart Pen) let you rest your wrist naturally on the glass while writing fast under timed conditions.

**Rule of thumb:** Always calculate the **total bundle price** (tablet + stylus + protective case) before you buy.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

**Q: Can I take a tablet into a Cambridge O or A Level exam hall?**
A: No. Under official Cambridge International exam day rules, tablets, smartwatches, mobile phones and any other electronic communication or storage devices are strictly banned inside the exam hall. Use your tablet for home revision and timed past paper practice, but practice writing with a black or dark blue pen on physical paper in the final two weeks before your exam so your hand stamina is ready.

**Q: Is 64GB enough storage on an iPad for O and A Level students?**
A: Yes, if you only use your iPad for PDF past papers, digital notebooks ([Goodnotes or Notability](https://www.sjacademy.pk/blog/notability-vs-goodnotes/)), and streaming live or recorded classes. Thousands of past paper PDFs take up less than 5GB. However, if you download large offline video lectures or games, 64GB will fill up quickly, and the 128GB [iPad 11 (A16)](https://priceoye.pk/tablets/apple/apple-ipad-11-a16), 128GB [Samsung Tab S9 FE](https://priceoye.pk/tablets/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s9-fe-wifi-x510) / [Tab S10 FE](https://priceoye.pk/tablets/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s10-fe-x520-wifi), or 256GB [Xiaomi Pad 6](https://priceoye.pk/tablets/xiaomi/xiaomi-pad-6) / [Pad 7](https://qeemat.com.pk/dp/121174/xiaomi-pad-7-price-in-pakistan) will serve you better.

**Q: Do cheap third-party styluses on Daraz work as well as the official Apple Pencil?**
