Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do A Levels privately in Pakistan?
Yes! Thousands of students in Pakistan appear as private candidates every year for both O Levels and A Levels through the British Council. Universities globally (including IBA, LUMS, and AKU) accept private candidate grades equally to regular school candidates.
What are the British Council private candidate fees for 2026?
The exact fee fluctuates based on the GBP to PKR exchange rate. Typically, an O Level subject costs between Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 35,000, while an A Level subject costs between Rs. 35,000 to Rs. 45,000. SJ Academy generates your exact, updated fee voucher during our registration process.
How do I do Science Lab Practicals as a private candidate?
This is the biggest challenge for private candidates. You must find an academy that allows you to perform physical labs. SJ Academy is one of the few institutes in Karachi offering dedicated lab practicals for A Level private candidates. Once you register through us, you can inquire about our Lab batches.
What documents do I need to register privately?
You will need a valid Smart NIC or Passport, recent passport-sized photographs, and your previous CAIE result statements (especially if you are carrying forward AS grades to A2 with your UCI number). We will guide you through organizing all of these.
