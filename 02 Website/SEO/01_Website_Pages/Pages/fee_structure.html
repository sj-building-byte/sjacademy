Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden charges besides the tuition fee?

No. SJ Academy believes in transparent pricing. Apart from the Rs. 3,500 one-time registration fee for new students, you only pay the monthly tuition fee for your selected subjects, or the one-time lab fee if applicable.

How does the lab practical fee work?

The A Level lab practical fee is a one-time payment of Rs. 25,500 per subject. This covers the entire practical preparation course for the exam session, without any recurring monthly costs for labs.

What is the difference between Regular and Oct/Nov batches?

Regular batches run throughout the academic year at standard pacing. The Oct/Nov batches are accelerated, intensive programs designed specifically to prepare students for the Oct/Nov series in a shorter timeframe, hence the different fee structure.

Does the monthly fee include the official British Council CAIE exam fee?

No, our monthly tuition covers the coaching, assessments, and study resources provided at SJ Academy. The final CAIE exam registration fee is paid separately to the British Council.

Are study materials and past papers included in the tuition fee?

Yes. We provide all essential topical worksheets, past papers, and proprietary revision notes as part of your regular tuition. There are no surprise costs for basic academy study materials.

Do you offer any scholarships or financial aid?

Yes, we believe every bright student deserves top-tier education. We offer merit-based scholarships and need-based financial aid for deserving students. Please reach out to our administration office to discuss eligibility.
