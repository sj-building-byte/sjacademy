Frequently Asked Questions

Is A-Level Math (9709) hard compared to O-Level?
Yes, there is a significant difficulty jump, particularly in Pure Mathematics 3 (P3) and Mechanics (M1). O-Level Add Math provides a good foundation, but A-Level questions require a much deeper understanding of application rather than just memorizing formulas. Our classes focus heavily on bridging this gap through visual learning and topic-by-topic past paper drills.
What is the syllabus structure for A-Level Mathematics 9709?
The Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics (9709) syllabus requires candidates to take four papers. Typically, AS Level students take Pure Mathematics 1 (P1) and either Probability & Statistics 1 (S1) or Mechanics (M1). For the full A Level, candidates must add Pure Mathematics 3 (P3) and the remaining applied paper (S1, S2, or M1) depending on their selected route.
