Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

At SJ Academy ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to transparent educational practices and the highest quality of Cambridge O Level, IGCSE, and A Level coaching. We believe in providing prospective students with the opportunity to evaluate our teaching methodology before making a financial commitment. This Refund and Cancellation Policy clearly defines how trial sessions, tuition fees, multi-month advance packages, and withdrawals are handled.

Quick Summary: Students may attend a free demo class with zero obligation and pay only once satisfied to enroll. For a complete list of monthly tuition rates and subject packages, please visit our official Fee Structure page. Once enrolled, tuition fees become strictly non-refundable after the student has taken at least three (3) classes. Approved refunds are processed via bank transfer within 10 to 14 business days.

1 Free Demo Classes & Post-Trial Enrollment

We want every student and parent to feel completely confident in our faculty, teaching pace, and learning environment:

  • Free Demo Session: Prospective students are invited to attend a free demo/trial class without paying any upfront tuition fees.
  • Post-Demo Payment: Students and parents only proceed with fee payment after attending the demo session and confirming they are satisfied and wish to continue with SJ Academy.
  • Enrollment Finalization: Once satisfaction is confirmed and fee payment is submitted, the student's seat is secured in their designated batch.

2 The 3-Class Non-Refundable Rule

To ensure fairness while protecting the operational continuity and limited seat capacity of our batches:

  • Satisfaction Window: If a newly enrolled student experiences irreconcilable scheduling or academic difficulties within their first two (2) classes, they may submit a withdrawal request for review.
  • Non-Refundable Threshold: Tuition fees become strictly 100% non-refundable once the student has attended at least three (3) classes in the session or billing cycle.
  • Attendance Definition: Attendance is counted across physical attendance at our DHA campus, joining live interactive online lectures, or accessing class session links.

3 Advance 3-Month Packages (Quarterly Drop-Out Policy)

Students who take advantage of our 10% advance tuition discount (detailed on our Fee Structure page) by prepaying for three (3) months upfront are governed by the following drop-out terms:

  • Completed Months: Completed months (where the student attended classes) are considered fully utilized and non-refundable.
  • 70% Refund on Remaining Months: If a student drops out after one (1) or two (2) months of a 3-month prepaid cycle, only 70% of the remaining unused month's fee will be refunded at the discounted rate.
  • 30% Administrative Retention: The remaining 30% of the unused balance is retained by SJ Academy to cover administrative onboarding costs, batch seat holding reservations, and the forfeiture of the advance quarterly promotion.

4 General Situations Not Eligible for Refund

Outside of the provisions outlined in Sections 1, 2, and 3, fees paid to SJ Academy are non-refundable and non-transferable under standard circumstances, including:

  • Change of Mind: Deciding to drop a subject or discontinue after taking three (3) or more classes.
  • Missed Classes & Absenteeism: Classes missed due to school tests, family travel, private commitments, or illness. (Recorded lectures and class materials are provided where available to help students catch up).
  • Partial Month Discontinuation: Stopping attendance mid-month without prior written notice before that month commenced.
  • Failure to Utilize Resources: Inability or failure to join live online lectures, watch recorded classes, or complete assigned mock examinations.
  • Disciplinary Dismissal: Suspension or removal resulting from behavioral misconduct, harassment, academic dishonesty, or violation of our Code of Conduct.

5 Registration & Admission Fees

Every student enrolling in regular cohorts pays a one-time registration fee (PKR 3,000, as listed on our Fee Structure):

  • This fee covers administrative setup, portal configuration, student database management, and diagnostic batch allocations.
  • The registration fee is strictly non-refundable and non-transferable once paid, regardless of class attendance.

6 Science Lab Practical Workshops & Recordings

Specialized workshops and auxiliary digital services have custom resource requirements:

  • A Level Science Practicals (Physics, Chemistry, Biology): Because laboratory apparatus, chemical reagents, and instructor workstations are strictly booked per seat (see our official Fee Structure for practical workshop charges), practical workshop fees are non-refundable and non-transferable once a workshop seat is confirmed.
  • Archive Recording Access (PKR 8,000): Fees paid for accessing past months' digital lecture archives are strictly non-refundable, as full digital access to proprietary recordings is unlocked immediately upon payment verification.

7 Advance Exam Months Payment Protection

For students appearing in CAIE external examination sessions:

  • Oct/Nov Session: September and October tuition fees are payable together in advance before the start of September.
  • May/June Session: April and May tuition fees are payable together in advance before the start of April.
  • Because our faculty dedicates extensive time to intensive mock evaluations, individual paper marking, and exam marathons during these periods, advance final-month fees are non-refundable once the revision phase has commenced.

8 Withdrawal Notice Procedure

To officially discontinue classes and avoid being billed for upcoming months:

  • Notice Submission: Written notice must be communicated via WhatsApp (+92 336 2144454) or email (contact@sjacademy.pk) prior to the 1st day of the next billing month.
  • Active Enrollment: If a student remains active in classroom WhatsApp groups, attends sessions, or receives teacher support into a new month without advance written notice, the full monthly fee remains payable.

9 Refund Processing Timeline & Method

When an eligible refund or drop-out adjustment is verified and approved by SJ Academy:

  • Disbursement Method: All refunds are issued exclusively via direct online bank transfer to the bank account registered by the student or parent. Cash refunds are not disbursed at the reception desk.
  • Timeline: Eligible refunds will be disbursed within 10 to 14 business days from the date of formal written approval by the accounts office.
  • Deductions: Applicable inter-bank transfer charges or transaction fees may be deducted from the net refunded amount.
