O Level and A Level: Which Class in Pakistan?

O Level is equivalent to Matric (SSC, Class 9 and 10). A Level is equivalent to Intermediate (HSSC or FSc, Class 11 and 12).

AS Level lines up with Class 11 (First Year) and A2 Level with Class 12 (Second Year). To use your Cambridge results for Pakistani admissions, you need an equivalence certificate from the IBCC.

Many Pakistani schools spread O Level over three years (O1 to O3), so students may sit O Level exams in what would be Class 10 or 11. IBCC sets the subject requirements for equivalence and updates them from time to time, so check the current rules on ibcc.edu.pk or estimate your marks with our IBCC equivalence calculator.
