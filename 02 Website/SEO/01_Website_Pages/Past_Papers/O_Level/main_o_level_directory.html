How to Use O Level Past Papers

Do not solve papers randomly. Start with topic-wise practice, then move to full timed papers. After each paper, check the marking scheme and write down exactly why marks were lost.

For science and maths subjects, focus on working, units, formulas and command words. For theory subjects, focus on structure, keywords, examples and examiner expectations.
