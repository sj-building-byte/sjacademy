    How this calculator works and what it supports

    Each grade is converted using the relevant published IBCC table. A* values depend on the subject, level and examination year. A–E convert to 85, 75, 65, 55 and 45.

    Standard Pakistani-national routes with exams entirely in Pakistan or entirely abroad are supported. Only listed subjects are supported. Exemptions, substitutions, mixed-location exams, retakes, AS-only results and double awards need individual review. Grade 2 requires IBCC confirmation; it is not treated as zero.

    For SSC, we show a converted-subject percentage and raw subtotal. We do not display certificate marks because the published SSC examples conflict. For supported legacy HSSC cases, we also show the unrounded estimate on a 1,100-mark scale.

    The February 2026 science-group notice concerns O Level completion in 2026 and A Levels thereafter. It does not apply automatically to all 2027 applicants or to Humanities. Future examination years are blocked until their data is verified.
