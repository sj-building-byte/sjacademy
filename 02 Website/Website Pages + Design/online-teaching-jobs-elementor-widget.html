The job

Marked answers, not just lectures

SJ Academy classes are built on Cambridge past papers. Students write answers in class, teachers mark them against the mark scheme, and every student sees where each mark went. If you already teach this way, you will fit right in.

  • Teach to the Cambridge syllabus and mark scheme for your subject
  • Set past-paper questions in class and mark the answers
  • Tell parents when a student starts falling behind
  • Start on time, every class. Karachi traffic is an explanation, not an excuse.

FAQ

Questions teachers ask us

Do I need teaching experience to apply?

No. Fresh graduates are welcome. If you know your subject well and can explain it clearly, apply. We talk about how you teach on the screening call.

Can I teach online from outside Karachi?

Yes. SJ Academy runs live online classes, so you can apply from anywhere in Pakistan. In-class teaching happens at our campus in DHA Phase 7, Karachi.

Which subjects are you hiring for?

O Level and A Level Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Business, Economics, Accounting, Computer Science, English Language, English Literature, Urdu, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat, Law, Psychology and Sociology. If your subject is not listed, add it under “Others” in the form.

Do you hire for IELTS and entry test prep?

Yes. Tick IELTS or Entry Test in the form, and name the tests you prepare students for, such as IBA, LUMS or DOW.

Can I teach part-time?

Yes. Tell us in the form whether you are free on weekdays, weekends or both, and we match classes to those times. Classes run on weekends too.

How long does the application take?

About 3 minutes. The form asks for your name, email, WhatsApp number, subjects, levels, availability and highest qualification. A CV is optional but recommended.

What happens after I apply?

We read every application ourselves. If your subjects and timings fit, we call you for a short screening call. Once approved, we match you with students and you start teaching.
