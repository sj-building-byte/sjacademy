LahorePrivateChecked October 2026

LUMS eligibility criteria for A Level students

BBB average across your best three A Levels, plus 8 O Levels (with Maths) averaging B. Science equivalence for SBASSE; Pre-Engineering for Electrical and Chemical Engineering.

LUMS SAT requirement (and ACT, LCAT)

SAT, ACT or LCAT (your choice). Law also needs the HEC LAT.

Holistic: grades, test, essays and the application as a whole. No published formula.

LUMS deadline and key dates, Fall 2027

  • 27 January 2027 Online application deadline (5 pm)
  • 28 January 2027 Documents and processing fee due
  • 26 February 2027 Financial aid application deadline
  • 6 March 2027 Last SAT sitting LUMS accepts
  • 21 March 2027 LCAT (tentative date)
  • 10 April 2027 Last ACT sitting LUMS accepts

Worth knowing

  • AS subjects and General Paper do not count towards the best three.
  • Already finished A Levels and short of the grades? Only October/November 2026 resits count for Fall 2027. June 2027 results are too late to fix a shortfall.

  • SAT scores from before March 2025 and ACT scores from before February 2025 are not accepted. LUMS takes your best single sitting.

Institute of Business Administration (IBA), Karachi admission criteria for A Level students

KarachiPublic sectorChecked October 2026

IBA Karachi eligibility criteria for A Level students

BBC for BBA, Accounting and Finance, Business Analytics. BCC for every other programme. Maths for Computer Science, Economics and Mathematics, and BS Mathematics.

IBA aptitude test and SAT score

IBA aptitude test (English and Maths MCQs). Top SAT scorers can get direct admission or an interview.

How IBA Karachi decides admission

Aptitude test result, then an interview for business programmes. No published weightage.

IBA Karachi fees

2026-27: Rs. 31,500 per credit hour, plus a one-time admission fee of Rs. 125,000.

ProgrammeExtra requirement
BBAAt least 2 Bs and 1 C
BS Accounting and FinanceAt least 2 Bs and 1 C
BS Business AnalyticsAt least 2 Bs and 1 C

IBA Karachi deadlines and key dates, Fall 2027

  • 9 December 2026 Round 1 application deadline
  • 20 December 2026 Round 1 aptitude test
  • 8 March 2027 Round 2 application deadline
  • 21 March 2027 Round 2 aptitude test
  • 9 June 2027 Round 3 application deadline
  • 20 June 2027 Round 3 aptitude test

Worth knowing

  • There is no minimum SAT score. Only the top of the SAT merit list gets direct admission or an interview, so IBA strongly advises everyone to sit the aptitude test as well.
  • One common test covers all three schools, but you pay and apply separately for each school. You cannot add a school later.
  • ACT is also accepted: composite 29 with English 25 for BBA and Business Analytics; composite 28 for the other programmes.
  • All O and A Level students need an IBCC equivalence certificate.

National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) admission criteria for A Level students

Islamabad (NET centre in Karachi)PublicChecked October 2026

NUST eligibility criteria for A Level students

60% in both SSC and HSSC equivalence. Maths, Physics and Chemistry (or CS) for engineering; Maths for computing; any subjects for BBA and social sciences.

NUST admission test

NUST Entry Test (NET), up to four series. ACT/SAT route for some programmes.

How NUST decides admission

NET 75% + O Level equivalence 25% while you are still in A Level.

NUST fees

Per semester: Rs. 171,350 (engineering, computing, natural sciences) or Rs. 250,380 (business, social sciences, architecture), plus Rs. 35,000 one-time processing fee.

BBA (NUST Business School)Any A Level subjects

BS EconomicsAny A Level subjects

BS MathematicsMathematics required

Biotechnology, Food Science, Environmental Science, AgricultureBiology, Chemistry and Physics (Food Science also takes Maths, Physics, Chemistry)

NUST deadlines and key dates, Fall 2027

  • 14 November 2026 NET-1 registration closes
  • 17 November 2026 NET-1 tests begin
  • 20 April 2027 ACT/SAT route opens
  • 25 July 2027 ACT/SAT route deadline
  • NET-2 registration runs December 2026 to January 2027, NET-3 February to March 2027, NET-4 April to May 2027 (tentative).

Worth knowing

  • You can sit NET in more than one series. NUST counts your best score.
  • NET in Karachi is paper-based at the DHA City centre. Islamabad and Quetta are computer-based.
  • ACT/SAT route (Fall 2027): business, social sciences and LLB need SAT 550+ in each section or ACT 25+; engineering and computing need ACT 25+ with ACT Science 25+. Same 75/25 merit split.
  • Biology, Chemistry and Physics students can apply to NUST engineering and computing programmes; computing then needs extra maths courses in year one.

Monash (Australia) admission test

No admission test. English: IELTS 6.5 or an accepted English qualification.

How Monash (Australia) decides admission

Entry score from your best three A Levels: A* or A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1, plus 1 bonus point for an A*.

Before you apply

The minimum is the start line

Grades get you through the door. The admission test usually decides the seat. At NUST, NET is 75% of your merit; at GIKI the test is 85%.

  • Book your tests early. NET-1 and IBA Round 1 both close before the end of 2026.
  • Apply for IBCC equivalence as soon as your results allow. Almost every university asks for it.
  • Apply to more than one university. Deadlines do not care about load-shedding, or about which university replied first.

FAQ

University admission questions

What A Level grades does LUMS ask for in Fall 2027?

LUMS asks for at least three full A Levels averaging B across your best three, plus eight O Levels, including Maths, averaging B. AS subjects and General Paper do not count. You also need the SAT, ACT or LCAT, and the online application closes on 27 January 2027.

What is the LUMS SAT requirement?

LUMS publishes no minimum SAT score. Every undergraduate applicant sends one of the SAT, the ACT or the LUMS LCAT. For Fall 2027 the last SAT sitting LUMS accepts is 6 March 2027, scores from before March 2025 do not count, and LUMS uses your best single sitting.

What grades does IBA Karachi need?

BBA, Accounting and Finance, and Business Analytics need at least two Bs and a C. Computer Science, Economics, Economics and Mathematics, Social Sciences and Mathematics need at least a B and two Cs, and Computer Science and the maths programmes also need A Level Maths. Everyone sits the IBA aptitude test unless they are high on the SAT merit list.

What is the NUST eligibility criteria for A Level students?

You need 60% in both SSC (O Level) and HSSC (A Level) equivalence from IBCC, and the right A Levels for the programme: Maths, Physics and Chemistry or Computer Science for engineering, Maths for computing, and any subjects for BBA and the social sciences. While you are in your final A Level year, merit is 75% NET and 25% O Level equivalence.

Can I study engineering with A Level Biology?

At NUST, yes: Biology, Chemistry and Physics is on the list for engineering programmes. GIKI, NED, FAST, PIEAS, Habib and LUMS engineering all ask for Maths and Physics, so Biology without Maths closes most engineering doors in Pakistan.

Do I need IBCC equivalence to apply?

Yes. Every Pakistani university in this tool asks O and A Level students for an IBCC equivalence certificate. NUST, FAST, NED, PIEAS and GIKI also use your O Level equivalence in merit while A Level results are pending. Our IBCC equivalence calculator gives you the exact figure.

Which Fall 2027 deadlines come first?

NUST NET-1 registration closes on 14 November 2026. IBA Round 1 closes on 9 December 2026, and LUMS closes on 27 January 2027. Habib runs test series until February 2027. GIKI, NED, FAST and AKU usually open in spring.

Can I check universities abroad with the same grades?

Yes, for Manchester, Toronto and Monash. Manchester sets an A Level offer per course, Toronto checks prerequisite subjects, and Monash turns your best three A Levels into a score. None of them needs IBCC equivalence, but each needs proof of English, such as IELTS 6.5 or an accepted English qualification.

Does meeting the minimum mean I will get in?

No. The minimum only lets you apply. Seats go to the strongest test scores and applications, and popular programmes fill well above the minimum. Treat a pass here as permission to compete.
