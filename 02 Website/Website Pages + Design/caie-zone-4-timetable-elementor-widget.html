    FAQ

    Questions about the date sheet

    What are the Oct/Nov 2026 exam dates in Zone 4?

    Papers run from Monday 28 September 2026 to Friday 13 November 2026: 259 papers across 35 exam days. Search the timetable on this page by subject name or code.

    When will the May/June 2027 date sheet be released?

    Cambridge publishes the final June series timetable in October of the year before, so October 2026 for May/June 2027. Cambridge’s 2026 calendar lists it under October. As of 28 October 2026 it is not published yet, and this page is updated as soon as it is.

    Where do I find the official Cambridge timetable?

    Cambridge International Education, which students also write as CAIE or CIE, posts each zone’s timetable as a PDF on the Cambridge International website. Exams officers also receive it through Cambridge Direct. Private candidates should check with the centre they entered through.

    Is Pakistan in Zone 4?

    Cambridge publishes one timetable for each administrative zone, and centres in Pakistan are in Zone 4. Papers can fall on different dates in different zones, so check your centre’s zone with your exams officer before you trust any date sheet, including this one.

    What do AM, PM and EV mean?

    They are Cambridge’s three exam sessions: morning, afternoon and evening. Your centre sets the start time under Cambridge’s key time rules, so ask your exams officer for the exact time. Every candidate in a session must be under full centre supervision at the key time.

    What is the difference between the provisional and final timetable?

    The provisional timetable goes to exams officers in May, and centres have four weeks to comment on it. The final one follows in October. Cambridge marks changes from the provisional version in red on the final timetable. See when Cambridge releases timetables.

    Will the timetable change after it is published?

    Sometimes. Cambridge can reissue a timetable: the June 2026 Zone 2 timetable, for example, carries a Version 2 dated January 2026. We note the version on this page and update it when a new one appears.

    Are AS and A2 papers on the same timetable?

    Yes. Cambridge lists IGCSE, O Level, AS Level and A Level papers together for each zone. A2 papers are labelled A Level (AL) in Cambridge’s timetable.

    Can private candidates use this timetable?

    Yes. Private candidates sit the same papers on the same dates as everyone else in their zone. You must be entered through a centre first, and your Statement of Entry confirms your venue. See our private candidate registration page.
