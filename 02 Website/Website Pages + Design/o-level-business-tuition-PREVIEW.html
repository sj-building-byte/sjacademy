The problem

Knew the topic. Lost the marks.

O Level Business feels easy in class, because most of it makes sense the first time you hear it. Then the paper comes back. The marks rarely go on what students do not know. They go in these four places.

The answer fits any business

Application is 30% of the marks in 7081. An answer that never uses the business in the question could have been written without reading it, and it is marked that way.

The point stopped too early

“Prices fall, so sales rise” is a start, not analysis. The marks are in the next link: what that does to revenue, profit or the business’s aim.

Two sides, no decision

A question that says “justify your answer” wants a verdict and a reason. “It depends” is only an answer when you say on what.

The numbers had no working

Break-even, margins, ratios and cash flow all show up in 7081, and a wrong final figure with the formula and working shown can still keep marks.

None of this means your child is bad at Business. These are writing habits, and habits change with answers that are marked properly.

Paper 2 style · operationsKarachi Kicks makes trainers by hand with 40 workers. Orders have doubled this year. The owner could buy machines that would replace 15 workers. Do you think Karachi Kicks should buy the machines? Justify your answer. [6]
depends on WHAT? decide.4/6

Pick a line on the answer above

The last line · 2 out of 3

The point and the reasoning are right, but nothing ties them to Chai Spot. A line that fits every business in the world earns no application mark. Full marks: Bilal has to repay Rs. 500,000 plus interest out of what two tea stalls earn, so if the new stall sells less chai than he expects, the repayments could leave Chai Spot short of cash.

The last line · 3 out of 4

The chain starts well, then jumps to a conclusion. Every sale now brings in 10% less, so revenue only rises if the number of sales rises by more than 10%. That is likelier here, because the competitor sells similar clothes and is two shops away. Full marks come from showing that condition, not from a guess.

The last line · 4 out of 6

Both sides are argued and applied, then the answer refuses to decide. “It depends” earns nothing unless it says on what, and which way it would go. Full marks: Karachi Kicks should buy the machines, because with orders doubling, the sales it loses by being slow cost more than the machines, as long as the owner explains the change to the workers who stay.

K = knowledge. App = application, using the business in the question. An = analysis, a developed chain of reasoning. The last line in each answer above was where the application, the final link of the analysis or the evaluation should have been. The syllabus

The O Level Business syllabus (7081), paper by paper

O Level Business covers six topics: understanding business activity, people in business, marketing, operations management, financial information and decisions, and external influences on business activity. Every candidate sits two papers of equal weight, and every question is compulsory.

Paper 1Short answer and data response

  • 1h 30mduration
  • 80marks
  • 50%of the O Level

What it asks. Four compulsory questions, each built on its own short stimulus such as a table, a graph or an image, mixing short answers with structured data response. From 2027 each question has four parts.

How to prepare. Learn the definitions word for word, then practise using the stimulus in every answer above the smallest marks, because that is where the application marks are.

Paper 2Case study

  • 1h 30mduration
  • 80marks
  • 50%of the O Level

What it asks. Four compulsory questions on one business, described in an insert with appendices such as tables, graphs, newspaper extracts and adverts. From 2027 every part (a) is an applied question.

How to prepare. Read the case once for the story and once for the numbers, use the appendices in your answers, and practise ending every “justify” question with a decision and a reason.

7115 to 7081: what changes from 2027

Cambridge O Level Business Studies (7115) becomes O Level Business (7081) for exams from June 2027. Most of the content carries over, so 7115 past papers are still good practice. These are the changes that matter in class.
