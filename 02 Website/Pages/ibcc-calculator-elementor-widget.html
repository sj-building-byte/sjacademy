How IBCC works out your equivalence

IBCC (the Inter Boards Coordination Commission) converts Cambridge and Edexcel grades into Pakistani board marks so you can apply to universities and colleges in Pakistan. There are three steps.

3. The right subjects are added up

  • O Level (Matric, SSC), taken in Pakistan: English, Maths, Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies and 3 electives. The total out of 800 is scaled to 900. Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Computer Science put you in the Science group.
  • O Level, taken abroad or foreign/dual national: English, Maths and 3 electives. The total out of 500 is scaled to 900.
  • A Level (FSc, HSSC): your 8 O Levels plus 3 A Levels for your group, out of 1100. Pre-Medical needs Physics, Chemistry and Biology; Pre-Engineering needs Physics, Chemistry and Maths; Computer Science needs Physics, Maths and Computer Science; Humanities takes any 3 subjects.
  • A Level, exempt students: 5 O Levels plus 3 A Levels, out of 800, scaled to 1100.
  • The February 2026 rule: for science-group students who finish O Levels in 2026 or later, only the 5 compulsory O Levels and the 3 A Levels count (English, Maths and the A Levels if you are exempt). Your O Level electives still need a pass.

Worked example from IBCC: eight O Level A*s from 2021 add up to 750 out of 800. 750 / 800 × 900 = 843.75, so the SSC equivalence is 844 out of 900 (93.78%). Add three A Level A*s (93, 93 and 94) and the HSSC equivalence is 1030 out of 1100 (93.64%).

FAQ

IBCC equivalence questions

How does IBCC calculate O Level equivalence?

IBCC turns each grade into marks out of 100: A is 85, B 75, C 65, D 55 and E 45, and A* gets a subject value set for each exam session (93 or 94 in 2026). If you took O Levels in Pakistan, your 8 subjects (English, Maths, Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies and 3 electives) give a total out of 800, which is scaled to 900. If you took them abroad, or you are a foreign or dual national, 5 subjects (English, Maths and 3 electives) give a total out of 500, also scaled to 900.

How does IBCC calculate A Level equivalence for FSc?

For most students your 8 O Levels and 3 A Levels are added together, giving marks out of 1100 with no scaling. If you are exempt from Urdu, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies, 5 O Levels and 3 A Levels give a total out of 800, which is scaled to 1100. For Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering and Computer Science students who finish O Levels in 2026 or later, the February 2026 rule applies instead (see below).

What is an A* worth in IBCC equivalence in 2026?

For the 2026 session, an O Level A* is worth 93 in English and Urdu and 94 in every other subject. An A Level A* is worth 95 in Physics, 93 in Computer Science and 94 in Chemistry, Biology, Maths and other subjects. The values change every year, so the calculator uses the table for the session in which you sat each subject. Before 2021, A* counted as a flat 90.

Does the February 2026 IBCC rule apply to me?

Only if you are in a science group (Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering or Computer Science) and finish your O Levels in 2026 or later. For you, IBCC counts only the 5 compulsory O Levels plus your 3 A Levels, and your O Level electives no longer count towards the marks, though you still need to pass them. If your O Levels finished in 2025 or earlier, the old rule still applies and all 8 O Levels count. Humanities is not covered by the new rule. The calculator picks the right rule from the O Level years you enter.

Is O Level equivalence out of 900 or 1100?

We show it out of 900. IBCC's worked example gives 750 out of 800 as 844, which is 750 / 800 x 900. Its older example, Cambridge's guide for Pakistan and other calculators also use 900. IBCC's newest copy of that page, uploaded in April 2026, labels the formula 1100 but still shows 844, so we have kept 900 until IBCC corrects it. Your percentage is the same either way.

Who is exempt from Urdu, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies?

Foreign nationals and dual nationals are exempt. For O Level (SSC) equivalence, so are students who took their O Levels abroad. For A Level (HSSC) equivalence, Pakistani students are exempt only if they took both their O Levels and A Levels abroad. If you took one in Pakistan and one abroad, you still need to pass all three.

Can I use IGCSE 9 to 1 grades?

Yes. IBCC converts 8 to 87, 7 to 82, 6 to 77, 5 to 70, 4 to 60, 3 to 50 and 2 to 40. Grade 9 is listed with A* as above 90, so the calculator uses the subject's A* value for it. Grade 1 is not accepted. In the calculator, open "More options" and tick the 9 to 1 box.

Which grades does IBCC not accept?

IBCC only accepts grades A* to E (or 9 to 2). A U, or an F or G at IGCSE, does not count, and you need at least an E in every subject IBCC requires for your group.

What if I sat my subjects in different years?

IBCC works out each subject using the A* table for the session you sat it in, so the same grade can be worth slightly different marks. Open "More options" in the calculator, tick "different years" and set the year for each subject. The calculator handles every session from 2006 onwards, with 2027 and 2028 as forecasts until IBCC publishes them.

Is this my official IBCC equivalence?

No. This is an estimate using IBCC's published rules. Your official equivalence certificate comes from IBCC when you apply with your original results, and universities use that certificate. SJ Academy is not part of IBCC.
