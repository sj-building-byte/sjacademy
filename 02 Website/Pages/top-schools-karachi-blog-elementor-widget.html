1. Karachi Grammar School (KGS)

Karachi Grammar School traces its history to 1847 and took its current name in 1879. It teaches the Cambridge curriculum through O Level and A Level, across Kindergarten, Junior, Middle and College Sections.

  • Curriculum: Cambridge (O Level and A Level)
  • Location: Kindergarten, Junior and College Sections in Clifton. Middle Section in Saddar
  • Founded: 1847

2. Bay View Academy (BVA)

Bay View Academy opened in August 1990 and moved to its purpose-built Defence campus in 1999. It added a Clifton campus in 2013 and a PECHS campus in 2015, and teaches from the early years up to Cambridge O Level.

  • Curriculum: Cambridge (O Level)
  • Locations: DHA (Defence), Clifton, PECHS
  • Founded: 1990

Fees Estimate

Tuition of about PKR 38,480 to 46,375 a month depending on the class, an admission fee of about PKR 290,000 and a refundable security deposit of about PKR 40,000. These are estimates, so confirm them with the campus.

Official website

3. Haque Academy

Haque Academy was established in 2008 on 32nd Street in DHA Phase 8. It teaches from Grade 1 up to Cambridge O Level, and many of its students join from the affiliated Mrs Haque's Nursery.

  • Curriculum: Cambridge (O Level)
  • Location: DHA Phase 8
  • Founded: 2008

Fees Estimate

Tuition of about PKR 40,000 to 55,000 a month depending on the grade, plus an admission fee and a refundable security deposit at enrolment. These are estimates.

4. The Lyceum

The Lyceum was founded in 1987 on Hatim Alvi Road in Old Clifton. It is a co-educational school for A Level only, built around a two-year Cambridge A Level programme for students aged about 16 to 19.

  • Curriculum: Cambridge (A Level only)
  • Location: Old Clifton
  • Founded: 1987

Fees Estimate

A non-refundable registration fee of about PKR 150,000, a security deposit of about PKR 70,000 and tuition of about PKR 50,000 to 56,000 a month depending on the programme. The Lyceum does not publish fees online, so treat these as estimates.

How to choose a school in Karachi

  1. Pick the curriculum first. Cambridge (O Level and A Level), Edexcel (IGCSE), American or Matric. Switching later costs your child time.
  2. Time the school run. Karachi traffic does not care about the school timetable. Do the drive at 7:30 am before you decide.
  3. Add up the full yearly cost. Admission, security deposit, tuition, annual charges, books, uniform, transport and exam fees, not just the monthly fee.
  4. Judge the campus, not the network. Large networks differ from campus to campus. Ask for that campus’s O Level results and meet the teachers for your child’s subjects.
  5. Check what happens after O Level. Some schools stop at O Level or IGCSE, so your child may need to move for A Level. See our guide to A Level colleges in Karachi.

FAQ

Questions parents ask about Karachi schools

Which are the top schools in Karachi?

There is no official ranking of Karachi schools. The 14 on this list are some of the best known in the city, across Cambridge, Edexcel, American and Matric streams. The right one for your child depends on the curriculum you want, how far you can travel each day and the total yearly cost.

Which school has the highest fees in Karachi?

Of the schools on this list that publish fees, Karachi American School is the most expensive: USD 17,134 a year for Grade 12 in 2026-27, including tax, plus a one-time registration fee of up to USD 11,330 for new students. Karachi Grammar School does not publish its fees.

Which schools in Karachi offer O Level?

On this list, Karachi Grammar School, Bay View Academy, Haque Academy, Habib Public School, St. Joseph’s Convent School, Foundation Public School, Beaconhouse, The City School, and Karachi Public School teach Cambridge O Level. British Overseas School and British International School teach IGCSE instead.

How much are school fees in Karachi?

On this list, monthly tuition runs from about PKR 7,000 to 20,000 at Foundation Public School to about PKR 55,000 to 65,000 at Karachi Grammar School (both estimates). Karachi American School and British Overseas School charge in US dollars and British pounds. Admission fees and security deposits are often the largest single payments.

Which school is better, The City School or Beaconhouse?

Neither network is better everywhere. Both teach Cambridge O Level and A Level, and the campus matters more than the name. Compare the specific campus: its O Level and A Level results, the teachers for your child’s subjects, the total yearly cost and the distance from home.

What do school fees in Karachi usually include?

Most schools charge a one-time admission fee, a security deposit that is usually refundable, monthly or annual tuition and annual charges for labs and activities. Books, uniform and transport cost extra. Cambridge exam fees for O Level and A Level are usually billed separately.
