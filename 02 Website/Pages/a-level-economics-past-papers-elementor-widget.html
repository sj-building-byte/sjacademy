How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPapers 1 and 3 are shorter multiple choice papers. Papers 2 and 4 are longer, with data response and essay questions.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeOn Paper 2 and 4, check whether you used the data given, not only whether the economics was correct.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksA missed evaluation point, a diagram not explained in words, or an answer that ignored the extract. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9708 past papers

Are these A Level Economics past papers free?

Yes. Every question paper and mark scheme on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9708 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge AS and A Level Economics (9708) past papers for May/June 2015-2026 and Oct/Nov 2015-2025, Papers 1 to 4, in all three variants.

What is on each Economics paper?

Paper 1 and Paper 3 are multiple choice. Paper 2 and Paper 4 are data response and essay papers, where you work from extracts, tables and charts to answer structured questions and write essays.

How do I read a 9708 past paper file name?

Every file follows the Cambridge pattern 9708_s25_qp_12. The letter is the session (s = May/June, w = Oct/Nov), the two digits are the year, qp is the question paper and ms is the mark scheme. The last two digits are the paper number followed by the variant, so 12 means Paper 1, Variant 2.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a session. It gives you three papers to practise from each series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. On Paper 2 and Paper 4, check where you lost marks for not using the data given, not only for missing economics content. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks convert to grades.

Is this the same as IGCSE or O Level Economics past papers?

No. This page is for Cambridge International AS and A Level Economics, syllabus 9708. IGCSE and O Level Economics is a separate syllabus, 2281, with its own past papers.
