The problem

Why O Level marks slip, even when students work hard

Most O Level students put in the hours. Fewer put them into the right thing. Rereading the notes a fourth time feels productive. It mostly just feels familiar.

Knowing is not scoring

Cambridge asks you to use what you know on a question you have not seen. Remembering the notes is where the answer starts, not where it ends.

Command words decide marks

Describe, explain and evaluate each ask for a different answer. A student who writes the same answer for all three leaves marks behind.

Feedback arrives too late

If the first properly marked paper you see is your mock, there is very little time left to fix what it shows.

The method

Every class runs on the same method

Different subjects, different teachers, one way of working. It is the reason an O Level coaching center should exist at all.

  1. Learn the syllabus properlyConcepts first, taught by a subject specialist, in the order the syllabus builds them.
  2. Practise on past papersReal Cambridge questions on every topic, so the exam style is never a surprise.
  3. Get marked to the mark schemeYour answers are marked the way an examiner marks them.
  4. Find out where the marks wentFeedback shows which marks were lost and what to write next time.
