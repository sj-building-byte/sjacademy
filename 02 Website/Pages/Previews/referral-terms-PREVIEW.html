1. Who these terms are between

These terms are between SJ Academy, Plot 29C, Lane No. 5, DHA Phase 7, Seher Commercial Area, Karachi (“SJ Academy”, “we”), and the person who joins the SJ Academy referral program (“you”, the “referrer”). A “friend” is anyone you refer to SJ Academy. By joining the program, you agree to these terms.

2. Who can join

  • Anyone who does not work at SJ Academy can join: current and former students, parents, school teachers, content creators and anyone else.
  • Under 18: a parent or guardian has to agree before you join. We ask for their name and WhatsApp number, and we pay your earnings into their account.
  • SJ Academy staff cannot join. This includes teachers, admin, finance and all other staff, full-time or part-time.
  • One account per person. We close duplicate accounts.
  • We can decline an application to join.

3. Your referral code

  • After you join, we send you a personal referral code on WhatsApp. It is your first name followed by two digits, for example ALI27.
  • Your code is for you only. You cannot sell it or give it to someone else to use as their own.
  • Your code can reach us in any of these ways: in the WhatsApp message your friend sends using your share link, when your friend tells us your code in their first message, call or visit, or in the registration form.

4. Which friends count

A friend is a qualified referral when all of these are true:

  1. They are new to SJ Academy. They have not studied with us in the 12 months before they enrol.
  2. We did not already know them. Before your code reached us, they had not messaged us, called us, visited us, filled in our form or booked a demo class.
  3. Your code reached us first. It reached us before they enrolled, and they enrolled within 60 days of first contacting us.
  4. They joined a monthly class. They enrolled in an O Level, IGCSE, AS or A2 class, in class or live online, and paid the monthly tuition fee.
  5. They stayed past 30 days. They were still enrolled, with their fee paid, 30 days after they enrolled.

These do not count:

  • Your own child or anyone who depends on you, if you are a parent or guardian.
  • Yourself.
  • A friend who joins only lab practicals, a practical workshop, mock exams, an ebook or another one-time course.
  • A code given to us after your friend has enrolled. Codes cannot be added or changed later.

First code wins. If a friend gives us more than one code, the first code recorded in our records counts.

6. The Rs. 5,000 bonus

  • You get Rs. 5,000 when your 5th friend qualifies, another Rs. 5,000 at your 10th, and so on. There is no limit.
  • Only friends who are qualified referrals and still enrolled count towards a bonus.
  • We pay the bonus in the payout after the friend who completes the five qualifies.
  • Once paid, a bonus is not taken back if a friend leaves later, unless the referral is cancelled under section 10.

7. What your friend gets

  • Your friend gets Rs. 1,000 off the one-time admission fee, as long as your code reached us before they enrolled and they are new to SJ Academy (section 4, points 1 and 2).
  • The discount is given once per student and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Your friend can sit one free demo class before paying anything, with or without a code. Lab practicals have no demo class.

8. Getting paid

  • Statement: at the end of each month, we work out your earnings for the fees we received that month and send you a statement on WhatsApp. It shows each friend’s first name, the months that earned and any bonus.
  • When: between the 5th and 10th of the following month.
  • How: bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or cash at the SJ Academy counter if you tell us in advance.
  • Whose account: the account must be in your name, or your parent’s or guardian’s name if you are under 18.
  • Minimum: we pay once your balance reaches Rs. 1,000. A smaller balance carries over to the next month.
  • Unclaimed money: if we cannot pay you for 6 months because your payment details are missing or we cannot reach you, after we have tried, the unpaid balance is cancelled.
  • You are responsible for any tax that applies to your earnings.

9. The first 30 days and refunds

  • If your friend leaves, or gets a full fee refund, within 30 days of enrolling, the referral does not count and earns nothing.
  • If we have already paid you for a friend whose fee is later refunded, we take that amount from your future earnings.

10. Rules for sharing

Share your code with people you know, on your own social media and in groups you belong to. You must not:

  • Post your code on SJ Academy’s own pages, posts, comments, Google reviews or groups.
  • Send spam, or message people who have not agreed to hear from you.
  • Promise grades, results, fees or discounts that are not in these terms.
  • Pretend to be SJ Academy or its staff, or create pages or accounts using the SJ Academy name or logo.
  • Run paid ads on “SJ Academy” or similar search terms.
  • Create fake students or fake enquiries, or refer yourself.

If you break these rules, we can cancel the referrals involved, close your account and cancel any unpaid earnings from those referrals.

11. Your information and your friend’s

  • We use your name, WhatsApp number, email and payment details only to run the program and pay you.
  • Your statement shows your friend’s first name and which months earned. We never share their fees, attendance, grades or contact details with you.
  • Only give us a friend’s number if they have agreed to hear from us.

12. Questions and disputes

  • Check each statement. If something looks wrong, tell us on WhatsApp within 14 days of receiving it.
  • We check our enrolment and fee records and reply. Those records decide who referred whom and what was paid.

13. Leaving and changes

  • You can leave at any time by messaging us. We pay what you have earned up to the end of that month.
  • We can close your account if you break these terms.
  • Changes: we can change these terms or end the program. We will give you 30 days’ notice on this page and on WhatsApp.
  • Friends who qualified before a change keep earning at the old rate until the end of the current exam session (May/June or Oct/Nov).
  • If the program ends, you keep earning for friends who already qualified until the end of the current exam session.
  • Earlier terms: friends who qualified under the terms dated December 2025 move to these terms on 1 December 2026.

14. Law and contact

  • These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan, and the courts of Karachi deal with any dispute.
