The problem

Fluent Urdu. Average marks.

Speaking Urdu at home helps, but 3248 is a written exam with strict tasks and word limits. Students who speak it fluently still lose marks in these four places.

The summary ran over 100 words

Paper 1 asks for a paragraph-length summary of no more than 100 words, in your own words as far as possible. Long or copied summaries lose marks.

The register was wrong

An email to a friend and a report for a newspaper are not written the same way. Purpose, format and audience are part of the mark.

The translation kept English word order

Paper 2 ends with a 150-word passage from English into Urdu, worth 15 marks. Word-for-word translation reads like English written in Urdu script.

Grammar slipped under pressure

Sentence transformation and the grammar cloze test verb forms, agreement and post-positions, and small slips add up quickly.

None of this means your child's Urdu is weak. These are exam skills, and they improve with writing that is marked properly.

Paper 1 · Reading and Writing · 50 marks

Exercise 1 Short-answer questions8 marks

Single words or phrases from a short text such as an advert or leaflet. Read the question carefully and give only what it asks for.

Exercise 2 Multiple matching9 marks

Match sentences to short paragraphs. Look for meaning, not a repeated word, because the matching word is often a trap.

Exercise 3 Note-making9 marks

Brief notes under given headings from a longer text. Notes should be short and specific, not copied sentences.

Exercise 4 Summary, up to 100 words10 marks

A paragraph about the Exercise 3 text, in your own words as far as possible. Stay under 100 words.

Exercise 5 Writing, about 150 words14 marks

A short functional piece such as an email. The purpose, format and audience are given, and all three are marked.

Paper 2 · Grammar, Writing and Translation · 50 marks

Exercise 1 Sentence transformation5 marks

Rewrite five sentences using a prompt without changing the meaning. One wrong verb form can cost the mark.

Exercise 2 Multiple-choice cloze10 marks

Fill gaps from options: verb forms, agreement, comparatives, post-positions and vocabulary in context.

Exercise 3 Extended writing, about 200 words20 marks

The biggest single task. Organise ideas in paragraphs with linking words, and use the right register.

Exercise 4 Translation, English to Urdu15 marks

About 150 words from English into Urdu. Translate the meaning in natural Urdu word order, not word by word.

Mark totals from the Cambridge O Level Second Language Urdu 3248 syllabus for exams in 2027 to 2029. Dictionaries are not allowed in either paper.
