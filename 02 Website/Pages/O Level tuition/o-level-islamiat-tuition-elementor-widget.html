The problem

Knew the topic. Answered a different question.

O Level Islamiyat is marked on how directly an answer meets the question. Both papers carry equal weight, and these are the four places the marks usually go.

A retelling, not an answer

The 14-mark questions need essay-length answers, marked in levels. A general retelling of the topic sits low. A direct answer with specific detail sits higher.

The passage was repeated, not explained

Question 1 in each paper sets passages from the prescribed lists. The answer has to explain them, not repeat them.

Names and events went missing

Specific people, events and examples are what lift an answer. The Caliph did many good things is not a fact.

Four answers, ninety minutes

Each paper asks for four answers in 1 hour 30 minutes. The 8-mark question should not get 14-mark time.

None of this means the content is the problem. It means the answer did not match the question. That part is fixable.

Question 1 Major themes of the Qur'an: two passages8 marks

Two passages chosen from the three set. Explain what each passage teaches instead of repeating it, and keep the answer short for the marks.

Question 2 The history and importance of the Qur'an14 marks

An essay-length answer, usually in more than one part. Plan it, and include specific details about how the Qur'an was revealed, compiled and used.

Two more questions From the life of the Prophet and the first Islamic community28 marks

You choose two more answers of 14 marks each. Pick the questions where you can give names, events and reasons, not just the topics you like.

Paper 2 · 1 hour 30 minutes · 50 marks

Question 1 Major teachings in the Hadiths: two passages8 marks

Two passages chosen from the four set. Explain the teaching in each passage and what it means for how a Muslim should live.

Question 2 The history and importance of the Hadiths14 marks

An essay-length answer, usually in more than one part. Include how the Hadiths were collected and why they matter.

Two more questions From the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the Articles of Faith and the Pillars of Islam28 marks

You choose two more answers of 14 marks each. Use events, policies and examples as evidence, and answer the exact question that is set.
