How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPapers 1 and 2 are 1 hour 30 minutes, Paper 3 is 1 hour 15 minutes and Paper 4 is 1 hour 45 minutes.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeCheck that you used sociological concepts, studies and evidence, and that the essay weighs more than one view.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksCommon sense with no sociology, a theory named but not applied, or an essay with only one side. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9699 past papers

Are these A Level Sociology past papers free?

Yes. Every file on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9699 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge International AS and A Level Sociology (9699) past papers from 2020 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, in every paper and variant we hold. Each year shows the paper names used that year.

Did the 9699 papers change in 2021?

Yes. From 2021 there are four papers: Socialisation, Identity and Methods of Research; The Family; Education; and Globalisation, Media and Religion. In 2020 there were three: The Family, Theory and Methods, and Social Inequality and Opportunity.

Which papers are AS and which are A Level?

From 2021, Papers 1 and 2 make up AS Level. Papers 3 and 4 complete the A Level.

How do I read a 9699 past paper file name?

Take 9699_w25_qp_12. 9699 is Sociology, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 12 is Paper 1, Variant 2. ms is the mark scheme.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.

Know the theory but still losing marks?

Sociology marks reward concepts and evidence used to argue, not opinions. Miss Tayyaba Yaseen teaches A Level Sociology at SJ Academy in DHA Phase 7, Karachi, and live online, with past papers marked to the mark scheme.
