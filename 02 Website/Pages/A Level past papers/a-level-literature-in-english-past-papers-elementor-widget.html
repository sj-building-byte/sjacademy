How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingEach paper is 2 hours for two essays, so about an hour an essay including planning.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeLiterature mark schemes use level descriptions. Find the level your essay fits, then read what the next level asks for.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksRetelling the plot, quotations with no analysis, or an argument that drifts from the question. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick a question on the same text or form from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9695 past papers

Are these A Level English Literature past papers free?

Yes. Every file on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9695 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge International AS and A Level Literature in English (9695) past papers from 2015 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, in every paper and variant we hold. Each year shows the paper names used that year.

Is this English Language or English Literature?

This page is Cambridge International AS and A Level Literature in English, syllabus 9695. English Language (9093) and English General Paper (8021) are separate subjects.

Did the 9695 papers change in 2021?

Yes. From 2021 the papers are Drama and Poetry, Prose and Unseen, Shakespeare and Drama, and Pre- and Post-1900 Poetry and Prose. Up to 2020 the papers were numbered 3 to 7. Set texts change often, so check the texts for your exam year in the syllabus.

How do I read a 9695 past paper file name?

Take 9695_w25_qp_12. 9695 is Literature in English, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 12 is Paper 1, Variant 2. ms is the mark scheme.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.

Know the texts but still losing marks?
