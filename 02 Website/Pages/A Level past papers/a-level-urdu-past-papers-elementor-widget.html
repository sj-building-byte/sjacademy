The exam

What is on each Urdu paper

As set in the October/November 2025 papers. Check the current syllabus on the Cambridge website before your exam.

PaperTimeMarksWhat you do
Paper 2 Reading and Writing1 hour 45 minutes70Answer all the questions, in Urdu. The reading passages are in the insert. You write on the question paper.
Paper 3 Essay1 hour 30 minutes40Write one essay in Urdu: 16 marks for content and 24 for quality of language. You write in an answer booklet. Dictionaries are not allowed.
Paper 4 Texts2 hours 30 minutes75Answer three questions in Urdu, each on a different set text: at least one from Section 1 and one from Section 2. You write in an answer booklet.
How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 2 is 1 hour 45 minutes, Paper 3 is 1 hour 30 minutes and Paper 4 is 2 hours 30 minutes for three answers.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeOn the Essay, 24 of the 40 marks are for language. Check both columns of the mark scheme, not only the content.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksLanguage slips that cost more than you think, an essay that drifts from the title, or a Texts answer that retells the story. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same task from another series and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9686 past papers

Are these A Level Urdu past papers free?

Yes. Every question paper, mark scheme and insert on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9686 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge International A Level Urdu (9686) past papers from 2015 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, Papers 2, 3 and 4.

Why does A Level Urdu start at Paper 2?

Cambridge assesses A Level Urdu (9686) with three papers, numbered 2, 3 and 4: Reading and Writing, Essay and Texts. There is no Paper 1 to download.

How do I read a 9686 past paper file name?

Take 9686_w25_qp_02. 9686 is Urdu, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 02 is Paper 2. Mark schemes are named ms_2, ms_3 and ms_4. Some older files are named qp_2 instead of qp_02.

Why are there no variants for A Level Urdu?

Cambridge sets one version of each A Level Urdu paper per series. Subjects sat in many time zones, such as Mathematics or Physics, have several variants instead.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.
