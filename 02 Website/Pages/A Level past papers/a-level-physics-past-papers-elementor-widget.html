What is on each Physics paper

As set in the October/November 2025 papers. Check the current syllabus on the Cambridge website before your exam.

PaperTimeMarksWhat you do
Paper 1 Multiple Choice1 hour 15 minutes4040 multiple choice questions. Answer all of them on the answer sheet.
Paper 2 AS Level Structured Questions1 hour 15 minutes60Answer all questions. You write on the question paper.
Paper 3 Advanced Practical Skills2 hours40Practical work in the lab. Answer all questions on the question paper. The confidential instructions (CI) tell the school how to set up.
Paper 4 A Level Structured Questions2 hours100Answer all questions. You write on the question paper.
Paper 5 Planning, Analysis and Evaluation1 hour 15 minutes30Answer all questions on planning an experiment and analysing data. You write on the question paper.
How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 1 is 1 hour 15 minutes for 40 questions. Papers 2 and 5 are 1 hour 15 minutes. Paper 4 is 2 hours.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeCheck the key words, the working and the units. On Paper 5, check every step of the method, not only the conclusion.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksA missing unit, working that skips the formula, or an uncertainty calculated the wrong way. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9702 past papers

Are these A Level Physics past papers free?

Yes. Every file on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9702 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge International AS and A Level Physics (9702) past papers from 2015 to 2026, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, in every paper and variant we hold. Each year shows the paper names used that year.

Which papers are AS and which are A Level?

Papers 1, 2 and 3 make up AS Level. For the full A Level you also sit Papers 4 and 5.

Why does Paper 3 have so many variants?

The practical paper is set in more versions than the written papers, so Paper 3 often runs from Variant 1 to Variant 5 or 6. They test the same skills. Practise the ones closest to your exam series.

Did the paper timings change?

Yes. Paper 1 was 1 hour in 2015 and has been 1 hour 15 minutes since 2017. Check your own series for the timing.

How do I read a 9702 past paper file name?

Take 9702_w25_qp_12. 9702 is Physics, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 12 is Paper 1, Variant 2. ms is the mark scheme, and ci is the confidential instructions.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.
