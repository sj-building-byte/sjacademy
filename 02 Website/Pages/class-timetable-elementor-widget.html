FAQ

Questions about class timings

Where do I find the SJ Academy class schedule?

On this page. Choose O Level, AS Level, A2 Level or lab practicals, then tap your subjects to see the days and times for each one.

Are classes held in-class or online?

Classes run in-class at our DHA Phase 7 campus in Karachi and live online. A class marked "online" on the timetable runs online only.

What does "Batch 2" mean on the timetable?

It is a second batch of the same subject at a different time. If the first batch clashes with another subject, the second one may fit.

What if two of my subjects are at the same time?

Message us on WhatsApp with your subjects. We will tell you which batches fit together.

Does the timetable change?

Sometimes, for example when a new batch starts. This page shows the current timetable, and your teacher will tell you about any change to your class.

Can I try a class before I register?

Yes, for O Level and A Level classes: the first demo class is free. Lab practicals have no demo class; students join directly.

What time zone are the timings in?

All timings are in Pakistan Standard Time. If you join online from another country, convert them to your local time.
