FAQ

Questions before you start sharing

Who can join the referral program?

Anyone outside SJ Academy. Current students, old students, parents referring other families, school teachers and content creators can all join. You do not have to study at SJ Academy. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian has to agree, and payouts go to their account. Parents cannot refer their own children, and people who work at SJ Academy, teachers included, cannot join.

How much do I earn for each friend?

You earn 10% of one subject’s monthly fee for each friend, however many subjects they take. On standard fees, that is Rs. 850 a month for an O Level friend and Rs. 1,050 a month for an A Level friend.

What does my friend get?

Rs. 1,000 off the one-time admission fee, as long as your code reaches us before they enrol. They also sit one class free before paying for anything.

How long do I keep earning?

Every month your friend stays enrolled and pays their fee. There is no cut-off. A Level runs over two years (AS and A2), so a friend who studies both with us keeps paying you for both.

How does the Rs. 5,000 bonus work?

You get Rs. 5,000 for every 5 friends you refer who are active students. Ten friends means Rs. 10,000 in bonuses. There is no limit.

When and how do I get paid?

Earnings are counted at the end of each month and paid between the 5th and 10th of the next month, by bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa or cash at the academy counter. The minimum payout is Rs. 1,000. Smaller balances roll over to the next month.

How does SJ Academy know my friend came from me?

Your referral code. Send your friend the link from the share kit on this page and your code is already in their first WhatsApp message to us. They can also give your code when they register. The code has to reach us before they enrol: it cannot be added later.

What if my friend gets a discount?

You earn on what your friend actually pays. If they get 10% off for paying three months upfront, or a need-based discount, your share is worked out on the lower amount.

What if my friend leaves after a few weeks?

If your friend leaves or gets a full refund within their first 30 days, that referral does not count. If a payment for that friend has already gone out, it is taken from your next earnings.
