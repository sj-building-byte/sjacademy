FAQ

Questions about SAT and IBA prep

Who is this batch for?

Students sitting the SAT on 5 December or the IBA entry test on 20 December, whether you are starting fresh or retaking for a higher score.

What is the fee for SAT or IBA preparation?

Rs. 32,000 for the full course, for either the SAT or the IBA batch. For 1-on-1 coaching, ask PrepDesk on WhatsApp.

When are the classes?

On Saturdays and Sundays, with a morning group and an evening group, so school or college during the week stays untouched. The batch starts on Saturday 31 October.

Who runs the classes, PrepDesk or SJ Academy?

PrepDesk teaches the batch and handles admissions, seats and fees, in partnership with SJ Academy. Every button on this page goes straight to PrepDesk.

Is the SAT batch for the digital SAT?

Yes. The full mocks follow the Bluebook format, the app the digital SAT is taken on, with pacing and Module 2 strategy for the harder questions.

Do I need to register for the SAT separately?

Yes. The SAT is booked with College Board, not through PrepDesk. For the 5 December test, the regular deadline is 20 November and the late deadline is 24 November.

Is there 1-on-1 SAT or IBA coaching?

Yes. 1-on-1 coaching is available for both tests, with sessions at times that suit you. It suits students aiming for 1450+ on the SAT or closing a big score gap.

Is financial support available?

Yes. If the fee is hard to manage, message PrepDesk and they will share the financial support application.

Which universities ask for the SAT or the IBA test?

It depends on the university and the programme. The SJ Academy university admission criteria tool shows what each one asks for.
