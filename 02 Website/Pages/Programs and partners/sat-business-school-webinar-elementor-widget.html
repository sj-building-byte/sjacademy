The problem

Good grades get you to the door. The test decides if it opens.

Your Cambridge CAIE grades qualify you to apply, but top business schools like IBA rank applicants on entrance exam performance. The SAT and university entry tests reward timing, question patterns and composure under a clock: skills no school syllabus teaches.

Most students wait until A2 exams end in June, leaving two rushed weeks to cram for tests that require months of strategy. Planning early gives you choices instead of panic.

habits take time to build

The three mistakes students make

  • Treating test prep like revision: Standardized tests are not about subject knowledge; they are about question traps, pacing and score margins.
  • Misunderstanding SAT exemption at IBA: Assuming any high SAT score guarantees test exemption. In reality, IBA has no fixed minimum SAT score for exemption; only top scorers on that round's SAT merit list qualify, cut-offs fluctuate (such as 1480 for Fall 2026 Round 2), and IBA strongly encourages taking its test.
  • Leaving preparation for the final month: Scrambling between final A Level coursework and entry tests destroys both.

I am in O Levels or AS Level. Is it too early for me?

Not at all. In fact, AS Level is the ideal time to clear your SAT before A2 coursework intensifies. O Level students benefit from learning university subject requirements early so they choose the right A Level combinations.

Can a SAT score exempt me from the IBA entry test?

IBA considers SAT scores for test exemption, but exemption, direct admission, or an interview call is never guaranteed. There is no fixed minimum SAT score for exemption; only the top scorers on the SAT merit list in each round receive an exemption, and thresholds change each cycle (for example, Fall 2026 Round 2 had a cut-off of 1480 total: 640 in Math and 600 in English). IBA strongly encourages all candidates to take its own test. In this session, Talal will explain how the SAT merit list works and what recent cut-offs looked like.
