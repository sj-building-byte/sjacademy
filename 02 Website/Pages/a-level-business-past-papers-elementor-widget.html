How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 1 and Paper 4 are 1 hour 15 minutes. Paper 2 is 1 hour 30 minutes. Paper 3 is 1 hour 45 minutes.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeGo line by line. Give yourself the mark only if the mark scheme would.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksMissing application, a chain that stops early, or evaluation that says "it depends". That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the weak question typePick the same question size from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9609 past papers

Are these A Level Business past papers free?

Yes. Every question paper, mark scheme and insert on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9609 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge AS and A Level Business (9609) past papers from 2020 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, Papers 1 to 4, in all three variants.

Are Business and Business Studies past papers the same?

Yes. Cambridge calls the subject Business, with the syllabus code 9609. Many students and schools still call it Business Studies. The papers on this page are the ones you need for either name.

How do I read a 9609 past paper file name?

Every file follows the Cambridge pattern 9609_s25_qp_12. The letter is the session (s = May/June, w = Oct/Nov), the two digits are the year, qp is the question paper and ms is the mark scheme. The last two digits are the paper number followed by the variant, so 12 means Paper 1, Variant 2.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a session. It gives you three papers to practise from each series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Note where you lost marks for missing application or structure, not only for wrong answers. Do you have A Level Business topical past papers?
