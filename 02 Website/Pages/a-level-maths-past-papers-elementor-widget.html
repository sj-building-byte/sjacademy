How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 1 and Paper 3 are 1 hour 50 minutes. Papers 4, 5 and 6 are 1 hour 15 minutes.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeGo line by line. M marks are for method, A marks are for the accurate answer that follows.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksA missed method mark, the wrong form of answer, or a rounding error carried through. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 9709 past papers

Are these A Level Maths past papers free?

Yes. Every question paper and mark scheme on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 9709 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge AS and A Level Mathematics (9709) past papers from 2020 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series: Paper 1 (Pure 1), Paper 3 (Pure 3), Paper 4 (Mechanics), Paper 5 (Statistics 1) and Paper 6 (Statistics 2), in all three variants.

Is A Level Math the same as A Level Maths?

Yes. Math and Maths are the same Cambridge subject, syllabus code 9709. The papers on this page are the ones you need for either spelling.

How do I read a 9709 past paper file name?

Every file follows the Cambridge pattern 9709_s25_qp_12. The letter is the session (s = May/June, w = Oct/Nov), the two digits are the year, qp is the question paper and ms is the mark scheme. The last two digits are the paper number followed by the variant, so 12 means Paper 1, Variant 2.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a session. It gives you three papers to practise from each series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Note where you lost marks for a missing method step, not only for a wrong final answer. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and our A Level grade thresholds guide explains how raw marks convert to grades.

Do you have Further Maths (9231) past papers?

Not on this page. This page covers A Level Mathematics, syllabus 9709. Further Maths is a separate Cambridge syllabus, 9231, with its own papers. Message us on WhatsApp if you need help finding those.
