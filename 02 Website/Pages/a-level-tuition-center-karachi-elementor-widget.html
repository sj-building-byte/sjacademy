The problem

Why A Level feels harder than it is

Most A Level students work hard. Fewer work on the right thing. Rereading the textbook a fourth time feels productive. It is mostly just familiar.

The jump from O Level

A Level questions ask you to apply, analyse and judge. Knowing the chapter is where the answer starts, not where it ends.

Marks follow the mark scheme

Examiners give marks for specific things. A student who has never seen how a mark scheme works leaves marks behind in every paper.

Feedback arrives too late

If the first properly marked paper you see is your mock, there is very little time left to fix what it shows.

The method

Every class runs on the same method

Different subjects, different teachers, one way of working. It is the reason an A Level coaching center should exist at all.

  1. Learn the syllabus properlyConcepts first, taught by a subject specialist, in the order the syllabus builds them.
  2. Practise on past papersReal Cambridge questions on every topic, so the exam style is never a surprise.
  3. Get marked to the mark schemeYour answers are marked the way an examiner marks them.
  4. Find out where the marks wentFeedback shows which marks were lost and what to write next time.
