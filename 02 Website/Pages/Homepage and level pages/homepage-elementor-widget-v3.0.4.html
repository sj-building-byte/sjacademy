The problem

Knowing the content is not enough

Cambridge gives marks for specific things: the working, the application, the explanation that links back to the question. Students who study hard but write loosely lose those marks and do not see where they went.

01

Right answer, marks missing

You reach the answer, but the mark scheme also wanted the working, the application or the link back to the question.

02

Papers done, never marked

You practise past papers without a mark scheme in hand, so you cannot see which line lost the mark.

03

The question left unasked

The basic question that felt too small to ask in school stays unasked, and the gap grows.

All three are habits, not talent, and habits can be fixed.

How we teach

Same student. Same question. A different mark.

You learn the way Cambridge wants answers written, then practise it on past papers marked against the mark scheme.

the mark scheme has opinions

Sample answer · A Level Business 9609 · Paper 1Analyse two benefits to a hotel of using job enrichment for its employees. [8]
Job enrichment is when workers get more interesting work. This is good because workers will be happy and work harder. The hotel will do better and make more profit. Workers will also not leave.
Job enrichment gives employees more challenging tasks and more responsibility.K If the hotel lets reception staff resolve guest complaints themselves instead of calling a manager,APP they feel trusted, which meets Herzberg's motivators, so they put in more effort.AN Problems get solved faster, so guests are more likely to rebook, raising revenue.AN A second benefit is lower labour turnover.K Hotels rely on trained front-desk and housekeeping staff who know regular guests.APP Enriched jobs make these employees less likely to leave, so the hotel spends less on recruiting and training replacements.AN Keeping experienced staff also keeps service quality consistent, which protects its reputation.AN
Describes. No chain of reasoning.3/8
