The exam

What is on each Biology paper

As set in the 2023 to 2025 papers. You sit Paper 1, Paper 2 and either Paper 3 or Paper 4, never both. Up to 2022 the alternative to practical was Paper 6 (1 hour, 40 marks), the practical test was 1 hour 15 minutes, and Paper 2 had three sections with a choice in Section C. Check the current syllabus on the Cambridge website before your exam.

PaperTimeMarksWhat you do
Paper 1 Multiple Choice1 hour4040 multiple choice questions, one mark each. Answer all of them.
Paper 2 Theory1 hour 45 minutes80Answer all questions. You write on the question paper.
Paper 3 Practical Test1 hour 30 minutes40Experiments in a lab. Answer all questions. The confidential instructions (CI) tell the school how to set up the apparatus.
Paper 4 Alternative to Practical1 hour40Written questions on practical skills, with no experiments. Answer all questions.
How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 1 is 1 hour for 40 questions. Paper 2 is 1 hour 45 minutes. Do the practical paper you will sit, Paper 3 or Paper 4.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeCheck the key words the mark scheme needs and the units on every answer. A right number with the wrong unit can lose the mark.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksA description where the mark scheme wanted an explanation, a missing key word, or a graph with no units on the axes. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 5090 past papers

Are these O Level Biology past papers free?

Yes. Every question paper, mark scheme and confidential instruction file on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 5090 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge O Level Biology (5090) past papers from 2020 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, in every variant we hold.

Do I need Paper 3 or Paper 4?

You sit one of them, not both. Paper 3 is the practical test in a lab and Paper 4 is the alternative to practical, a written paper on the same skills. Ask your school or exam centre which one you are entered for, then practise that one.

Why is there a Paper 6 in older years?

Up to 2022 the Biology alternative to practical was Paper 6. From 2023 it is Paper 4. They test the same practical skills, so the older Paper 6 papers are still good practice.

How do I read a 5090 past paper file name?

Take 5090_w25_qp_41. 5090 is Biology, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 41 is Paper 4, Variant 1. ms is the mark scheme and ci is the confidential instructions.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and the O Level grade thresholds show how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.
