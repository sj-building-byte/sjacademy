How to use them

Past papers only work with marking

Doing ten papers without marking them is ten rehearsals of the same mistakes. One paper, marked properly, teaches more.

  1. Sit it under exam timingPaper 1 is 1 hour 15 minutes for 35 questions, about 2 minutes each. Paper 2 is 1 hour 45 minutes.
  2. Mark it against the mark schemeOn Paper 2, check the format of each statement and the labels, not only the final figure. Own figure marks reward correct method after an earlier slip.
  3. Find the pattern in the lost marksA missing heading or date, debit and credit the wrong way round, or a comment with no figures from the question. That is what to fix next.
  4. Redo the same question typePick the same question from another variant and try again.

FAQ

Questions about 7707 past papers

Are these O Level Accounting past papers free?

Yes. Every question paper and mark scheme on this page is free to open and download. You do not need to sign up.

Which years of 7707 past papers are on this page?

This page has Cambridge O Level Accounting (7707) past papers from 2020 to 2025, for the May/June and Oct/Nov series, Paper 1 and Paper 2, in every variant we hold.

Is Accounting the same as Principles of Accounts?

Cambridge O Level Accounting (7707) replaced Principles of Accounts (7110) from 2020. Many students still call it Accounts. This page has the 7707 papers.

How do I read a 7707 past paper file name?

Take 7707_w25_qp_22. 7707 is Accounting, w25 is October/November 2025 (s is May/June), qp is the question paper and 22 is Paper 2, Variant 2. ms is the mark scheme.

Which variant should I practise?

Cambridge sets different variants of the same paper for different time zones. They test the same syllabus to the same standard, so practise every variant from a series.

How should I use the mark schemes?

Attempt the paper under timed conditions first, then mark it yourself against the mark scheme. Our guide on how to use a Cambridge mark scheme walks through the method, and the O Level grade thresholds show how raw marks turn into grades.

Can I see the paper and the mark scheme side by side?

Yes. Every paper on this page has a "Side by side" link that opens it in our past paper viewer, with the question paper on the left and the mark scheme on the right. The mark scheme stays covered until you have written your answer.
