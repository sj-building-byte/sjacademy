Private candidate questions

Can I do O Levels or A Levels privately in Pakistan?

Yes. Thousands of students in Pakistan sit Cambridge O Level, IGCSE, AS and A Level exams as private candidates every year through the British Council. You sit the same papers as school candidates and receive the same Cambridge certificate.

How do I register as a private candidate for CAIE exams?

Private candidates register on the British Council's online registration system, choose their subjects, upload their details and pay the Cambridge fees by the deadline. With SJ Academy, you send us a WhatsApp message and we do the registration and payment for you, then send you the confirmation.

What are the British Council fees for private candidates?

Cambridge fees are charged per subject and shown in the British Council registration system. They change with the pound to rupee exchange rate, so we send you the exact figure for your subjects before you pay. Our service fee is a flat Rs. 3,000 per student on top of that.

When is the registration deadline?

Each exam series has a normal deadline and a later one that costs an extra late fee. The British Council publishes the dates for each series. Message us with your series and we will tell you the current deadline and how much time you have.

What documents do I need?

A valid NADRA CNIC, or a B-form or Smart Card if you are under 18 (a passport if you are not Pakistani), a recent photo, and any earlier Cambridge result statements. Bring the same original ID to every exam: you cannot sit a paper without it.

How do I carry my AS grades forward to A Level?

When you register for A2, your previous centre number, candidate number and the series you sat AS in are entered with your entry, so Cambridge can combine both into one A Level grade. We take these from your AS result statement and enter them for you.

Do I need to study at SJ Academy to use this?

No. The registration service is open to every private candidate, wherever you study and wherever you live in Pakistan. Everything happens over WhatsApp, so you never need to visit us.

Is it safe to pay my exam fees through SJ Academy?

You see the exact Cambridge fee from the registration system before you transfer, and after we pay we send you the British Council payment slip and your registration confirmation, so you can check every step yourself.

What if I want to withdraw after registering?

The British Council refunds withdrawals made before its deadline, minus an administration fee of Rs. 1,500 per subject. Late fees are not refunded. Our Rs. 3,000 service fee is non-refundable once you are registered.

How do private candidates do science practicals?

The practical paper is part of your exam whether you study at a school or not, so you need lab time before it. SJ Academy runs A Level lab practicals for Physics, Chemistry and Biology in Karachi, built around private candidates.
