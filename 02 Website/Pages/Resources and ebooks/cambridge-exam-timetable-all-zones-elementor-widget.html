                FAQ

                Questions about zones

                Which Cambridge zone am I in?

                Cambridge puts every country into one of six administrative zones. Type your country into the zone finder at the top of this page, which uses Cambridge’s own zone list, then confirm with your exams officer. A few countries span two zones, including the United States, Canada, Indonesia and Greenland, so the city of your centre decides there.

                Why does each zone have a different timetable?

                Cambridge runs one timetable per zone to keep its exams secure and to manage timetabling. The same subject can fall on a different day or session in another zone, and the question paper variant changes too, which is why a code such as 0580/22 in one zone becomes 0580/21 or 0580/23 in another.

                When are the May/June 2027 Cambridge exams?

                Most papers run from Monday 26 April to Friday 11 June 2027. Zone 6 runs one day longer, to Saturday 12 June, and Zones 5 and 6 both include Saturday papers. Some practicals and speaking tests sit outside the main timetable, on fixed dates or in windows between January and early June.

                Is the UK timetable the same as Zone 3?

                For June 2027, yes: every date and session matches. Cambridge still publishes a separate UK version for centres in the United Kingdom, so UK candidates should use the UK tab.

                What do AM, PM and EV mean?

                They are Cambridge’s three exam sessions: morning, afternoon and evening. Your centre sets the start time under Cambridge’s key time rules, so ask your exams officer for the exact time.

                Where are the official Cambridge timetables?

                Every tab links to its official PDF, and Cambridge lists them all on its timetables page. Exams officers can also download the dates through Cambridge Direct.

                Will the timetables change?

                Sometimes. Cambridge can reissue a timetable with a new version number. Every timetable on this page is Version 1, November 2026, and we update it when a new version appears.

                Can private candidates use these timetables?

                Yes. Private candidates sit the same papers on the same dates as everyone else in their zone. Your Statement of Entry from your centre confirms your papers and venue.
