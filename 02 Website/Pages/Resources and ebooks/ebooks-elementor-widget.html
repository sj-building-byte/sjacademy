FAQ

Questions about the library

Are SJ Academy ebooks and notes free?

Most are. Free items say Free on the card; some ask for your details in a short form first, others download straight away. Paid items show their price on the card before you click anything.

What is the difference between ebooks, notes and other resources?

Ebooks are complete books: a full topic guide or a full set of solved past papers. Notes are chapter-by-chapter summaries for revision. Other resources are the quick tools: formula sheets, worksheets and answer planners.

How do I pay for paid ebooks or notes?

Tap the button on the card, read what is inside, then tap Buy now and you go straight to checkout on sjacademy.pk. Pay once, in Pakistani rupees, with a credit or debit card, SadaPay, JazzCash, Easypaisa and more, through SmartPay, the secure payment gateway on our site.

When do I get the PDF after paying?

Straight away. The download link shows on the order confirmation page and is also sent to the email address you enter at checkout.

Can I print them?

Yes. Print them, annotate them, highlight them until the pages are more yellow than white. They are for your own study, so please send friends to this page instead of forwarding the file.

Are these official Cambridge materials?

No. SJ Academy is an independent tuition centre in Karachi and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge. Our teachers write these for the Cambridge O Level and A Level syllabuses they teach.

Can you make notes for my subject?

Tell us on WhatsApp which subject and topic you want. Requests decide what we write next.

What if a paid download does not work?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and we will sort it out. Refunds follow our refund policy.
