FAQ

Questions about CAIE dates

When do the CAIE May/June 2027 exams start in Pakistan?

Written papers in Zone 4, which includes Pakistan, run from Monday 26 April to Friday 11 June 2027. A few practicals happen earlier, from 31 March. Your own paper dates are on the Zone 4 timetable.

What is the registration deadline for May/June 2027?

Cambridge’s final entries deadline falls in February 2027, and the British Council sets its own deadlines for private candidates. Neither has been announced yet, and this page adds them as soon as they are. School candidates should follow their school’s earlier date.

Do Cambridge exams stop for Eid ul Adha?

Not completely. Eid ul Adha is expected around 16 to 18 May 2027, subject to moon sighting. Cambridge has no papers on Monday 17 May, but ten papers are timetabled on Tuesday 18 May.

When does Ramadan fall before the 2027 exams?

Ramadan is expected from about 8 February to 8 March 2027, subject to moon sighting, with Eid ul Fitr holidays around 9 to 11 March. It ends about seven weeks before the first paper.

When are the Oct/Nov 2026 results released?

AS and A Level results come out on Thursday 7 January 2027, and O Level and IGCSE results on Thursday 14 January 2027, both at 11:00 am Pakistan time.

Is this the official Cambridge calendar?

No. SJ Academy compiles it from Cambridge International, British Council Pakistan and government holiday notices. Holidays that depend on the moon are estimates, so confirm with your school or the British Council before you rely on one.
