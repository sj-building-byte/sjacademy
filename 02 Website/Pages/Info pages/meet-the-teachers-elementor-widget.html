FAQ

Questions about our teachers

Who teaches at SJ Academy?

SJ Academy's teachers are subject specialists for Cambridge O Level and A Level. They include Sir Salman Junaid for Maths and Physics, Sir Kashaan Ali for Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies, Sir Riza Walji for Computer Science, Sir Umair for Biology, Sir Sunyyan Junaid for Business and Sir Khawar Shuaib for Economics. Every teacher is listed on this page with their subjects and experience.

Who teaches A Level Maths at SJ Academy?

Sir Salman Junaid, SJ Academy's co-founder. He is an NED University engineer and ex-KGS faculty with 12+ years of teaching, and teaches 9709 Pure 1, Pure 3, Mechanics, Statistics 1 and Statistics 2. See A Level Maths tuition.

Can my child meet the teacher before we pay?

Yes, for O Level and A Level classes. The first demo class is free: message us the subject and level on WhatsApp and we will book it. Lab practicals have no demo class; students join them directly.

Do the same teachers teach the online classes?

Yes. Classes run in class at DHA Phase 7 and live online, taught by the teachers on this page. The one split is O Level Physics: Sir Basharat teaches it in class and Sir Salman Junaid teaches it online.

Who teaches the A Level lab practicals?

Sir Salman Junaid teaches Physics practicals, Sir Abdullah Zaman teaches Chemistry practicals and Sir Umair teaches Biology practicals, with real apparatus in SJ Academy's lab. Practical students join directly; there is no demo class. See A Level lab practicals.

How will I know if my child is falling behind?

If your child starts falling behind, the teacher will tell you. You can also ask for an update at any time on WhatsApp.

Do your teachers offer one-on-one or home tuition?

No. SJ Academy does not offer one-on-one or home tuition. Every teacher teaches small-batch classes, at the academy and live online.

SJ Academy is an independent tuition centre and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge.
