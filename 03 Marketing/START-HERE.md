## Brand rules for marketing
- Brand system v4: read `../01 Brand/SJ-ACADEMY-BRAND-BOOK.md`, then `SJ-ACADEMY-SOCIAL-ADS-PLAYBOOK.md` for posts and static ads, `SJ-ACADEMY-VIDEO-MOTION-PLAYBOOK.md` and `SJ-ACADEMY-SOUND-PLAYBOOK.md` for any video.
- Every ad set uses at least three formats. Never repeat the last format.
- No trending songs from Instagram's library on the business account or in ads. Log every licensed track in `../01 Brand/Sound/licence-log.csv`.
- `Ads/Video ads (sj-ads)/ads/frame.md` (the Broadside preset) is retired. New HyperFrames projects take their tokens from the Video playbook, section 11.

## Rules
- Target A Level students in Karachi and their parents. Students are taking subjects for the first time, so do not assume exam experience.
- Logo at the top of every ad design. Plain-text captions.
- Lead lists and form exports never go in this folder. They live in `SJ Academy - Private`.

Finished videos (ads, brand film, reels, teacher videos, trial sessions, testimonials) are in `../✅ Finished/Videos/`. All raw footage, photos and snippets are in one folder: `../SJ Academy Media/Footage & assets/`.
