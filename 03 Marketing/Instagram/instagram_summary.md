# SJ Academy Instagram Performance Summary (@sjacademypk)

This document summarizes the historical performance, key statistics, and strategic content ideas for the SJ Academy Instagram account (18.5K Followers). It is designed to be fed into an AI to generate a 3-day content calendar and plan content for the upcoming month.

## 1. What Has Worked Well (History & Stats)

Based on lifetime performance data, the account excels at Top-of-Funnel (awareness) and Middle-of-Funnel (value creation) content. The audience (O/A Level students) highly engages with relatable humor and highly actionable study hacks. 

Here are the top-performing content archetypes and their stats:

*   **The Exam Panic Meme (Top-of-Funnel)**
    *   *Format:* Reels
    *   *Examples:* "35 days left for CAIE", countdowns to results day, shared CAIE trauma.
    *   *Stats:* **80k - 100k+ Reach | 10k - 16k Shares | 100 - 400 Saves**
    *   *Why it works:* Hits an emotional nerve, highly relatable, and highly shareable in WhatsApp groups.
*   **The High-Value Hack / "Cheat Codes" (Middle-of-Funnel)**
    *   *Format:* Carousels / Screen-recording Reels
    *   *Examples:* "Stop solving math like it's 1999 / Calculator Hacks", "15 cheat codes for X subject".
    *   *Stats:* **80k+ Reach | 7k+ Shares | 5,000+ Saves**
    *   *Why it works:* Extreme utility. Students hoard this content for revision, building immense authority and trust.
*   **The "Negative Hook" Warning (Middle-of-Funnel)**
    *   *Format:* Carousels
    *   *Examples:* "5 A Level subjects students regret taking", "3 Mistakes CAIE Examiners Penalize".
    *   *Stats:* **45k - 70k+ Reach | 2k - 4k Shares | 400 - 900+ Saves**
    *   *Why it works:* Fear-based/warning hooks drastically outperform positive framing.

**What to Avoid (Low Performers):**
*   **Direct Promos / Traditional Flyers:** Standalone promotional flyers ("Join Sir Salman's classes") get less than 3,000 reach and near-zero engagement.
*   **Generic Questions:** Posts like "Solve this in your head" lack emotional triggers and perform poorly.

## 2. Strategic Ideas & Frameworks to Work On

To improve conversion and grid aesthetic, the following strategies should be implemented:

1.  **"Value-First Selling" (Integrated Promos):** Stop posting traditional flyers. Instead, integrate promotions into high-value carousels. For example, provide 4 slides of pure value (e.g., "Examiner's Secret"), and use the 5th slide as the pitch ("Join our May/June batch to avoid these mistakes").
2.  **Aggressive Lead Capture via Auto-DMs:** Expand on ManyChat automation. Prompt students to comment a keyword (e.g., "Comment 'CALC' for our free PDF") to deliver resources and capture leads.
3.  **Scale the Winners:** 
    *   More "Cheat Code" carousels (command word breakdowns, examiner report summaries).
    *   More relatable POV memes regarding CAIE exam stress.
4.  **Profile Optimization:**
    *   Update bio to a results-driven hook (e.g., "Helping CAIE students turn C's into A*s").
    *   Ensure a Linktree/landing page is always present for lead magnets.
    *   Standardize Story Highlights (Results, Free Notes, Campus Tour, Faculty, Demos).

## 3. Proposed 3-Day Content Calendar Framework

Based on the data above, here is a suggested 3-day cycle that balances growth (ToFu) and authority/sales (MoFu/BoFu):

*   **Day 1: Growth & Shareability (The Exam Panic Meme)**
    *   *Format:* POV Reel with trending audio.
    *   *Concept:* A relatable skit about the panic of seeing an unfamiliar question on a past paper. 
    *   *Goal:* High shares and broad reach.
*   **Day 2: Authority & Saves (The High-Value Hack)**
    *   *Format:* 4-5 Slide Carousel.
    *   *Concept:* "3 A-Level Subject Combinations top universities reject" or a specific calculator hack.
    *   *Goal:* High saves and building trust.
*   **Day 3: Value-First Selling (Integrated Promo)**
    *   *Format:* Carousel or Screen-recording Reel.
    *   *Concept:* "The 3 words killing your Economics grade (Examiner Report)." Slide 1-4 is value. Slide 5 is a CTA for academy enrollment or a free demo.
    *   *Goal:* Lead generation and class sign-ups without killing engagement.
