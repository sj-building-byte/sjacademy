# SJ Academy Instagram Audit & Growth Strategy (@sjacademypk)

## 1. Executive Scorecard
**Overall Health Score:** 7.5 / 10

**Primary Takeaway & Bottleneck:** 
The account is doing a phenomenal job at Top-of-Funnel (ToFu) awareness and Middle-of-Funnel (MoFu) value creation. You have successfully cracked the code on what makes Pakistani O/A Level students share and save content (relatable exam panic and high-value "cheat codes"). 

**The Bottleneck:** There is a severe disconnect at the Bottom-of-Funnel (BoFu). When the account transitions from educational/relatable content to promotional content (e.g., "Join Sir Salman's classes"), engagement drops by over 95%. The grid aesthetic suffers when institutional, text-heavy flyers interrupt the native, highly optimized carousels. The academy needs to transition to "Value-First Selling"—embedding the pitch natively within educational hooks rather than posting standalone flyers.

## 4. Stop / Start / Scale Framework

### 🛑 STOP
*   **Posting Traditional Flyers:** Stop posting single-image graphics that look like newspaper ads. They destroy grid aesthetic and get punished by the algorithm.
*   **Vague CTAs:** Stop ending posts without telling the student *exactly* what to do. 

### 🟢 START
*   **Value-Integrated Selling:** If you want to promote Sir Salman's Physics class, do not post a flyer. Post a carousel titled *"3 Mistakes CAIE Examiners Penalize in Physics P2"*. Slide 1-4 is pure value. Slide 5 is: *"Want to avoid these? Join Sir Salman's May/June batch. Link in bio."*
*   **ManyChat/Auto-DM Automation:** Use prompts like *"Comment 'CALC' to get our free calculator hack PDF"* (you are already doing this well, scale it aggressively for lead capture).

### 🚀 SCALE
*   **"Cheat Code" Carousels:** The data proves your audience hoards this content. Scale "15 cheat codes for X subject", "Command word breakdowns", and "Examiner Report Summaries".
*   **Relatable Skits/Memes:** Lean into the shared trauma of CAIE exams. The "countdown to results day" and "paper leak" content drives your highest viral loops.
