TITLE
Price elasticity of demand explained ⛽🍔 | A Level Business 9609

DESCRIPTION
⛽ Why can petrol go up in price and people still buy it, but if a burger place does the same, everyone leaves? 🍔 That's price elasticity of demand.

📌 In under a minute:
• What PED means, and the formula: % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price
• 🍔 Burgers: price +10%, quantity −30%, PED = −3. Elastic.
• ⛽ Petrol: price +10%, quantity −2%, PED = −0.2. Inelastic.
• 🏷️ Why burger places fight with deals and discounts, and why petrol prices can rise without sales falling much
• 🤔 The one question to ask before you touch your prices
