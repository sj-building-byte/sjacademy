### Five-line brief (brand book, section 9)

1. **Who and when:** an O or A Level student in Karachi (and their parent) scrolling Instagram, choosing where to study for May/June 2027.
2. **The one thing:** SJ Academy has real, named teachers for your subjects, in class at DHA Phase 7 or live online, and the first class is free.
3. **The idea:** SJ Academy presented the way a premium tech brand launches a product. Big clean type, footage inside letters, windows that fly between shots, spec lines with a dry punchline. The academy year is "the 2027 edition", and it is "available in two sizes".
4. **The proof:** real footage of real classes, labs and teachers; facts from `FACTS.md` only.
5. **Format and level:** brand film, 9:16, about 43s, level 3 (Loud), building from Teach to Hype.

### Specs

- 1080x1920, 30fps, 42.74s: 16 bars of music at 96.65 BPM (bar 2.483s, beat 0.621s), then a 3.0s end card.
- Text inside x 100 to 980, y 240 to 1680 (Instagram side crop and grid crop).
- No speech. Every word is on screen, so it works on mute.

### The motion language (new for this film, approved)

| Move | What it does | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Type window | Footage plays inside giant letters or numbers, then the camera pushes through a letter into the shot | Opener; subject codes |
| Window fly | A shot shrinks into a rounded window, travels, and lands inside a screen in the next shot | In class becomes online |
| Bento grid | Several clips tile the frame; cells resize on the beat | Online teachers; subjects |
| Word behind | A giant name sits behind the teacher, the teacher cut out in front of it | Teacher reveals |
| Spec swap | A label holds while the item under it swaps in place on the beat | "Also included" |

Brand signatures stay: the yellow highlighter swipe (once per act, on the key word), the red pen circle (on the real flask and the real flame), Poppins only (600 for lines, 800 for the code numbers), ink and canvas grounds, the on-brown logo on footage. No device mockups, no glows, no glass, no spins.
