# Price cut, more money? (short version, with PED)

Say it like you're talking a student through it: relaxed but quick. Lean on "minus two" and the last line.

---

Does cutting your price always make you more money?

Say a burger drops from a thousand to eight hundred. That's minus twenty percent, and sales go up forty percent.

That's a PED of minus two, so demand is elastic and revenue goes up.

But if each burger costs five hundred to make, you're now earning three hundred a burger instead of five hundred.

So what you actually earn goes from fifty thousand down to forty-two.

Elastic means more revenue, not more profit.

---

## The numbers (checked)
- PED = +40% ÷ −20% = −2. Elastic, so the price cut raises revenue: Rs 100,000 → Rs 112,000.
- Earned per burger (contribution) = price − cost to make: Rs 500, then Rs 300.
- Total: 100 × 500 = Rs 50,000, then 140 × 300 = Rs 42,000. Fixed costs don't change, so profit falls by Rs 8,000.
- About 75 words, roughly 25 seconds at a natural pace.
