# Voice clone script (about 1 minute)

Read it like you're explaining something to a student you like. Relaxed, warm, a small smile. Don't perform it. Pauses are fine.

---

You know that feeling when a topic finally clicks? That's what I love about teaching.

Most students think economics is just graphs and definitions. It isn't. It's mangoes in June, petrol prices in Karachi, and why your favourite shoes go on sale.

So here's the thing. When something is rare, people pay more. When there's too much of it, sellers drop the price. Simple, right?

Physics works the same way. Drop a pen. It falls. Every single time. Why? Because the whole Earth is quietly pulling on it.

And honestly, that's the fun part. Once you see how it works, you can't unsee it.

So stay curious, ask the weird questions, and never just memorise an answer. Understand it.

---

## Why it's written this way
- The first two lines are calm and warm, ideal for the 10 seconds Chatterbox actually uses.
- It mixes questions, statements, a little excitement and a calm close, so the voice has its natural ups and downs.
- It covers most English sounds (th, sh, ch, j, v, z, r, w) and the words our videos use: mangoes, price, Earth, pull, supply.
- No numbers or lists. Those make people read in a flat "presenter" voice.

## How to record
1. Quietest room, fan and AC off, phone a hand's width from your mouth (Voice Memos).
2. Stay silent for 2 seconds, then read it once. Read it a second time if the first felt stiff.
3. Save both takes, unedited, in this folder.
