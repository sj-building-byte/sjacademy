# ElevenLabs voice clone script (about 5 minutes)

Read it the way you'd teach a student you like: relaxed, warm, with a small smile. Let the questions sound like questions and the exciting bits sound excited. Mistakes are fine; pause, then say the whole sentence again. Leave out the headings in brackets.

---

[Warm opening]

Hi, I'm Sunyyan from SJ Academy. If you're watching this, you're probably a student who wants things to make sense, not just to memorise them. Good. That's exactly what we're going to do.

I've been teaching for a while now, and here's what I've learned. Nobody is bad at a subject. They've just been taught it in a way that doesn't click. So let's fix that.

[Curious and playful: physics]

Let's start with something simple. Drop a pen. It falls. Every single time. But have you ever stopped and asked why? Why down? Why not sideways? Why not up?

Here's the secret. Everything with mass pulls on everything else. You pull on your phone, and your phone pulls on you. It's a tiny, tiny pull, so you never notice it. But the Earth is enormous, so its pull wins. That's gravity. And it's the reason your pen, your coffee and your homework all end up on the floor.

[Clear and steady: maths]

Now, maths. A lot of students tell me they hate maths. I don't think that's true. I think they hate feeling lost.

Take a right-angled triangle with sides three and four. How long is the third side? You could square both numbers, add them and find the square root. Or you could just know that three, four and five belong together. Double them, and you get six, eight and ten. Multiply by five, and you get fifteen, twenty and twenty-five. Once you spot the pattern, the question answers itself.

That's what good maths feels like. Not a wall of working. A shortcut you earned by understanding.

[Lively: economics]

Economics is my favourite, because it's everywhere. Why are mangoes so cheap in June? Because suddenly every farmer has mangoes to sell. There are too many of them at the old price, so sellers cut the price until people buy them. That's supply and demand, and you've watched it happen your whole life without knowing its name.

And it's not just mangoes. Petrol prices in Karachi, concert tickets, the price of the shoes you've been eyeing for months. Once you see supply and demand, you can't unsee it.

[Thoughtful: business]

Business is really about people. Why does one shop on the corner stay full while the one next to it stays empty? Usually it's not the price. It's how they make you feel. A good business solves a real problem, and treats people well while doing it. Simple to say. Very hard to do.

[Calm and reassuring: exams]

Now, let's talk about exams, because I know that's what's really on your mind.

Exams are not designed to trick you. They're designed to check whether you understand the idea, and whether you can explain it clearly. So when you practise, don't just check if your answer is right. Ask yourself, could I explain this to a friend? If you can, you're ready. If you can't, that's not a failure. It's just the next thing to learn.

And please, sleep the night before. A tired brain forgets things a rested brain knows perfectly well.

[Energetic: motivation]

Here's what I want you to remember. You don't need to be a genius. You need to be curious, and you need to keep going. Ask the weird questions. Get things wrong. Try again. Every topic that feels impossible today will feel obvious in a few weeks. I've seen it happen hundreds of times.

[Light humour]

And if you're watching this at two in the morning before your paper, I see you. Close the video, open your notes, and do one past paper question. Just one. Then go to sleep. Seriously.

[Warm close]

So that's it from me for now. Stay curious, keep asking why, and never settle for memorising when you could understand. I'll see you in the next one.

## Before you record (ElevenLabs)
- **5 minutes suits Instant Voice Cloning**, which needs only a minute or two of clean audio. For their **Professional Voice Clone**, ElevenLabs asks for much more: at least 30 minutes, ideally a couple of hours. If 5 minutes isn't enough, read this script several times in different moods, or add other scripts.
- Use the same room, the same mic and the same distance for every recording. The clone learns your setup as well as your voice.
- Quietest room, fan and AC off, no music, no echo. Phone or mic about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- Don't edit, enhance or add noise reduction. Upload the raw recording.
- Keep your normal accent and pace. The clone will copy whatever you give it, including a stiff "reading" voice, so talk rather than read.
- Only clone your own voice. ElevenLabs will ask you to confirm the voice is yours.
