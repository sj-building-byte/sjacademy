## Accuracy check
- Desmos: the line y = x − 1 and the curve y = x² − 4x + 3. Setting them equal: x² − 5x + 4 = 0, so (x − 1)(x − 4) = 0, giving x = 1, y = 0 and x = 4, y = 3. Checked twice: 1 − 4 + 3 = 0 = 1 − 1, and 16 − 16 + 3 = 3 = 4 − 1. The on-screen question shows both equations in full.
- PhET Ohm's Law sim (checked 21 Oct 2026): at 4.5 V, 500 Ω gives 9.0 mA; 1000 Ω gives 4.5 mA. I = V/R, so doubling R at constant V halves I.
- Anki: free spaced-repetition flashcards; AnkiWeb works in any browser. "Shows each card right before you'd forget it" is the plain-English version of its scheduling. "Forty definitions" is an example, not a syllabus count.
- Cambridge command words: the 9708 Economics syllabus for 2026 to 2028 (page 37) defines Evaluate as "judge or calculate the quality, importance, amount, or value of something". "Judge the value of something" is a fair short version; the screen shows the full wording.
- SJ grade strategy tool: after an AS result, it compares carrying forward to A2, an Oct/Nov AS retake and a May/June composite, with the score each path needs.
- SJ subject combinations tool: a four-step questionnaire that suggests A Level subject combinations for degree and career clusters.
- SJ past papers: free, no sign-up, question papers and mark schemes (the 9709 page lists 2020 to 2025, 360 files; checked 21 Oct 2026). Years are left out of the voiceover because the hub and subject pages give different ranges.
