# Lab practical trailer: voiceover script

Read as a bored court clerk, never as a trailer narrator. The joke dies if you sound like you are enjoying it.
Record close to a lapel mic in a quiet room. Three takes of each line, with a two-second gap between lines. Save the recording in this folder.

| Time | Line | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Robotic arm. | Flat, like a weather report. |
| 0:01 | Or mechanical hand. | Same, slightly more tired. |
| 0:05 | Every exam series, the reviews come out. | Slightly slower. Small lift on "reviews". Must fit in 1.8 seconds. |
| 0:07 | Some did not read the burette to the expected level of accuracy. | Even pace. Lean on "expected". Must fit in 2.9 seconds. |
| 0:11 | An increase in mass. | Mild wonder on "increase". |
| 0:12 | After heating. | Pause first. Let it land. |
| 0:14 | Or were too thick. | Quiet disappointment on "thick". |
| 0:15 | Turns limewater milky. | Three clean takes at normal pace. I speed them up into the chant. |
| 0:19 | All on your phone. | Dry. |
| 0:20 | At one point five ex. | Beat first. |
| 0:22 | Only limited experience. | Slow, close to the mic, nearly a whisper. |
| 0:24 | It is essential for candidates to have experienced practical work. | Slowest line. Pause after "essential". Land "practical work" and stop. Must fit in 2.9 seconds. |
| 0:39 | Some candidates. | Two words, dry, a small shrug in the voice. |
| 0:43 | Message us before your practical. | Plain and warm. The only line that sounds like a person. |
