## Executive Summary

SJ Academy earns a **49/100 (Grade D)** — below average for a business of its age and market position, but with a clear, fixable path to 70+ within one quarter. The score reflects a genuine product quality gap between what SJ Academy delivers in the classroom and what its website communicates to the outside world.

The academy's single biggest strength is its **Google Maps presence**: a #1 Local Pack ranking for "O level tuition center Karachi" backed by 201 reviews at a perfect 5.0 rating. This is a hard-won asset most competitors will take years to replicate. The blog content strategy is also producing real SEO wins — ranking for "best degrees in Pakistan," "top A level colleges in Karachi," and earning at least one Google Featured Snippet. These are not small achievements.

The biggest gap is a **conversion funnel that systematically undermines trust at every step**. Pricing is hidden until a visitor is inside the registration form — the worst possible moment. The primary CTA asks cold traffic (visitors who found the site via Google) to immediately enroll, skipping the low-friction "free demo class" that exists on the site but is buried. And 7 of 10 indexed pages share the exact same meta description, rendering them invisible in competitive search results.

The **three actions that would move the needle most** are: (1) restructure the conversion funnel so the "Free Demo Class" is the primary CTA for cold traffic — this alone could triple lead volume; (2) fix all meta descriptions and add subject-specific landing pages — this would unlock 30–50% more organic traffic within 60 days; and (3) build a structured social proof section with real student results and photos — this addresses the single largest trust gap preventing parents from committing.

Implementing all recommendations in this report has a realistic upside of **PKR 650,000–1,500,000/month in additional enrollment revenue** (based on estimated current traffic, conversion improvement projections, and PKR 25,000/subject/session pricing), with the quick wins alone recoverable within 2–3 weeks.

## Strategic Recommendations (This Month)

1. **Restructure the full conversion funnel: Demo → Inquiry → Registration**

   Current funnel forces a bottom-of-funnel commitment from top-of-funnel cold traffic. The restructured funnel:
   - **Step 1 (Cold Google traffic):** "Book Free Demo Class" — 4-field form: Name, WhatsApp, Level (O/A/IELTS), Subject. No pricing shown.
   - **Step 2 (After demo attended):** WhatsApp follow-up with fee context + link to Registration Form
   - **Step 3 (Warm, post-demo):** Registration Form — now contextualised, pricing pre-framed

   Implementation: 2–3 weeks. Estimated improvement: conversion rate from ~3% to 8–12% of site visitors.

2. **Build a "Student Results Wall" page at `/results/`**

   Create a dedicated page with:
   - Aggregate A*/A rate for the last 3 exam sessions (e.g., "412 A*/A grades since 2022")
