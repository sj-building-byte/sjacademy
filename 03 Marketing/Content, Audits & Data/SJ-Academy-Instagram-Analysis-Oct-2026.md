# SJ Academy Instagram Analysis: Teachwink Benchmark, Our Gap, and Stories Archive Findings

Prepared 21 October 2026. Data pulled 20 October 2026.
Accounts covered: @teachwink (competitor benchmark) and @sjacademypk (ours).

Companion sheet: [SJ Academy – Stories Poll & Interaction Archive](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1zFabTPPcMWWsUeQZqGugppXNujaJFZ8uCxhIrZAdJ1E/edit) (Google Drive, sjacademypk@gmail.com).

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Teachwink is 3x our size (62.1K vs 20.2K followers) but we are stronger per follower.** Our recent carousels earn about 1.2% of followers in likes at the median (247 likes). Theirs earn about 0.74% (462 likes). Content quality is not our problem. Reach and topic range are.
2. **Reel reach is roughly level.** We have 34 reels above 100K views and 4 above 1M. They have 21 above 100K and 2 above 1M. Our total reel views (about 27.5M across 297 reels) are higher than theirs (about 14.5M across 250 reels).
3. **Our biggest reels are narrow promos; theirs are broad "what's next" content.** Our top reels are A-Level Practicals workshop promos. Their top reels cover what every O Level student must know, internships, scholarships, careers, subject combinations, exam-hall memes and student-poll stats. That content reaches the whole CAIE audience, including parents.
4. **Their carousel ceiling is about 4x ours.** Their best organic carousels reach 8K to 13K likes ("exact number of past papers", "most repeated topics"). Our best is 3.2K, and our top posts are news, leak commentary and rants.
5. **We almost never use Stories polls.** The archive holds 11 polls, all from 2019 to 2021, none since October 2021. Q&A is our strongest Stories interaction, with 2026 prompts averaging about 20 replies each. Results-day sliders drew 600 and 554 votes.
6. **Instagram does not show shares publicly.** Nothing in this analysis ranks by shares. Likes, comments and reel views are used as proxies.

## 2. Scope, method and limits

### What was collected

| Source | Collected | Of total |
|---|---|---|
| Teachwink grid posts | 647 | 854 posts |
| Teachwink reels (Reels tab) | 250 | n/a |
| SJ Academy grid posts | 658 | 662 posts |
| SJ Academy reels (Reels tab) | 297 | n/a |
| SJ Academy Stories archive | 2,848 stories | 854 archive days, Jan 2019 to Oct 2026 |

### How

- Grid likes and comments were read from each tile's hover overlay while scrolling the profile. Reel views were read from the Reels tab.
- Top reels were identified visually from thumbnails, and the top reel was opened to check likes, comments and reposts.
- Stories data was read from the same data Instagram's web app loads for the account owner on the Archive page, using the logged-in session. This was read-only.

### Limits to keep in mind

- **Shares are not public.** The paper-plane count is hidden. On the 3.8M-view Teachwink reel, only "1" repost is shown.
- **The grid has no dates.** Recency is inferred from position (index 0 is newest).
- **Paid versus organic can't be confirmed.** Very high like counts on old promo videos with "sign up" captions and almost no comments were treated as likely boosted ads.
- **The Teachwink grid stopped loading at 647 of 854 posts.** The Reels tab holds more reels than the grid shows, so some reels are not on their main grid.
- **Theme analysis is keyword-based on truncated captions (first 90 characters).** Read it as directional. Small groups (for example, 10 parent/POV posts) are flagged where used.

## 6. Where our gap lies

1. **Topic range.** Their biggest content is "what comes after exams": internships, scholarships, degrees, careers, A Level vs FSC, subject combos, forces entry, and parent POV. These are saved and forwarded to friends and parents. Our top posts stay inside the exam and the news cycle.
2. **Format ceiling.** Their top carousels are numbers and lists that people save ("exact number of papers", "most repeated topics"). Ours are reactions to events. Result: about 4x lower peak engagement.
3. **Calls to action.** They run comment-keyword mechanics ("comment internship", E-book giveaway) on their highest-reach reels, converting views into leads and comments. We have the IBCC keyword plan but not yet at that scale.
4. **Follower conversion.** Reel views are comparable, but they have 3x the followers. Our top reels are narrow promos that attract buyers, not followers. Reasons beyond this (account age, paid reach) cannot be seen from outside.
5. **Student-data hooks.** Their 467K reel opened with a count from their own students. We have 100 to 200 paying students and a Stories audience to poll.
6. **Parents.** Parents decide on fees and are our secondary audience. Their parent/POV posts have a median of 641 likes versus a baseline of 128 (small sample of 10). We have little parent-facing content.

Where we are ahead: per-follower engagement, comment rates on carousels, reel volume, and a deep archive of Q&A, quiz and slider interactions that we are not yet turning into content.

## 8. Strategy: how to use this

### Priority 1: broaden into "after CAIE" content (closes the topic gap)

- Launch a recurring series on internships, scholarships, universities, careers and subject combos, built for Pakistan.
- Add a Stories Q&A "Where do you want to study after A Levels?" to source questions and prove demand.
- Add a comment-keyword to each piece ("comment SCHOLARSHIP") that triggers a DM with a resource or tool. This extends the planned IBCC comment-to-DM automation.
- Aim at both students and parents (parents decide on fees).

### Priority 2: shift carousels from reaction to utility (closes the ceiling gap)

- Use numbers and lists people save: "the exact number of past papers for [subject]", "most repeated topics in [paper]", "[subject] mistakes by command word".
- Keep news and leak coverage, but as a secondary format.
- Test whether utility carousels reach the 1,000+ shares-per-post target. Track saves and shares in Insights on our own posts.

### Priority 3: build data hooks from our own students

- Poll the Stories audience and open reels with the result ("X students said…"), as Teachwink did with 674 students.
- Run a monthly poll or Q&A that asks "which chapter or paper is worst?" per subject and turn results into a reel and a carousel.

### Priority 4: rebuild polls, sliders and quizzes around exam and results moments

- **Polls have a zero modern baseline.** Run "pick a side" polls through exam season and treat the first month as a test.
- **Results eve is an annual event.** Repeat the expected-grades slider (600 and 554 votes), then post "X% expected an A*, Y% got it" after results.
- **Bring back Maths, Physics and Chemistry basics quizzes** at a daily or near-daily cadence. 2025's quizzes averaged about 37 answers each.
- **Run "Which paper humbled you?" after each paper day.** It was one of the highest-reply prompts.

### Priority 5: reach plays

- Test Roman Urdu relatable exam-hall memes. Their 517K reel supports the plan for a separate meme page that feeds SJ Academy later.
- Keep one broad "every [O/A Level] student must know" reel per week.
- Keep practicals promos in rotation, but do not treat them as the follower engine.

### Priority 6: parents

- One parent-facing POV or results-analysis post per week. Their parent/POV posts have a median of 641 likes versus a baseline of 128 (small sample, treat as a test).

### Suggested metrics (against our stated goals: 50,000 followers, posts that reliably earn 1,000+ shares)

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Shares per post (from Insights) | Direct measure of the 1,000+ shares goal |
| Saves per carousel | Proxy for utility |
| Reel views to follower conversion | Detects promo-heavy versus broad reels |
| Keyword-comment count and DM replies | Lead capture |
| Story Q&A replies, poll votes, slider votes | Audience size and engagement trend |

## 9. Caveats

- Shares are hidden for every account, including ours via this method. Use Insights on our own account for real share counts.
- Instagram returns only the first 20 replies for each Q&A prompt, so the sheet holds 1,081 of 1,518 Q&A responses (usernames removed).
- Quiz "percent correct" is not shown. Instagram does not return a reliable correct-answer index for every quiz, so only total answers per quiz are exact.
- The Stories archive does not expose story reach or impressions, only interaction counts.
- Teachwink's grid loaded 647 of 854 posts, and the grid has no dates (recency is by position).
- Boosted versus organic cannot be confirmed from outside. High-like, low-comment promo videos were treated as paid.
- Theme medians use truncated captions and some groups are small.
