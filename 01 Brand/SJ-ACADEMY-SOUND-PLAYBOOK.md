# SJ Academy sound playbook

**Version:** 1.2, October 2026
**Builds on:** `SJ-ACADEMY-BRAND-BOOK.md` (the core) and `SJ-ACADEMY-VIDEO-MOTION-PLAYBOOK.md`.
**Covers:** the sonic logo, the desk sound kit, music, voice, mixing levels and licensing.
**Files:** `01 Brand/Sound/` (prototype WAV files at 48kHz; MP3 copies in `Sound/mp3/`). Play them all in `sj-academy-brand-book.html`, "Sound" section.

### The short version

1. **The voice comes first.** Music sits 14 to 22 dB under speech; lessons have no music under explanation.
2. **Our sounds are desk sounds:** pen ticks, highlighter, stamp, paper. No stock whooshes.
3. **The sonic logo ends every video over 10 seconds**, with the logo on screen.
4. **License everything.** No trending songs on the business account or in ads.
5. **Mix to about -14 LUFS** and check every video on a phone speaker.

---

## 1. Why sound matters

Sound is the asset most brands skip, and owned sounds (a sonic logo, a recurring effect) do more for recognition than borrowed ones (a trending song). A student who hears the same pen tick and three notes at the end of every SJ Academy video starts to know it is SJ Academy before the logo appears, even with the phone face down.

Three rules run through everything below:

1. **Own the sound.** A small set of sounds that are only ours, used every time.
2. **The voice comes first.** Music and effects never compete with a teacher explaining something.
3. **License everything.** If we did not make it or buy a licence for this use, we do not use it.

## 2. The sonic logo

**The idea:** the sound of a paper being marked right. Two quick pen ticks, then three notes rising to a resolved, bright finish. Marked, then correct.

| Part | What it is | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Two ticks | A dry pen tick, twice, a tenth of a second apart | 0.00 and 0.11s |
| Three notes | Rising: the 3rd, 5th and top note of a major chord (E, G, C in the prototype), on a warm mallet sound | 0.26, 0.40 and 0.56s |
| The ring | The last note rings out with a soft low note under it | to about 2s |

**The melody is the asset, not the instrument.** The two ticks and the three rising notes can be played on a mallet, a guitar pluck, a piano, a synth, a sitar or a rubab, at any key and speed, and it is still the SJ Academy sound. Change the instrument to suit the video; never change the rhythm or the shape of the melody.

### Versions

| File | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `sj-sonic-logo-full.wav` | about 2s of sound, with a ring | End card of every video over 10 seconds. The logo lands with the first note |
| `sj-sonic-logo-short.wav` | 1s | YouTube bumper after the cold open, chapter changes, 9-second ads |

### Rules

- **Always at the end, never at the start.** Openings belong to the hook.
- Always with the logo on screen. The sound and the logo arrive together.
- When music is playing, the logo's three notes match the music's key, or the music stops for it. Never play it on top of a clashing chord.
- Do not remix it into a jingle, add words to it, or loop it.

**Status: prototype, not final.** These files were synthesised to set the idea and the timing. They are not good enough to ship as the brand's sound, and the melody needs work: three notes rising up a major chord is the most common "success" sound there is (game level-ups, app confirmations), so on its own it is not ownable. The two pen ticks are the distinctive part. Keep them, and let a composer find a melody that is ours.

### Brief for the composer

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| The idea | A paper being marked right: two real pen ticks, then a short melody that resolves, bright and confident |
| Length | 1.5 to 2 seconds of sound, plus a natural ring-out. A 1-second short version |
| Notes | Three to five. Must be hummable after hearing it twice |
| Must not | Sound like a game level-up, a phone notification, a school bell, a news jingle, or any existing sonic logo |
| Must work on | Mallet, plucked guitar, piano, a synth, and one South Asian instrument (rubab or sitar), at different tempos |
| Deliver | The full and short versions as 48kHz WAV (a celebration version later, if needed), plus the melody written out, and the stems |
| Rights | Full ownership, or an exclusive, perpetual licence covering all media and ads |
| Test | Play the two or three best options to ten students. Ask them to hum it back the next day. Pick the one most of them remember |

Record the pen ticks with a real pen on exam paper (section 3) and give them to the composer to build on.

## 3. The desk sound kit

Each of the five desk moves in the video playbook has a sound. Together they make SJ Academy videos sound like a real desk, not a template.

| Sound | File | Plays with | Level under voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pen tick | `sj-sfx-pen-tick.wav` | A tick drawn on paper, a score appearing | 6 dB under |
| Pen circle | `sj-sfx-pen-circle.wav` | A circle or underline drawing on | 8 dB under |
| Highlighter swipe | `sj-sfx-highlighter-swipe.wav` | The highlighter swipe and wipe | 6 dB under |
| Stamp | `sj-sfx-stamp.wav` | A stamp landing | 3 dB under |
| Paper slide | `sj-sfx-paper-slide.wav` | Paper, cards and tickets sliding in | 10 dB under |

Rules:

- **Sync to the frame.** The sound starts on the first frame of the move (the stamp sound on the frame it touches the paper).
- **Not every move needs a sound.** In a busy edit, sound the important moves and let the rest be silent. Three or four effects in 15 seconds is plenty.
- **No stock "whoosh" on every cut.** Our transitions sound like paper and pens. Whooshes, risers, booms and swooshes from sound packs make a video sound like every other ad.

### Record your own (recommended)

The prototypes are synthesised. Real recordings will sound better and will be truly ours. One 30-minute session in a quiet classroom:

1. Phone or recorder 20 to 30cm from the desk, AC and fans off.
2. Record each sound ten times: pen ticks on exam paper, a circle drawn quickly, a highlighter swiped across a word, a rubber stamp on a pad of paper, a sheet slid across the desk.
3. Keep the best two of each. Trim, remove noise, and name them like the files above with `-rec` added.
4. Save them in `01 Brand/Sound/` and replace the prototypes in the table.

## 4. Music

### Direction by video type

| Video | Tempo | Feel | Instruments | Under speech |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ads (Hype) | 120 to 140 BPM | Confident, bright, moving forward | Claps, plucks, light drums, bass. Optionally one local percussion element (see below) | Ducked 14 to 18 dB under voice |
| Teacher reels | 85 to 105 BPM | Light and focused | Soft keys, plucks, gentle beat. Or no music at all | Ducked 18 to 22 dB, or none |
| Lessons and YouTube walkthroughs | | No music under explanation | Music only in the cold open, bumper, chapter cards and end screen | None |
| Testimonials | 75 to 95 BPM | Warm, honest | Piano, acoustic guitar, soft pads | Ducked 18 to 22 dB |
| Results day, events | 120 to 150 BPM | Celebration | Full drums, dholak or tabla, claps, bright synths | Ducked 12 to 16 dB under any speech |
| Brand films | Builds from 90 to 130 BPM | From quiet to proud | Starts sparse, builds, ends on the sonic logo | Natural sound moments with music pulled out |
| Memes | Whatever the joke needs | | Licensed library sounds, our own desk sounds, or voice only | |

### The local touch

A Karachi brand should not sound like a stock music library. **Where music is used in Hype videos, consider one local percussion element**: dholak, tabla, matka or a hand-clap pattern from a local rhythm, layered into an otherwise modern track. It is a sound Karachi students recognise, and few tuition brands use it well.

- One local element, not a pastiche. No sitar-and-tabla clichés over every video.
- Never sample a famous song, qawwali or film track.

### What music never does

- Plays louder than a teacher explaining.
- Uses a trending song on the SJ Academy account or in an ad without a licence (section 6).
- Changes the mood mid-sentence. Edit music to phrase and sentence breaks.
- Ends without resolving. Cut to the end of a bar, or let the sonic logo resolve it.

## 5. Voice

| Voice | Use | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Teachers** | Reels, lessons, ads, voiceover | Their own natural voice and speech, including Urdu mixed in. Captions in English |
| **Students and parents** | Testimonials, results day, campus videos | Unscripted. Written consent to record and publish their voice |
| **Voiceover for ads** | When a video needs narration | A teacher's voice first. A hired voice artist second, with a written licence for ads |
| **AI voices** (proposed, to confirm) | Rough drafts and timing only | Never used to imitate a real teacher, student or parent. Never published as if it were a real person |

Recording:

- A lapel mic for talking heads, close to the mouth (15 to 20cm).
- A quiet room: soft furnishing, AC off, door closed. Record 10 seconds of room silence each time.
- Speak to one person, not to a crowd. Short sentences.
- Leave a breath of silence before and after each take for clean edits.

## 6. Licensing

**This is practical guidance, not legal advice.** When in doubt, do not use the track.

| Source | Organic posts on SJ Academy's Instagram and Facebook | Paid ads | YouTube |
|---|---|---|---|
| Our own recordings and the sonic logo | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Meta Sound Collection | Yes (Instagram and Facebook only) | No | No |
| Instagram's main music library (trending songs) | No. Business accounts are limited to licensed or Meta Sound Collection audio | No | No |
| Paid music library with a commercial and ads licence | Yes | Yes, if the licence covers ads | Yes, if the licence covers YouTube |
| A commissioned track (written agreement, we own or license it) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| A song from a film, a famous artist or a viral video | No | No | No |

Rules:

- **"It's in the app" is not a licence.** A track being available in Instagram's library does not clear it for a business account, and never for an ad.
- Keep a simple licence log (track, source, licence type, date, where used) in `01 Brand/Sound/licence-log.csv`.
- Prefer one-off licences or commissioned tracks for ads, so a lapsed subscription does not leave old ads exposed.
- The unbranded meme page is outside this system, but the same copyright law applies to it.

## 7. Mixing and levels

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Final mix, all social and YouTube | About -14 LUFS integrated, true peak no higher than -1 dBTP |
| Speech | The loudest, clearest thing in the mix. Consistent level from start to end |
| Music under speech | 14 to 22 dB under the voice (table in section 4). Duck smoothly, 200 to 400ms |
| Desk sounds | 3 to 10 dB under the voice (table in section 3) |
| Sonic logo | At the same loudness as the voice, or 2 dB above it on end cards with no speech |
| Phone check | Every mix is checked on a phone speaker and on earphones before publishing |

Clean-up order for speech: remove noise (gently), cut low rumble below 80Hz, even out the level, then limit the final mix.

---

## 8. Sound-off design

Most feed viewers start with sound off. Sound must reward, never be required:

- Captions carry every word that matters (Video playbook, section 6).
- Jokes that depend on a sound need a visual cue too.
- The sonic logo always arrives with the logo, so it is never the only brand signal.

## 9. Checklist

- [ ] Speech clear and steady on a phone speaker
- [ ] Music licensed for this exact use; logged
- [ ] Music ducked under every line of speech
- [ ] Desk sounds on the important moves only; no stock whooshes
- [ ] Sonic logo with the logo on the end card, in key with the music
- [ ] About -14 LUFS, peaks at -1 dBTP or below
- [ ] Works with sound off
