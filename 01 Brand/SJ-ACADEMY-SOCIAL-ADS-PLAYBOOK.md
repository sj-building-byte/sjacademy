# SJ Academy social and ads playbook

**Version:** 1.2, October 2026
**Builds on:** `SJ-ACADEMY-BRAND-BOOK.md` (the core). Where they disagree, the brand book wins.
**Covers:** Instagram and Facebook posts, carousels, stories, static ads, reel covers, YouTube thumbnails, WhatsApp status and share images.
**Visual reference:** `sj-academy-brand-book.html`, "Formats" section.

### The short version

1. **Shares are the goal.** Every post should be something a student sends to a friend. Target: 1,000+ shares per post on the way to 50,000 followers.
2. **Teach in carousels, entertain in reels, never post a flyer.**
3. **Choose a format, not a template.** Six formats; never repeat the last one; at least three per campaign.
4. **Real or nothing:** real photos, real answers, real reviews. No stock, no placeholders.
5. **Measure by format every month.** Make more of what gets shared, drop what does not.

---

## 1. What this playbook fixes

Until now, social posts and ads borrowed the website's page layout: dotted canvas, highlighted headline, polaroid with tape, offset card, orange button. Every ad looked like a small web page, and every ad looked like the last one.

On social, people are not reading a page. They are scrolling past hundreds of things, and they stop for an idea, a face, a number or a joke. So this playbook gives you **formats, not one template**: six shapes to choose from, each with its own job, all carrying the same core assets.

What the data says about SJ Academy's own account (Instagram export, September 2025 to May 2026, 309 posts):

| What | What happened |
|---|---|
| Value carousels (cheat codes, examiner traps, "you're doing past papers wrong") | The most saved and shared posts on the account: up to 5,000+ saves |
| Short relatable reels (6 to 9 seconds, countdowns, exam-hall jokes) | The most shared: 10,000 to 16,000 shares on the best ones |
| Plain promotional images and flyers | High paid reach, almost no shares or saves |
| Median carousel | About 9,900 reach, 359 shares, 197 saves |
| Median reel | About 9,850 reach, 172 shares, 35 saves |

So: **teach in carousels, entertain in reels, and never make an ad that looks like a flyer.**

## 2. Before you design

1. **Write the five-line brief** (brand book, section 9): who and when, the one thing, the idea, the proof, the format and level.
2. **Pick the format** from section 4. Check the last three posts and ads; do not repeat their format.
3. **Pick the expression level** (1 Calm, 2 Clear, 3 Loud) and the brand presence (Signed, Branded).
4. **Pick one ground** from the brand book, section 7.

If you cannot write the idea in one line, the post is not ready to design.

**The share test.** Before designing, answer: who would send this, to whom, and what would they type with it? ("Sending this to the group chat: this is literally us before Paper 3.") If there is no honest answer, the idea is not finished. Shares are what the account is measured on.

## 3. Sizes and safe zones

| Item | Canvas | Keep important content inside |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post, carousel, feed ad | 1080 x 1350 (4:5) | 72px from every edge. The profile grid shows posts at 3:4, which trims about 34px from each side of a 4:5 post, so the 72px margin covers it |
| Story, Reel, story ad, WhatsApp status | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | Top 270px and bottom 670px are covered by the app. Right 230px of the lower half holds the like and share buttons. The safe area is roughly the middle 950 x 980px. For ads with a disclaimer, keep the bottom 40% clear |
| Reel cover | 1080 x 1920 | Title and face inside the middle 1080 x 1440, so the grid crop keeps them |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280 x 720 | Keep the bottom-right corner clear (the video length covers it) |
| Share image (Open Graph) | 1200 x 630 | 60px from every edge |
| Square fallback | 1080 x 1080 | 72px from every edge |

Check platform specs every six months. They change.

### Type sizes on a 1080px-wide canvas

A 1080px post is shown about 390 points wide on a phone, so everything is three times smaller than it looks on a laptop.

| Role | Size | Font |
|---|---|---|
| Display (one word or number) | 200 to 600px | Poppins 700 (800 for numbers only) |
| Headline | 72 to 120px | Poppins 600 or 700 |
| Subhead | 44 to 56px | Poppins 500 |
| Body | 34 to 40px. Never below 30px | Poppins 400 |
| Small print, labels | 26 to 30px. Never below 24px | Poppins 500, uppercase for labels |
| Handwritten note | 48 to 72px | Caveat 600 |

Headlines: 8 words or fewer on ads, 12 or fewer on carousel covers.

## 4. The formats

Six formats. Each one carries the core assets in its own way and has one job. Never run the same one twice in a row; use at least three per campaign. Six is enough to keep the feed varied and few enough that every one gets a proper template.

### F1. The marked answer

**Job:** shows the method. A real or sample exam answer, marked in red pen, with the score it got.
**Best for:** ads to students, "why you lost marks" posts.
**Build:** the exam paper (navy text on off-white) fills most of the frame at a slight tilt. Red ticks, a circled score like "6/12", one margin note. A short headline above it. Labelled "sample" unless it is real and consented.
**Do not:** use an answer that is unreadable at phone size.

### F2. Big type

**Job:** one number or one sentence does all the talking.
**Best for:** countdowns ("7 months"), deadlines and event dates, statements people agree or argue with.
**Build:** Poppins 800 for a number or Poppins 700 for a sentence of 3 to 8 words, filling most of the frame on a full yellow, brown or ink ground. The highlighter on one or two words. At most one short line and one handwritten aside. Lots of empty space.
**Do not:** add icons, badges or a second message to fill the space. Use only numbers and dates that are facts.

### F3. The phone

**Job:** speaks in the formats students use all day, so it does not look like an ad.
**Best for:** relatable plans and lists, conversations, reviews.
**Build:** a recreated phone screen: Notes app list, WhatsApp chat, search bar, calendar, screen time, or a real Google review as it appears. SJ Academy's words, one highlighter, the logo at the top of the frame.
**Do not:** imitate a real person's chat or another app's logo in a way that could be mistaken for the real thing. Invented names only; reviews word for word.

### F4. The mark scheme

**Job:** pure value. The posts students save and share.
**Best for:** command words, examiner traps, checklists, formula sheets.
**Build:** a carousel. One rule per slide with one example, numbered ("3/8"). The last slide offers the class.

### F5. The real moment

**Job:** proves the people and the place are real.
**Best for:** teacher posts, in-class and lab ads, thumbnails, results day.
**Build:** a real photo or video frame, full-bleed, cropped tight on hands, faces or work. One pen mark on it (a circle, an arrow). For teachers, a name tag with name and subject code. An ink scrim only where text needs it.
**Do not:** use stock or placeholder images, students without written consent, or text over faces. This format waits for the photo shoot (section 7).

### F6. The take

**Job:** shares. Puts the account in students' group chats.
**Best for:** exam panic, countdown jokes, POV posts, tier lists ("subject combos ranked by how much they ruin your sleep").
**Build:** a familiar meme or ranking structure made with our own photos, footage or type. Yellow somewhere; logo small or absent.
**Do not:** use copyrighted film stills, celebrity photos or songs. Never rank or joke about a named school, academy or person.

### Adding a format

A new format joins this list only when it has been used once, beat the account's average shares, and has a one-line job. If a format is added, consider which one it replaces.

## 5. Carousels

| Slide | Job | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cover | Stop the scroll | The hook in 12 words or fewer. The format must be obvious at a glance. Logo at the top. A swipe cue on the right edge |
| 2 | Pay off the hook immediately | No warm-up slide. The first useful thing goes here |
| 3 to 8 | One idea per slide | 40 words at most per slide. Same layout grid on every slide. Number the slides ("3/8") |
| Second last | The surprise or the best tip | Keep the best one late so people swipe |
| Last | The next step | Save, share or send this to a friend, plus the class offer: "Book a free demo on WhatsApp" |

Rules:

- 5 to 10 slides. Value carousels can run to 10. Ads, 3 to 5.
- One ground for the whole carousel, with one change at most (for the last slide).
- The cover may use a different format from the inner slides; the inner slides share one layout.
- Captions: plain text, no markdown. Hook in the first line. 3 to 5 hashtags at the end.

## 6. Static ads

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hook | 8 words or fewer. Names the student's situation, not the academy |
| Proof | One proof point from `FACTS.md`, or a marked answer, or a real face |
| Offer | The free demo class. Never a discount unless it is real and approved |
| Action | A button-shaped CTA: "Book a free demo class". The platform adds its own button too |
| Logo | At the top |
| Contact | WhatsApp number or sjacademy.pk, small, at the bottom |

Rules:

- **Every ad set has three or four creatives in different formats.** For example: a marked answer, a teacher, a big number and a phone format. Never four colour versions of one layout.
- **Write for students taking the subject for the first time.** They do not yet know they are losing marks, so do not assume exam experience.
- Text on the image is short. The ad copy above it carries the detail.
- Refresh creative every two to three weeks, or sooner if results drop.

## 7. Photography and imagery

### What to shoot

Build a library of real photos, shot once a term. A phone is fine, in daylight.

| Shot | Use |
|---|---|
| Each teacher at the board, mid-explanation, chest up | Teacher format, thumbnails |
| Each teacher looking at the camera, plain background, daylight | Cut-outs, thumbnails |
| Teacher marking a paper, red pen visible | Marked answer, classroom |
| Students working, from behind or side-on (faces only with consent) | Classroom |
| The lab in use, close-ups of hands and apparatus | Practicals |
| The desk from above: papers, highlighter, calculator | Desk format |
| The building, the sign, the corridor at class time | Proof of place |

### How it should look

- Natural light first. Warm and true to life. No heavy filters, no teal-and-orange look.
- Tight crops are better than wide, empty rooms.
- Candid beats posed. If posed, mid-action.
- Flash photos are allowed for Level 3 (results day, events). They look honest.

### Graphics and illustration

- **Allowed:** pen marks (ticks, circles, arrows, underlines), the highlighter, stamps, the exam paper, line icons.
- **Allowed with care:** a 3D subject object (a flask, a calculator) at Level 3, one per frame, only where real footage does not exist.
- **Not allowed:** stock photos, AI-generated images, cartoon mascots, emoji as decoration, clip art, lens flares, glows, gradients between brand colours.

## 8. Grid and rhythm

- On the profile grid, alternate grounds so the grid has rhythm: a yellow post, a photo post, a light post. Avoid three posts of the same ground in a row.
- Reel covers follow the cover rule in section 3 so the grid stays readable.
- Pinned posts: one brand film, one "how classes work", one proof post. Refresh each term.

## 9. Moments

The academic year has fixed moments. Plan the creative six weeks ahead.

| Moment | When | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Exam season | May to June, October to November | Countdowns (big number), cheat-code carousels, calm reassurance reels |
| Results day | May/June results in August, Oct/Nov results in January | Report card format, consented results only, the celebratory sonic logo |
| Registration windows | Before each new batch | Ticket format, teacher format, offer ads |
| Independence Day | 14 August | One post with a Karachi or Pakistan detail. Green and white may appear in the photo, not as new brand colours |
| Ramadan and Eid | Changes yearly | Calmer, brown and canvas grounds, timing posts around iftar and sehri |
| Winter break | December | Revision plans, phone format |

## 10. Measure and learn

A brand grows fastest when it learns faster than it posts. Every post and ad gets a format code in the tracker, so results can be compared by format, not by guesswork.

| Code | Format |
|---|---|
| F1 to F6 | The formats in section 4 |
| Fx | A new format being tested |

**What to measure:**

| Content | Main number | Also watch |
|---|---|---|
| Organic posts | Shares per 1,000 reach | Saves per 1,000 reach, follows |
| Ads | Cost per WhatsApp conversation | Demo bookings, registrations |

For reference, the account's median carousel (September 2025 to May 2026) earned about 36 shares per 1,000 reach. 1,000 shares on 10,000 reach is 100 per 1,000, almost three times that, so the bar is high and the format matters.

**Every month:**

1. List the top three and bottom three posts by shares per 1,000 reach, with their format codes.
2. Make three new variations of the top format: new hook, new person or new topic.
3. If a format is in the bottom three for three months in a row, retire it from regular use.
4. Make one bet: a new format or a new series, marked Fx.
5. For ads, keep the cheapest format per conversation and replace the most expensive one.

## 11. Checklist before posting

- [ ] Share test answered: who sends it, to whom, and why
- [ ] Format code recorded in the tracker

- [ ] Five-line brief written
- [ ] Format chosen, different from the last three
- [ ] Logo at the top (Signed) or on the last slide (Branded)
- [ ] Yellow and the highlighter present
- [ ] Only Poppins and Caveat
- [ ] One ground, one accent
- [ ] Text inside the safe zone, body 30px or larger
- [ ] Facts only from `FACTS.md`; sample work labelled
- [ ] Consent for every student shown or named
- [ ] No em dashes; sentence case; no buzzwords
- [ ] Caption in plain text, hook in the first line
