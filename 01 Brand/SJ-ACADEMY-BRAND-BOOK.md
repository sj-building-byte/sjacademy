# SJ Academy brand book

**Version:** 4.2, October 2026
**Status:** Current. The core of the SJ Academy brand system. Every channel playbook builds on this file.
**Owner:** Sunyyan Junaid. Changes need his approval and a change log entry (section 12).
**Visual reference:** `01 Brand/sj-academy-brand-book.html` shows the expressive layer, the formats, motion and sound, live.

Read this file first, then the playbook for the channel you are working in.

### The short version

1. **Lock the assets, free the ideas.** Logo, SJ yellow, the highlighter, the teacher's pen, Poppins, the voice and real people never change form. Everything else can.
2. **Start every piece with the five-line brief** (section 9), never with the last file.
3. **Pick a level:** Calm for information, Clear for teaching, Loud for ads and moments.
4. **One idea per frame, page or slide.** If it needs two, make two.
5. **Only true things:** facts from `FACTS.md`, real people with consent, sample work labelled.

## 1. Why the system changed

Version 3 was a website design system, and it was being used for everything. Ads, reels and carousels borrowed the web page layout (dotted canvas, highlighted headline, polaroid, offset card, orange button), so they all started to look alike. There were no rules for video, motion or sound, so each video invented its own look and none of it carried over.

Version 4 splits the brand into two layers:

- **A small core that never changes.** These are the things that make SJ Academy recognisable: the logo, the yellow, the highlighter, the teacher's pen, Poppins, the voice and the facts. They appear everywhere, in the same form.
- **A wide expressive range that changes on purpose.** Layout, scale, photography, formats, motion, music and humour are free to move, within the ranges in each playbook.

The rule behind it: **lock the assets, free the ideas.** People recognise a brand by a few assets that always look the same. Everything around those assets should keep changing, or the audience stops noticing.

## 2. The idea

**SJ Academy is a good teacher's desk.**

On it: the exam paper, the red pen, the highlighter, sticky notes, the mark scheme, the stamp, a phone full of student messages, a calculator, past papers in a pile. Every piece of SJ Academy work is something that could come off that desk, said in the voice of the teacher sitting at it.

Version 3 described this as a notebook. The desk keeps the notebook and adds everything a real teacher uses, which gives video, sound and social far more to work with:

| On the desk | Becomes |
|---|---|
| The highlighter | The signature mark, a transition in video, a sound |
| The red pen | Marking: ticks, circles, scores, margin notes |
| The exam paper | Proof of method: real questions, marked answers |
| The stamp | A verdict: a grade, a deadline, "seats full" |
| The phone | Native formats: Notes app, WhatsApp chat, search bar, screenshots |
| The teacher | The face of the brand: real people talking straight to camera |
| The classroom | Real footage, real sound, real students (with consent) |

### Brand platform (proposed, to confirm)

The design system says how SJ Academy looks. The platform says what it stands for, so every piece, however playful, says the same thing underneath. Everything here is a proposal until the owner approves it; nothing below is a new public claim.

**What we do:** SJ Academy teaches Cambridge O Level and A Level students how marks are actually awarded, so effort turns into grades.

**Tagline:** none set yet. The approved A Level line is "We teach the method that gets you A*s." "Center of Excellence" belongs to the logo only, and "Cambridge doesn't mark effort. It marks method." is not the brand promise.

**Three pillars, each with its proof** (proof only from `FACTS.md`):

| Pillar | What it means to a student | Proof we can show |
|---|---|---|
| **Method** | You learn where the marks are, not just the content | Past papers marked to the mark scheme. Feedback on the marks you lost. A marked answer on screen |
| **Teachers** | You know exactly who teaches you | Named teachers from LUMS, IBA and ex-KGS, with faces, subjects and codes |
| **Access** | You can try it before you commit, wherever you are | A free demo class (except lab practicals). In class at DHA Phase 7 or live online |

**Personality:**

| SJ Academy is | Never |
|---|---|
| Sharp: specific, exam-literate | Smug or show-off |
| Funny: dry, relatable, a little Gen Z aware | Mean, or aimed at a named place or person |
| Confident: proves things with real work | Loud about itself, or vague ("best", "top") |
| Warm: honest feedback because it cares | Soft, or full of empty praise |

**What each reader should hear:**

- **Student:** "You're already working hard. Here is where the marks actually are."
- **Parent:** "Named teachers, marked work, clear fees, and your child can try a class before you pay."

## 3. The core: what never changes

These are SJ Academy's distinctive assets. They are the same on a web page, a reel, a thumbnail and a printed worksheet. Their **form** never changes. **How they are used** (size, position, timing, motion) can change within each playbook.

| # | Asset | The fixed form | Must appear |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Logo** | The open book forming S and J, wordmark, "Center of Excellence". Never redrawn, retyped, tilted or recoloured (section 6) | On everything except unbranded formats (section 5) |
| 2 | **SJ yellow** | `#FCE004`. Never a tint, never a gradient | On every piece, within the first glance or the first 3 seconds of a video |
| 3 | **The highlighter** | A flat yellow band covering the lower half of the letters, with soft ends. Never an underline, never a box | At least once on every branded piece |
| 4 | **The teacher's pen** | Red pen marks the work (ticks, scores, circles). Orange-brown pen talks to the reader (notes, arrows) | Wherever work is being judged |
| 5 | **Poppins** | The typeface for all reading text, on every channel | Every piece with text |
| 6 | **The voice** | A teacher who knows the mark scheme and has a dry sense of humour. Specific, short, never a buzzword | Every word |
| 7 | **Real people** | Real teachers, real students, real classrooms. No stock photos, no AI-generated people | Every photo and video of people |
| 8 | **The sonic logo** (once approved) | The two pen ticks and the rising notes (Sound playbook, section 2) | The end of every video longer than 10 seconds |

Assets 1 to 7 are in use today. Asset 8 joins the core when the final version is recorded and approved.

If a piece has the logo, the yellow, the highlighter and Poppins, it is SJ Academy, whatever else it does.

## 4. The range: what is free to change

Everything not in section 3 is free, within the ranges below. Each playbook gives the detail.

| Element | Website (calm) | Social, ads and video (expressive) |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | Page families and templates | Any of the formats in the Social playbook, plus new ones that pass section 9 |
| Ground colour | Canvas, white, one brown band, one yellow band | Full-bleed yellow, brown, ink, canvas or a real photo |
| Display type | Poppins 600 and 500 | Poppins up to 700 (800 for numbers only), at any size the idea needs |
| Type scale | H1 32 to 54px | As large as the idea needs. One word can fill the frame |
| Paper elements | Required by page family, with density limits | Optional. Use them when they carry the idea, skip them when they do not |
| Photography | Polaroids and rounded frames | Full-bleed, cropped tight, phone-shot, flash, overhead, behind the scenes |
| Motion | 120 to 200ms interface feedback only | The motion language in the Video playbook, at two speeds |
| Sound | None | Music, desk sounds and the sonic logo (Sound playbook) |
| Humour | One dry line per section at most | Memes, POV formats, skits, borrowed formats, as the Social playbook allows |
| Speech | English only | Natural speech, including Urdu mixed in. On-screen text and captions stay English |

### The expression dial

Every piece sits at one level. Pick the level before you start.

| Level | Name | Used for | Feels like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Calm** | Fee notices, timetables, policies, worksheets, Info and Tool pages | The mark scheme. Clear, quiet, useful |
| 2 | **Clear** | Most website pages, value carousels, lesson videos, PDFs, teacher reels | The good teacher explaining. Notebook marks, steady pace |
| 3 | **Loud** | Ads, launch reels, results day, events, thumbnails, brand films | The teacher who stands on the desk. Big type, full colour, fast cuts, music |

Level 3 is where most of the new creative freedom lives. Level 1 stays strict, because people come to those pieces to get something done.

## 5. Brand presence

How much of the brand shows depends on the job.

| Presence | When | What appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Signed** | Ads, announcements, thumbnails, end cards, PDFs, the website | Logo, yellow, highlighter, Poppins, CTA |
| **Branded** | Value carousels, teacher reels, memes on the SJ Academy account | Yellow and highlighter always. Logo small, or on the last slide or end card only |
| **Unbranded** | The separate O/A Level meme page | None of the SJ Academy assets. Not covered by this system |

A post that teaches something can carry less branding, because the value earns the attention. An ad carries more, because it has to be remembered.

## 6. Logo

The full rules, file list and minimum sizes are in the Web playbook, section 3. The rules that apply everywhere:

- Pick the version by background: on-light, on-brown or on-yellow. One-colour versions for photocopies and single-colour print.
- Clear space: one quarter of the logo's height on every side.
- Never stretch, rotate, tilt, recolour, outline, add a shadow or glow, rearrange, or retype the wordmark.
- The logo is never taped, stamped, circled or animated apart. In video it may fade or slide in as one piece, and nothing else.
- **On ads and social posts, the logo sits at the top** (owner's rule). On 9:16 video it sits just below the top 270px, so the platform's interface does not cover it.
- On photography, only over a calm area or a solid patch. Never over faces.
- One logo per screen or slide.

## 7. Colour

### Core palette (every channel)

| Name | Value | Job |
|---|---|---|
| SJ yellow | `#FCE004` | The highlighter. The brand's loudest asset |
| Ink | `#151515` | Type, outlines |
| Canvas | `#F7F5EF` | Paper |
| Brown | `#453516` | Depth, method, proof |
| CTA orange | `#CC4A00` | Buttons and actions. White text on it |
| Pen red | `#D63A2F` (strokes) and `#B92F25` (text) | Marking: ticks, scores, circles, margin notes |
| Orange-brown pen | `#B54200` | The teacher talking to the reader: notes, arrows |

### Expressive grounds (social, ads, video, print covers)

Off the website, these may fill the whole frame. Each ground has fixed partners so contrast always holds.

| Ground | Headline | Body | Highlighter | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow `#FCE004` | Ink | Ink | A white band, or none | Brown, pen red |
| Brown `#453516` | Canvas or yellow | `#EDE6D3` | Yellow | Yellow. CTA orange only as a button fill with white text |
| Ink `#151515` | Canvas | `#D9D4C7` | Yellow | Yellow. Pen red for strokes only, never text |
| Canvas `#F7F5EF` | Ink | `#2B2B2B` | Yellow | CTA orange, pen red |
| Photo | Canvas over an ink scrim | Canvas | Yellow | One accent only |

Rules:

- **Yellow appears on every piece.** As a ground, a highlighter or a band.
- **One accent per piece** besides yellow.
- **No gradients between brand colours.** The only gradient allowed is an ink scrim over photography or video, from 0% to 70% ink, for legibility.
- **No glows, no glass, no neon, no metallic gold.** Yellow is a highlighter, not a luxury colour.
- Bright orange `#FE6100` is for strokes and shapes only, never text and never behind white text.
- WhatsApp green `#25D366` only on WhatsApp buttons.
- Body text on any ground must reach 4.5:1 contrast. Large display type, 3:1. Checked: pen red text on yellow 4.5, on canvas 5.5; pen red on ink 3.9 (strokes only); CTA orange on brown 2.6 (never as text).

## 8. Type

| Family | Weights | Role | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Poppins** | 400, 500, 600 (web). Off the website also 700, and 800 for numbers | Every word people read: headlines, body, captions, buttons | Everywhere |
| **Caveat** | 600 | The teacher's handwriting: short notes, asides, marks | Everywhere, never for information the reader must not miss |

Rules:

- **Two families, never more.** No other fonts in any piece, including the theme's fonts, Canva defaults or editing app presets.
- **Expression comes from scale, weight and the highlighter, not from more typefaces.** One number in Poppins 800, or one word in Poppins 700, can fill a frame.
- Caveat is lowercase, six words or fewer, never below 20px on screen or 14pt in print.
- Sentence case everywhere. Uppercase only for small labels (eyebrows, chips, stamps).
- Big type is set tight: letter spacing from -0.02em to -0.04em as size grows, line height 0.9 to 1.1 for display.
- Fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts. Install both on every machine and in Canva.

## 9. How to stay creative without losing the brand

Most sameness comes from starting with the template. Start with the idea.

**Every piece begins with a five-line brief:**

1. **Who and when:** who sees this, and what are they doing when they see it? ("A Level student, scrolling at 11pm the week before mocks.")
2. **The one thing:** what they should think or do afterwards, in one sentence.
3. **The idea:** the twist that makes it worth stopping for (a borrowed format, a joke, a surprising number, a real moment), and who would send it to a friend, and why.
4. **The proof:** what makes it true: a marked answer, a real student, a fact from `FACTS.md`.
5. **The format and level:** which format from the Social or Video playbook, and which expression level.

**The variety rules:**

- **No two consecutive posts or ads use the same format.** Check the last three before choosing.
- **Every campaign uses at least three formats.** Test three or four variations of each ad, changing the opening, the person or the format, not just the colour.
- **Borrow formats from students' lives.** Notes app lists, WhatsApp chats, search bars, report cards, mark schemes, boarding passes, tier lists, player cards, year-in-review summaries. The format is borrowed; the assets stay SJ Academy.
- **Each new idea must pass three tests:**
  1. Does it still carry the core (logo where needed, yellow, highlighter, Poppins)?
  2. Is it true? Real people, real work, facts from the register.
  3. Would a good teacher be happy to have made it? Funny is fine, mean is not.

If it passes all three, it is on brand, even if it looks nothing like the last piece. That is the point.

## 10. Voice in one page

The full rules are in the landing page and blog guidelines. The core, on every channel:

- **The voice of a teacher who knows the mark scheme.** Specific over general: "6 of the 12 marks" beats "lots of marks".
- **Dry, sarcastic humour that never offends.** No targeting a named place, school or person. Jokes about the exam, the syllabus, procrastination, Karachi life and student life are fine.
- **Short sentences. Active voice. No buzzwords. No em dashes. Sentence case.** No expletives, even mild or censored ones.
- **No unsupported claims.** No "best", "guaranteed", "highest-rated". Only facts from `FACTS.md`.
- **Name the student's problem before the solution.**
- **Language:** written copy, on-screen text and captions are English. In video, teachers and students speak naturally, including Urdu mixed in, and the captions translate it into natural English.
- **Never name an outside brand, copywriter or creator** that the voice or design draws on.

## 11. Rules that apply everywhere

- **Facts:** only from `00 Start Here/FACTS.md`. Do not lead with 950+ A*s. Never promise a class size. Label sample work "sample".
- **Consent:** no student's photo, name, voice, face or result without written consent from a parent or guardian (Web playbook, section 13). This covers video and audio too.
- **Cambridge:** never imply affiliation. No Cambridge logos. Resource content carries the "not affiliated" line.
- **Music and sound:** only licensed or owned audio (Sound playbook, section 6). A song being available in Instagram's library does not make it free to use on a business account or in an ad.
- **Accessibility:** captions on every video, contrast on every graphic, alt text on every web image.
- **Fonts and colours:** only those in sections 7 and 8.

## 12. Governance

- **Owner:** Sunyyan Junaid approves changes to this book and every playbook.
- **Versions:** patch (4.0.1) for fixes, minor (4.1) for new formats, sounds or rules, major (5.0) for a change to the core in section 3.
- **The core changes rarely.** Assets get stronger the longer they stay the same. Change the core only for a strong reason, and never because it feels familiar to the team. The audience notices it far less than you do.
- **The range changes often.** New formats, sounds and motion ideas are added to the playbooks whenever they work. Add them with an example.
- **Review:** twice a year, after each exam series (July and December), together with `FACTS.md`.
- **Where things live:** one current file per thing in `01 Brand`. Older versions go to `01 Brand/Archive`.
