# SJ Academy PDF and print playbook

**Version:** 1.2, October 2026
**Builds on:** `SJ-ACADEMY-BRAND-BOOK.md` (the core).
**Covers:** handouts and worksheets, model answer packs, revision guides and ebooks, lesson decks, LinkedIn and PDF carousels, posters and flyers, certificates, letters and notices.
**Existing templates:** `01 Brand/Templates/SJ Academy Letterhead 2026.pdf`.

### The short version

1. **Worksheets must survive a black-and-white photocopy.** Never rely on yellow alone; no yellow text, ever.
2. **Body text 10.5pt Poppins on A4**; lesson deck body never below 24px.
3. **Covers, posters and certificates can go loud**; inside pages stay calm.
4. **Proof before printing**, and name files to the pattern.

---

## 1. The idea in print

A printed SJ Academy page should look like the best handout a student has ever been given: clear enough to revise from, marked like a teacher marked it, and good enough to keep. Most of these documents are photocopied, printed at home in black and white, or read on a phone as a PDF. Design for all three.

| Document | Expression level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Worksheets, handouts, notices, fee letters | 1 Calm | Function first. Must survive a black-and-white photocopy |
| Revision guides, model answer packs, ebooks, lesson decks | 2 Clear | The notebook marks, used to teach |
| Covers, posters, flyers, certificates, PDF carousels | 3 Loud | The expressive layer: full colour, big type |

## 2. Page setup

| Item | A4 portrait (documents) | 16:9 (lesson decks) | 1080 x 1350 (PDF carousels) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Margins | Top 20mm, bottom 22mm, inside 20mm, outside 18mm | 64px all round on a 1920 x 1080 slide | 72px all round |
| Grid | 12 columns, 5mm gutter | 12 columns | 6 columns |
| Header | Small logo top left, document title top right, 9pt | Small logo top left on content slides | Logo top left on slide 1 and the last slide |
| Footer | sjacademy.pk, page number, document code and date, 8pt | Slide number, 12pt | Slide number |
| Bleed (print) | 3mm on every edge for anything printed to the edge | | |

## 3. Type in print

| Role | A4 document | Lesson deck (1920 x 1080) |
|---|---|---|
| Cover title | Poppins 700, 36 to 54pt | Poppins 700, 80 to 110px |
| H1 | Poppins 600, 22 to 26pt | Poppins 600, 56 to 64px |
| H2 | Poppins 500, 15 to 17pt | Poppins 500, 40 to 44px |
| H3 | Poppins 500, 12pt | Poppins 500, 32px |
| Body | Poppins 400, 10.5pt on 15pt leading | Poppins 400, 26 to 30px. Never below 24px |
| Small print, captions | Poppins 400, 8 to 8.5pt. Never below 7.5pt | 20px |
| Handwritten notes | Caveat 600, 14 to 18pt | Caveat 600, 36px or larger |
| Exam questions | Poppins 400 on the exam paper component, with marks in brackets: [8] | Same |

Rules:

- Lines of body text: 60 to 75 characters. On A4 use two columns for long notes, or one column with a wide margin for notes.
- Bold (Poppins 600) for key terms on first use. The highlighter for the single most important term on a page.
- Numbers in tables right-aligned.
- Lesson decks may be dense: they are presented in class and kept as study guides, so a full slide of structured notes is fine. Dense means organised, never crowded: clear headings, consistent grid, white space between blocks.

## 4. Colour in print

### Screen to print

| Colour | Screen | In print |
|---|---|---|
| SJ yellow | `#FCE004` | Near process yellow with a touch of magenta. For spot colour, check Pantone 102 C against a printed swatch book |
| Brown | `#453516` | Convert from the hex value, then match by eye on a printed proof. Brown shifts the most between printers |
| CTA orange | `#CC4A00` | Convert from the hex value and proof |
| Bright orange | `#FE6100` | For spot colour, check Pantone 1505 C against a swatch book |
| Ink | `#151515` | 100% black for text. A rich black for large solid areas, as the printer recommends |
| Pen red | `#D63A2F` | Convert from the hex value and proof |

Let the design app convert from the hex values, then print a proof before any large run. Once a printer gives values that match, write them into this table.

### Black-and-white survival

Worksheets and handouts are photocopied. They must still work in greyscale:

- The highlighter prints as a light grey band. Never rely on yellow alone; the word is also bold.
- Red pen marks print as dark grey. Ticks and crosses keep their shape, so meaning survives.
- No text in yellow, ever. No light grey text below 9pt.
- Answer lines and boxes in 0.5pt mid-grey, so students can write over them.
- Test: print one page in black and white before sending the file out.

## 5. Templates

| Template | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Worksheet** | Header with subject, code and topic. Name and date line. Numbered questions with marks in brackets. Ruled answer space (8mm lines). Footer |
| **Model answer** | The question on the exam paper component, the answer with marks in red pen, the examiner note, "Why it scored" in three steps. "Sample" label unless real and consented |
| **Revision guide or ebook** | Cover (Level 3), contents, one topic per spread, definitions in boxed notes, one boxed takeaway per topic, practice questions, answers at the back |
| **Lesson deck** | Title slide, objectives, concept slides with diagrams, a case study slide with company images (background-free PNGs), an exam question slide, a mark scheme slide, a summary slide |
| **PDF carousel** (LinkedIn, downloadable) | Same rules as Instagram carousels (Social playbook, section 5), exported as a PDF |
| **Poster or flyer** | Any format from the Social playbook, scaled to A4 or A3. Logo at the top. One message. A QR code to the page, with the address written under it |
| **Certificate** | Canvas or white, Poppins 700 for the student's name, Poppins for the rest, the stacked logo, a signature line, one yellow highlighter on the achievement. Real achievements only |
| **Letter and notice** | The letterhead template. Poppins 10.5pt. Plain and short |

### QR codes

- Point to a short, clean address on sjacademy.pk, never a raw tracking link.
- At least 20mm square in print, with a quiet border of 4mm.
- Ink on white or canvas only. Never on yellow photos or patterns.
- Write the address under it, so it works without a camera.

## 6. Exporting

| Use | Export |
|---|---|
| Print shop | PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4, CMYK, 300dpi images, 3mm bleed, fonts embedded, crop marks |
| Digital download | PDF, RGB, fonts embedded, images at 150 to 200dpi, under 10MB where possible, tagged headings for screen readers, document title set in the file properties |
| WhatsApp sharing | Under 5MB. First page works as the preview: a clear title and the logo |
| Lesson decks | PDF for students, the editable file kept in the subject's teaching folder (A Level Business: `Documents/A Level Business`) |

**File names:** `sj_[type]_[subject-code]_[topic]_v[n]_[YYYY-MM].pdf`, for example `sj_worksheet_9609_break-even_v2_2026-10.pdf`.

## 7. Canva and other tools

- Set up a Canva brand kit with the exact colours in the brand book, the two fonts (Poppins, Caveat), and the logo files from `01 Brand/Logos/SVG`.
- Build each template in section 5 once, then duplicate it. Do not start from Canva's own templates; they bring their own fonts and layouts.
- Do not use Canva's font pairings, effects (shadows, glows, curved text), stock photos of people or stickers.
