# SJ Academy sound files

Prototype sonic logo and desk sound kit, made 12 October 2026. Rules for using them are in `../SJ-ACADEMY-SOUND-PLAYBOOK.md`.

- `.wav` files: 48kHz, 16-bit, for editing and final mixes.
- `mp3/`: small copies for previews and the visual brand book.
- `licence-log.csv`: every music track and sound used in published work, with its licence.

These are synthesised prototypes. Replace the desk sounds with real recordings (`-rec` in the file name) and have the sonic logo recorded on real instruments, keeping the rhythm and melody.
