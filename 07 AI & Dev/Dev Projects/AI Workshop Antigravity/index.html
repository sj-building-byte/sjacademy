Course overview

Take your first real step into AI

You've probably heard about ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Perplexity, and Canva AI. You may have even tried some of them. But if you still don't know what to ask, which tool to use, or how AI can help with your actual work, study, or business, this workshop is for you.

This is a hands-on beginner workshop where you'll learn what AI can do, how to write better prompts, how to avoid common mistakes, and how to use AI for practical daily tasks.

You won't become an AI expert in 2 days. But you will stop feeling lost.
