How students should choose

For STEM, prioritise A*STAR, DSTA, NUS research and AI Singapore. For medicine or psychology, choose NUHS job shadowing, healthcare volunteering or research exposure. For law, look for the SAL Junior College Law Programme through school channels. For business, choose EnterpriseSG, MFA, sustainability, social sector or marketing internships.

Before applying, verify:

  • Application deadline and intake date
  • Age, school level and citizenship rules
  • Whether school nomination is required
  • Duration, allowance, fee and certificate policy

Internship Singapore FAQ for Students

Can secondary or JC students get an internship in Singapore?

Yes, but many good options are called attachments, research programmes or job shadowing instead of internship. DSTA, MFA, EnterpriseSG, A*STAR and NUHS are the strongest official places to start.

What is the best summer internship Singapore option for JC students?

DSTA JC Internship, A*STAR research attachments, Youth Corps roles and selected school-nominated research programmes are usually better than generic summer internship listings.

Is there a legal internship Singapore option for pre-university students?

The Singapore Academy of Law Junior College Law Programme is the strongest verified legal exposure pathway. Students usually access it through school channels when applications open.

What about psychology internship Singapore searches?

True psychology internships are usually for older students. For pre-university students, use healthcare job shadowing, social service internships, Youth Corps, and mental health adjacent community roles first.

What does sp internship mean?

Many people use sp internship to mean Singapore Polytechnic internships. This page is broader and focuses on secondary, IB, JC, A Level and pre-university opportunities across Singapore.

Are travel internships available for Singapore students?

Most verified opportunities here are Singapore-based. For travel internships, students should check school-approved overseas attachments, official university programmes and government-linked exchanges.
