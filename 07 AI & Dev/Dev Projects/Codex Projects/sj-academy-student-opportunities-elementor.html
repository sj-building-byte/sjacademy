How students should choose

For pre-med, choose hospital exposure with proper supervision. For business students, choose real communication, sales, fundraising, or project work. For tech students, choose programmes where they build something visible, not just attend lectures.

Before applying, verify:

  • Deadline and batch dates
  • Fee, refund and certificate policy
  • Daily timings and parent permission needs
  • Whether the link is official
