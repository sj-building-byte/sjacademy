# Offers And Academic Products

## Current Core Offers

SJ Academy currently offers:

- O Level tuition
- AS Level tuition
- A2 Level tuition
- A Level Lab Practicals
- Online classes

These should remain the core of most admissions and landing-page messaging unless the page is for a specific product.

## O Level And A Level Tuition

Position tuition around:

- Structured subject teaching
- Syllabus coverage
- Past-paper practice
- Exam technique
- Subject-specialist teachers
- Student and parent communication
- Physical and live online access

Avoid making pages sound like a generic list of subjects. The value is not "we teach many subjects." The value is that students get structured preparation for Cambridge exams.

## Scholarships And Student Opportunities

Scholarship and opportunity pages should be credibility-first.

Rules:

- Prefer official sources.
- Use a focused high-trust shortlist, not a broad low-confidence dump.
- Clearly label each opportunity as open, upcoming, closed, paused, form live, or deadline passed.
- Show important deadlines near the top.
- Separate different opportunity types instead of mixing everything into one list.
- For O/A Level audiences, include both Pakistan-based and international options unless the user narrows the geography.
- Re-check deadlines and statuses at build time.

Useful structure:

1. Hero with clear audience and outcome
2. Deadline or status panel
3. Trust strip explaining how opportunities were selected
4. Filters or category navigation if there are many cards
5. Grouped cards by status, geography, or student fit
6. How to choose section
7. Verification checklist
8. FAQ
9. CTA for help choosing

## Offer Page Rule

Every offer page should answer:

- Who is this for?
- Which exam, subject, paper, or student problem does it solve?
- What exactly does the student get?
- What makes SJ Academy credible for this?
- Is this physical, online, or both?
- What is the next step?

If the page cannot answer those quickly, fix the offer clarity before improving design.
