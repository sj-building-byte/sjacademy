# A Level Business Playbook

## Core Positioning

SJ Academy's A Level Business offer should be positioned around CAIE 9609 exam performance, not generic business theory.

Teacher to emphasize:

- Sir Sunyyan Junaid

Core promise:

Students learn business concepts, but more importantly, they learn how to use case data, build analysis chains, evaluate options, and write recommendations the way CAIE Business answers require.

- CAIE 9609 exam technique

- Paper 2 support
- Paper 3 support
- Paper 4 support
- AS and A2 differences where relevant
- How students lose marks despite knowing theory
- How to move from definitions to high-level answers

## Student Problem

Many A Level Business students:

- Memorize theory but fail to apply it
- Write generic answers
- Ignore case data
- Stop at weak analysis
- Struggle with evaluation
- Do not know how to recommend properly
- Treat the mark scheme like a model answer
- Do not adapt writing style across papers

## Strong Message

Use this idea often:

```text
A Level Business is not only about knowing the theory. It is about using the theory to make a business argument from the case.
```

## Paper-Specific Framing

Use exact paper details only after verifying the current Cambridge syllabus.

Safe framing:

- Paper 1: Shorter structured answers and core understanding
- Paper 2: Data response and application to business scenarios
- Paper 3: Case-study style thinking and strategic analysis
- Paper 4: Deeper evaluation, recommendation, and business decision-making

Before publishing, verify current paper names, marks, duration, and syllabus details from the official Cambridge 9609 syllabus.
