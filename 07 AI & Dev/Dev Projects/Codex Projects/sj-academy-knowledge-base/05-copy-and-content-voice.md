# Copy And Content Voice

## Voice

SJ Academy copy should sound:

- Direct
- Intelligent
- Practical
- Student-aware
- Locally relevant
- Slightly witty where appropriate
- Emotionally aware
- Confident but not arrogant
- Tactical, not fluffy
- Clear enough for students and parents

It should not sound like:

- A generic tuition centre
- A motivational page
- A corporate brochure
- A robotic AI response
- A hype-heavy ad

## Writing Rules

Use:

- Short sentences
- Specific academic language
- Clear CTAs
- Student pain points
- Parent trust language
- Cambridge and CAIE terms accurately
- Concrete examples

Avoid:

- Em dashes
- Empty hype
- Unsupported claims
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Forced slang
- Textbook language
- Long intros
- Filler sections
- Generic phrases like "unlock your potential"

## Landing Page Copy Pattern

Good page copy should:

1. Name the student problem.
2. Explain why it affects marks, confidence, or time.
3. Show how SJ Academy solves it.
4. Give a clear next step.

Example:

```text
Many A Level Business students know the theory, but lose marks because they do not apply it to the case. Our classes focus on case data, analysis chains, evaluation, and recommendation writing so students learn how CAIE answers are actually built.
```

## CTA Copy

Good CTAs:

- WhatsApp for Class Details
- Ask About Seats
- Register for the Batch
- Get Practical Batch Details
- View Course Structure
- Talk to SJ Academy
- Check Available Timings

Weak CTAs:

- Submit
- Click Here
- Learn More
- Get Started
- Join Now, when the next step is actually an enquiry

## Instagram Carousel Voice

Based on prior SJ Academy Instagram analysis, strong carousels are:

- Practical
- Save-worthy
- Exam-tactic focused
- Locally relevant
- Familiar with CAIE anxiety
- Clear enough to screenshot or share

Best-performing strategic categories:

- Exam tactics and CAIE guide carousels
- Career, degree, university, and subject-choice carousels
- Shareable reels for emotional or relatable student moments
- Timely CAIE news when relevant
- School or college reviews

Recommended content mix:

- 50% exam tactics and CAIE guide carousels
- 20% career, degree, university, and subject-choice carousels
- 15% reels for emotional or shareable student moments
- 10% current affairs or CAIE news when relevant
- 5% school, college, or promotional posts

## Carousel Structure

Most strong carousels follow:

1. Hook
2. Problem framing
3. Authority layer
4. Practical breakdown
5. Recap or action plan
6. CTA

Useful hooks:

- You are doing past papers wrong.
- This calculator trick can save you time.
- Most A Level students choose subjects backwards.
- Your mark scheme is not an answer key.
- CAIE examiners silently punish this.
- If you started late, read this.

## Accuracy Rules

Do not invent:

- Paper codes
- Exam durations
- Mark allocations
- Syllabus requirements
- Admission requirements
- University policies
- Grade thresholds
- Cambridge rules
- Statistics
- Rankings
- Salary claims
- "Examiner says" claims

If a claim cannot be verified:

- Remove it
- Rewrite it cautiously
- Label it as a general observation
- Ask for a source
- Say it needs verification before posting

## Parent-Facing Copy

Parents respond to:

- Structure
- Trust
- Clarity
- Communication
- Exam relevance
- Responsible claims

Use language like:

- Parents are not only buying teaching time. They are buying confidence that their child is being guided properly.
- The goal is not just to finish the syllabus. The goal is to prepare students for how Cambridge questions are marked.
- Students get subject support, paper practice, and clearer next steps.

Avoid fear-based exaggeration. Use calm urgency instead.

## Student-Facing Copy

Students respond to:

- Practical tactics
- Honest diagnosis
- Specific exam mistakes
- Quick clarity
- Relatable pressure
- Actionable checklists

Use language like:

- Knowing the topic is not enough if the answer does not match the command word.
- Past papers only help when you review the mistake pattern.
- The mark scheme is not a model answer. It is a scoring guide.

## SEO Copy

For SEO pages:

- Match search intent early.
- Use the keyword naturally in the H1 and first section.
- Keep sections useful, not keyword-stuffed.
- Add FAQs when they answer real search questions.
- Use official sources for education, admission, scholarship, and opportunity details.
- Include internal CTA only when it matches the page intent.

Good SEO pages for SJ Academy can target:

- O Level and A Level guides
- Subject-specific exam tactics
- School and college reviews
- Scholarships
- Student opportunities
- Past paper methods
- A Level practicals
- A Level Business 9609
