Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the practical session last?

Our practical session continues until the exam. However, students are prepared completely by mid-September so that all core practical skills are covered well before the exam period. Students are then called back before their exam for final revision, exam technique practice, and expected practical practice if reliable information is available.

When is the best time to join?

The best time to join is when the session starts. This gives students more time to perform each practical in detail, understand the method properly, correct their mistakes, and build confidence gradually. Students who join later can still cover the practicals, but they may need to attend more regularly to complete everything before the exam.

Can I join just before the exam?

Yes, students can join close to the exam through our practical workshops, where the key experiments and exam skills are covered in a short time. However, joining earlier is strongly recommended because students get more hands-on practice, more teacher feedback, and more time to improve. The fee remains the same regardless of when a student joins, so it is better to start early.

What do I need to bring for the classes?

Students should bring basic stationery, a calculator, and a 30 cm ruler. These can also be purchased from the academy if needed. For Chemistry and Biology practicals, students are recommended to keep a lab coat. SJ Academy will provide the practical manual.
