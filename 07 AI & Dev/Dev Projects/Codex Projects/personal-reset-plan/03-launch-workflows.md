# Launch Workflows

## A Level Business Launch

Goal:

- 10 AS students.
- 10 A2 students.
- Launch by June 1, 2026.
- Target Pakistan and Gulf families.

Positioning:

SJ Academy A Level Business is for students who know the content but lose marks because they do not apply case data, build analysis chains, or write strong evaluation and recommendations.

Core promise:

Learn CAIE 9609 Business answer writing with Sir Sunyyan Junaid, focused on application, analysis, evaluation, and paper-specific exam technique.

Offer checklist:

- [ ] AS and A2 batches clearly separated.
- [ ] Physical and live online options stated.
- [ ] Class start date stated.
- [ ] Teacher named clearly.
- [ ] Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3, and Paper 4 support explained where relevant.
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA is the main action.
- [ ] No long registration form before inquiry.

Landing page structure:

1. Hero: what it is, who it is for, start date, CTA.
2. Problem: why students lose marks in Business.
3. Method: case data, analysis chains, evaluation, recommendations.
4. Teacher: Sir Sunyyan Junaid and exam-focused support.
5. Class details: AS, A2, physical, online, timings if available.
6. Proof or credibility.
7. Final WhatsApp CTA.

WhatsApp inquiry message:

Hi, I want details for A Level Business classes at SJ Academy. I am interested in:

- AS Business
- A2 Business
- Physical classes
- Online classes

Please share timings, fee, and start date.

Tracking checklist:

- [ ] WhatsApp clicks tracked.
- [ ] Meta Pixel event checked.
- [ ] GA4 event checked.
- [ ] UTM source saved where possible.
- [ ] Lead sheet created with name, class, location, source, status, next follow-up.

## Investor Pitch Deck

Goal:

Create a clear investor story for a bigger SJ Academy location and possible 10% ownership offer.

Deck sections:

1. What SJ Academy is.
2. Current traction.
3. The problem with the current capacity.
4. Why a bigger place can unlock growth.
5. Revenue opportunity.
6. Expansion plan.
7. Investment ask.
8. Use of funds.
9. Expected upside.
10. Risks and how they will be managed.

Important rule:

Do not use financial numbers casually. Recheck the workbook before putting numbers in the deck.

## AI Mastery Workshop

Goal:

Target:

- 10 students minimum.
- Weekly income potential if repeated.

Build in this order:

1. Course outcome.
2. Student audience.
3. Two-day outline.
4. Pricing and bonus.
5. Marketing angle.
6. Landing page.
7. Instagram posts.
8. LinkedIn posts.
9. Reddit posts.
10. Ad budget and launch plan.

Do not start the landing page until the course outcome and audience are clear.

## Homepage Redesign

Do this after urgent revenue launches.

Homepage rule:

The homepage should make SJ Academy feel clear, premium, exam-focused, and easy to contact within 5 seconds.

Do not start a page-wide redesign without first auditing:

- Mobile layout.
- Header and footer.
- Elementor structure.
- Current CTAs.
- Forms.
- Tracking scripts.
- WhatsApp journey.
- Duplicate or confusing sections.
