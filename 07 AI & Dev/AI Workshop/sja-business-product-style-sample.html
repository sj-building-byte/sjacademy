Why this works

The page sells the method by letting students interact with it.

Instead of listing “exam technique” as a feature, the interface shows how SJ Academy thinks about papers, levels, modes, and the next conversation.

Answer writing is treated like a skill system.

Students are not just told to “analyze more.” They see the difference between a basic answer and an answer that uses case data, builds consequences, and makes a judgement.

Weak: Advertising increases sales because more customers will know about the product.
Stronger: Advertising may increase demand in the selected market segment, but the recommendation depends on whether extra revenue covers the campaign cost.
Exam focus: connect the point to revenue, cost, capacity, stakeholders, and the final decision.
