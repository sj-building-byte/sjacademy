Learn the CLEAR Speaking Framework

A simple way to structure answers in interviews, meetings, presentations, and workplace conversations.

C

Context

Start with the background.

L

Logic

Make your main point.

E

Example

Support it with proof.

A

Action

Say what happens next.

R

Result

End with impact.

Weak

“I am hardworking and I have good communication skills.”

Stronger

“In my previous role, clarity mattered. I learned to understand the audience, structure the message, and explain ideas in a way customers could act on.”
