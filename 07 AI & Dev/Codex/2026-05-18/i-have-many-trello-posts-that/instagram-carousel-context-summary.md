## Typical Carousel Formula

Most strong SJ Academy carousels follow this structure:

1. Hook or cover slide
   - Sharp promise, warning, or identity-based line
   - Example: "You are doing past papers wrong."

2. Problem framing
   - Names the student pain point clearly
   - Connects the problem to marks, stress, time, or future outcomes

3. Authority layer
   - Uses CAIE language where relevant
   - Examples: syllabus, mark schemes, examiner reports, paper codes, command words

4. Practical breakdown
   - Checklists
   - Formulas
   - Mistakes
   - Examples
   - Step-by-step methods

5. Final recap or action plan
   - Makes the post save-worthy

6. Caption
   - Short hook
   - Summary
   - CTA
   - Hashtags
